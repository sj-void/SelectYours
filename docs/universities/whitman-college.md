# Whitman College

Whitman is a private liberal arts college in Walla Walla, Washington, with about 1,470 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Under 2% of highest-need applicants get in.** Whitman says fewer than 2% of international applicants who need full support with no other funding are admitted [4].
- **Full need if admitted.** Whitman meets 100% of demonstrated need for internationals it admits [4].
- **Real aid numbers.** 201 international undergraduates received Whitman aid, averaging $51,730, or $10.4 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [1].
- **Free Whitman form, not the CSS Profile.** File the Whitman International Student Financial Aid Application (WISFAA), which opens after you submit the Common App [5].
- **Merit scholarships** are available to some internationals without demonstrated need, with no extra materials [4]. There is no first-year work expectation for internationals (our list) [2].
- **English minimums:** TOEFL 85, IELTS 7.0 or Duolingo 110 (2025–26 catalog). Waived if English is your first language or your high school taught mainly in English [6].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 1,468 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [1]; Whitman says about 1,450 students [8].
- **Campus:** 100 acres, three blocks from downtown Walla Walla, Washington [8].
- **Nearest airports:** Walla Walla (ALW), about 10 minutes' drive; Pasco/Tri-Cities, under an hour. Seattle-Tacoma is about four hours away, and Whitman runs no shuttle from Seattle [9].
- **Housing:** about two-thirds of students live on campus [8].
- **Founding year:** Not confirmed on the pages checked (whitman.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Highly need-aware; 100% of demonstrated need for admitted internationals [4].
- **Form:** WISFAA, due by your admission deadline [5].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 201 students; average $51,730; total $10,397,671 [1].
- **Work:** Up to 15 hours a week on campus during term and up to 40 during breaks [4].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 71 departmental majors, all leading to a BA, plus 38 departmental minors [8].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 9.5 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [1].
- **Study abroad:** about 38–40% of juniors study abroad for a semester; two Whitman pages differ [8][10].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 23%, biological and biomedical sciences 16%, visual and performing arts 14%, physical sciences 10%, psychology 8% [14].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 75.4% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), which helps with transition, orientation and immigration [9].
- **Orientation:** required for all F-1 and J-1 students, in late August for 2026–27 [9].
- **Host families:** a Friendship Family Program pairs students with local families [9].
- **Work:** up to 15 hours a week on campus during term and up to 40 in breaks. No first-year work expectation for internationals [4][2].
- **English minimums (current applicant page):** TOEFL iBT 4.5 (85 on the old scale), IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 110 [11]. The 2025–26 catalog listed IELTS 7.0 [6].
- **OPT and CPT details:** Whitman's International Student and Scholar Services lists the F-1 employment options (on-campus work, CPT and OPT) at https://www.whitman.edu/international-student-and-scholar-services/current-international-students/employment-options [17].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a 100-acre campus three blocks from downtown Walla Walla [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Walla Walla Regional Airport, WA, 4.4 km from campus: average temperature 54.3°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 31.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 85.9°F; 18.74 inches of precipitation a year, including 6.5 inches of snowfall [12].
- **Housing:** about two-thirds of students live on campus [8].
- **Annual Security Report:** The newest report found is the October 2023 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report, https://www.whitman.edu/documents/offices/security/2023-Whitman-College-Annual-Security-and-Fire-Safety-Report.pdf. Check Whitman Security for a newer edition [13].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** International students are given a college-provided plan, charged to their student account [18].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Disability Support Services (Academic Resource Center) reviews requests individually, weighing functional impact rather than diagnosis alone [15].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a small-town campus close to downtown [8]; about 40% of juniors study abroad [10]; merit aid open to some internationals [4]; the Friendship Family Program [9].
- **Notable alumni:** Whitman's alumni include a Nobel laureate in physics, the lead engineer of a Mars rover, a US Supreme Court justice and a NASA astronaut [16].
