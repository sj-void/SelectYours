# New York University

New York University (NYU) is a private research university in New York City with about 28,700 undergraduates. It also has portal campuses in Abu Dhabi and Shanghai, which admit and fund students separately and are outside this profile [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **The NYU Promise covers internationals.** NYU meets 100% of demonstrated need for first-year students admitted to the New York campus, "regardless of citizenship". Packages have no loans (our list) [2][4].
- **Only if you file on time.** Promise scholarship review depends on submitting the CSS Profile by NYU's aid deadline [2][5].
- **First-years only.** Transfer students are excluded [2]. Abu Dhabi and Shanghai have their own admission and aid [2].
- **Tuition rises are covered.** Promise scholarships are adjusted each year for tuition increases [2].
- **Huge international community:** about 26% of undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** The NYU Promise: full demonstrated need for New York campus first-years, regardless of citizenship [2].
- **Form:** CSS Profile, by the aid deadline [5].
- **Extra:** Families with income under $100,000 and typical assets also have tuition covered, if they apply by the deadlines [2].
- CDS H6 counts: Not published on the pages checked (nyu.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

NYU publishes 2026–27 cost estimates separately for each undergraduate school [3][6]. The itemized figures were not on the pages checked (nyu.edu, searched 2026-11-23). College Scorecard lists an earlier-year cost of attendance of $86,574 [1]. The NYU Promise meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for New York campus first-years [2][4].

## 6. How to apply

- **English proficiency:** TOEFL iBT, Duolingo, IELTS Academic, PTE Academic, or Cambridge C1 Advanced/C2 Proficiency. There's no minimum; competitive applicants have roughly Duolingo 135, IELTS 7.5, PTE 70 or Cambridge 191 [7].
- **Not accepted:** TOEFL "MyBest" scores and IELTS One Skill Retake [7].
- **No waivers:** SAT, ACT, AP, IGCSE, A-level and IB results don't waive the English requirement [7].
- **Aid:** CSS Profile by NYU's aid deadline [5].
- **Testing policy and deadlines:** Not published on the pages checked (nyu.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **Structure:** many undergraduate schools in New York, including the College of Arts and Science and the Tandon School of Engineering in Brooklyn [3][8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** visual and performing arts 16%, social sciences 12%, business and marketing 12%, computer and information sciences 10%, liberal arts and humanities 8% [13].
- **Careers:** The Office of Global Services runs workshops on on-campus jobs, OPT and STEM OPT; career support is listed on NYU's international student hub [9].
- **Majors:** The College of Arts and Science alone offers over 75 majors and minors (another NYU page says over 120) [17].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (nyu.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 95.8%; 6-year graduation rate 87.8% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 26% of undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** the Office of Global Services (OGS), 383 Lafayette Street, Manhattan, with a Brooklyn office for Tandon students. It handles I-20s, visas, employment and tax status [8].
- **Arrival:** a mandatory check-in workshop within 10 days of arriving in New York, plus New International Student Orientation for first-semester F-1 and J-1 students. Bring your passport, I-20 and I-94 [10].
- **Workshops:** OGS runs sessions on on-campus jobs, OPT, STEM OPT, housing, health insurance and career planning [9][11].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Manhattan (Washington Square) and Brooklyn (Tandon) [1][8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at New York Cntrl Pk Twr, NY, 6 km from campus: average temperature 55.8°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 30.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 82.6°F; 49.52 inches of precipitation a year, including 29.8 inches of snowfall [12].
- **Housing guarantee:** NYU says entering first-years who live in university housing stay eligible for four years of housing (pages differ on the exact wording); housing isn't required [16]. OGS has run workshops on off-campus housing [11].
- **Annual Security Report:** Not published on the pages checked (nyu.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** OGS covers student health insurance in orientation workshops [11]; costs and waiver rules weren't on the pages checked.
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Moses Center for Student Accessibility assigns each student an accessibility specialist and sets accommodations case by case [14].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the NYU Promise of full need without loans for New York first-years, regardless of citizenship [2]; the largest international share on our list [1]; New York City location [1].
- **Notable alumni:** NYU's Nobel laureates include George Wald (WSC '27) and Mohamed ElBaradei (LAW '74). Tisch film and TV alumni include Martin Scorsese, Oliver Stone and Vince Gilligan [15].
