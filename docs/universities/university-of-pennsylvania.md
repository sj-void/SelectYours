# University of Pennsylvania

The University of Pennsylvania (Penn) is a private research university in West Philadelphia, just west of Center City, with four undergraduate schools [9][19].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware.** Penn counts a family's ability to pay in admission decisions for applicants who are not citizens or permanent residents of the US, Canada or Mexico. Some admitted-quality candidates may be declined once the international aid budget is used [1][2].
- **Full need for those admitted with aid.** Penn aims to meet 100% of demonstrated need with grants and work-study. The pages read mention no loans [2][3]. Penn commits "more than $18 million" a year to this group [2]. Our CSV says about 45 awards a year; that count was not on the pages read (see Sources, note under [2]).
- **Apply for aid now or never.** International students can request aid only during the initial admissions process. Requests after admission "cannot be considered" [1][2].
- **Aid forms:** CSS Profile and tax returns (or the home-country equivalent) [3]. Aid materials are due Nov 6 (ED) and Feb 1 (RD) [5]. Penn does not give merit or athletic scholarships [2].
- **SAT/ACT required for everyone,** including internationals. A hardship waiver exists (for example limited test-centre access, financial hardship or civil unrest). If you have taken a test you must report it [6].
- **English proficiency:** TOEFL, IELTS Academic or Duolingo, unless English is your native language or the main language of instruction for at least three years. Only scores from exams taken on or after April 1, 2025 count this cycle [4][6].
- **ED share is large.** 52% of the Class of 2030 was admitted Early Decision [10].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 10,325 full-time undergraduates and 29,384 total students, Fall 2025 [9].
- **Undergraduate schools:** College of Arts and Sciences, School of Engineering and Applied Science, School of Nursing, and The Wharton School [9].
- **Campus:** 299 acres in West Philadelphia, plus New Bolton Center (679 acres) and Morris Arboretum (92 acres) [9][19]. The Facts page says Penn's history "dates back to 1740"; it gives no founding year [9].
- **Academic calendar:** semesters. In 2026–27, fall classes begin August 25, 2026 and end December 7, with finals December 10–17 [32].
- **Nearest airport:** Philadelphia International (PHL). SEPTA's Airport Line runs to University City station; a taxi takes about 20 minutes [33].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for international applicants, defined as non-citizens and non-permanent residents of the US, Canada and Mexico. Admitted aid applicants receive need-based aid covering 100% of demonstrated need, as for domestic students [1][2].
- **Forms and deadlines:** CSS Profile and tax returns or equivalent [3]. Aid materials due Nov 6 for ED and Feb 1 for RD [5]. Penn's checklist for international aid applicants asks for parents' tax returns, or an earnings statement for families abroad, with English translations and amounts in US dollars. The pages checked mention no separate certification of finances for aid applicants. Penn asks international applicants who can pay not to apply for aid [34].
- **What a package contains:** Grants and work-study. The pages read do not mention loans for international students [2][3]. Summer savings expectation: $3,200 is the standard figure. Qualifying summer activities can be waived through the Summer Waiver Award Program [35].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 448 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid. The average award was $80,607, and the total $36,111,968 [29]. Penn separately says it commits more than $18 million a year to students who aren't US, Canadian or Mexican citizens or permanent residents [2].
- **Rules:**
  - Renewal: support runs for all four years while need continues. International students packaged as "highly aided" need not submit anything after the first year [2][3].
  - Late applicants: no aid for those who did not apply at admission [1][2].
  - Outside scholarships: they first reduce the summer savings, then work-study, then need-based grant. They can't reduce the parent contribution [35].
  - Transfers: international applicants are considered for aid in transfer admission [2].
- **Merit scholarships:** None. Aid is need-based only [2].
- **Quaker Commitment (domestic context):** families earning up to $200,000 receive full-tuition aid. The pages do not say whether this applies to internationals [11].
- **Net price calculator:** No version for international students was published on the pages checked (upenn.edu, searched 2026-11-23) [1].

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common App or Coalition Application (use only one) [4][5]. QuestBridge application is accepted for QuestBridge applicants [5]. Penn's QuestBridge page does not say whether internationals are eligible [7].
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027:** ED Nov 1, 2026 (decisions mid-December); RD Jan 5, 2027 (decisions by April 1; reply by May 1). Aid documents: Nov 6 (ED), Feb 1 (RD) [5][22]. ED II: none. Penn's CDS lists a single Early Decision plan, closing November 1 [29].
- **Fee and waivers:** $75. Waiver requests go through the Common App or Coalition section [5]. Whether internationals qualify: not stated.
- **Required parts:** a short thank-you note (50–200 words); a community prompt (50–200 words); one school-specific essay for the undergraduate school you choose (some dual-degree programs need extra essays); a counselor recommendation and a core-subject teacher recommendation, with one optional extra; mid-year report [5].
- **English proficiency:** TOEFL, IELTS Academic or Duolingo. Penn sets no minimum. Exempt if English is your native language or the main language of instruction for three years. Scores must come from the testing agency and from exams on or after April 1, 2025. TOEFL code 2926. IELTS Indicator, IELTS One Skill Retake and TOEFL MyBest are not accepted [4][6]. Competitive score ranges: Not published on the pages checked (upenn.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **International documents:** all four years of grades, with all non-English documents officially translated. Predicted results are requested if your exam results arrive after the deadlines. Certified copies of national exams (for example IGCSE) are sent with the application. Final exam results are needed before you enrol [4]. Applications are read by school location, not citizenship [4].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 90 undergraduate majors, Fall 2025 [9]. Bachelor's degrees by field, July 2024–June 2025 (CDS J1): business/marketing 20%, social sciences 13%, biological sciences 11%, computer and information sciences 9%, engineering 8%, health professions 7%, philosophy and religious studies 7% [29]. Minors and curriculum style: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 10:1 (Fall 2025) [9]. CDS 2025–26: student–faculty ratio 10 to 1 (I2). Of 1,977 class sections in Fall 2025, 1,153 (58%) had fewer than 20 students and 102 had 100 or more (I3) [29].
- **Fields:** Engineering (School of Engineering and Applied Science), nursing, and Wharton business are undergraduate schools [9]. Pre-health, pre-law advising and dual-degree programs: Not published on the pages checked (upenn.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Opportunities:** Penn describes its education as research-driven [26]. Study abroad: the Facts page cites study-abroad credits in 2023–24, but no participation rate was found [9].
- **STEM majors:** Penn's iPenn page maps each major code to a CIP code and marks it STEM or not. Computer Science (11.0701), Mechanical Engineering (14.1901), Chemical Engineering (14.0701) and Chemistry (40.0501) were seen as STEM. Check the CIP code printed on your I-20 [28]. The full list is on the iPenn page [28].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:**
  - First-year retention 98.9% (full-time students; IPEDS via College Scorecard) [30].
  - Graduation: 86.2% within 4 years and 96.9% within 6 years; 98.1% within 6 years for nonresident students [30]. Penn gives a six-year rate of 97% [9][18].
- **After graduation:** For the Class of 2025, 91% were employed full-time or continuing their education within six months; 19.6% were pursuing further education; 68% of undergraduates had an offer by the end of fall of senior year [18]. Top employers and graduate schools: not on that page [18].
- **Earnings:** Median starting salary of full-time employed Class of 2025 graduates $103,418 (mean $101,125), school-reported [18]. College Scorecard median earnings 10 years after entry are $121,687 [30]. These cover federal-aid recipients only, so they exclude internationals.

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 6,785 international students at all levels in Fall 2025; Class of 2029 international students are 35% Asia, 20% Europe, 13% Canada and Mexico, 11% Central and South America and the Caribbean, 4% Australia and the Pacific [9]. The Class of 2030 has home addresses in 76 countries (admissions profile) or 77 (press) [10][11]. CDS 2025–26 B2: 1,337 nonresident degree-seeking undergraduates, 311 of them first-years [29].
- **Support:** International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), part of Penn Global. Penn does not send I-20s automatically: admitted students apply through iPenn, with up to about three weeks' processing in peak season. New students complete an arrival form within 15 days of the I-20 start date and attend one mandatory ISSS information session during International Student Orientation (the 2026 week ran Aug 17–21) [14][17].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: no ISSS authorisation needed, up to 20 hours a week in term [15].
  - CPT: approved by ISSS and shown on the I-20. A court stayed new federal CPT guidance in 2026 and ISSS is preparing updated instructions, so check before planning an internship [15][16].
  - Pre-completion OPT: not after 12 months or more of full-time CPT [15].
  - Post-completion OPT: 12 months, with a 24-month STEM extension for eligible majors [15].
- **Practicalities:** Some buildings close for winter break, so check before staying [20]. Penn's CDS (F2) lists an International Student Organization [29]. Mentor programmes: ISSS launched the International Peer Mentorship Program in 2025: in its first year nearly 200 mentors supported more than 400 incoming international students over the summer [36].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** Undergraduates must live in a College House for their first four semesters [21]. Roughly 5,500 undergraduates live in the 13 College Houses [21]. CDS 2025–26 F1: 100% of first-years and 56% of all undergraduates live in college housing [29].
- **Location:** West Philadelphia, just west of Center City [19]. Nearest airport: Philadelphia International (PHL), with SEPTA rail to University City station [33].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Phila Franklin Inst, PA, 2 km from campus: average temperature 57.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 30.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 87.1°F; 50.69 inches of precipitation a year, including 16.1 inches of snowfall [31].
- **Safety:** Annual Security and Fire Safety Report, published Sept 14, 2026, covers 2023–2025: https://www.publicsafety.upenn.edu/files/2026_ASR_PENN.pdf [19]. For 2025 the Philadelphia main-campus table (extracted from the PDF, whose layout is partly garbled) shows 0 murders, 0 rapes, 0 aggravated assaults and 2 motor vehicle thefts on campus [19]. Check the PDF for the other rows.
- **Student body:**
  - Class of 2030: 52% female, 48% male, as stated at application [10].
  - All undergraduates: 55.0% women, 45.0% men (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [30]. CDS B2 isn't readable.

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Full-time students must have comprehensive coverage. The Penn Student Insurance Plan costs $5,018 for 2026–27. A student with a qualifying plan may apply to waive it through the Insurance Portal (fall window July 1–Aug 31) [12][13]. The waiver criteria were not listed; each request needs Penn approval [12]. ISSS lists J-1 minimum coverage standards; for F-1 students insurance is part of showing you can cover costs [13].
- **Services:** Student Health and Counseling offers free short-term counseling and drop-in hours; Student Health Service gives primary care; Weingarten Learning Resources Center houses Student Disabilities Services [24].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

Penn was named a Fulbright U.S. Student Program Top Producing Institution for 2024–25, and has had 12 Rhodes Scholars in the past decade [25]. Penn-affiliated Nobel laureates include Ahmed Zewail (chemistry, 1999), Stanley Prusiner (medicine, 1997) and Gregg Semenza (medicine, 2019) [25]. Mentioned via search-index text, not fully read.
