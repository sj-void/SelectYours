# College of the Holy Cross

Holy Cross is a private Jesuit liberal arts college in Worcester, Massachusetts, with about 3,110 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, very competitive.** Holy Cross meets 100% of demonstrated need for all entering students [2]. Our list notes that funding for internationals is limited, so competition is very stiff [3].
- **CSS Profile for International Students** is the aid form. Fee waivers are considered only for applicants who have met all of the Admission Office's international requirements [2].
- **File on time.** Materials filed after the priority deadline are considered only as time and funds allow [5].
- **Small international community:** about 3% of undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1843 by the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) in Worcester, Massachusetts [6].
- **Enrollment:** 3,106 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]; about 3% international [1].
- **Campus size:** 174 acres; the campus is a registered arboretum. Holy Cross was founded in 1843 by the Jesuits [17].
- **Nearest airport:** Worcester Regional Airport is about 15 minutes from campus. Boston Logan is about an hour away, with shuttles from Knight's Limousine (reserve ahead) [14].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for entering students [2]; limited funds [3].
- **Form:** CSS Profile for International Students [2].
- **Certification of Finances:** required. International applicants submit it with a bank letter or financial affidavit [8]. This confirms LeBeau's "COF required" label [3].
- CDS H6 counts: Not published on the pages checked (holycross.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **Study abroad:** semester, year-long and Maymester programs. Participants are enrolled in International SOS [9].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 33%, biological and biomedical sciences 13%, psychology 12%, physical sciences 8%, mathematics and statistics 6% [12].
- **Majors:** 40 majors, 29 minors, 6 concentrations and 5 career programs [18].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (holycross.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 94.8%; 6-year graduation rate 86.4% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 3% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** the Office of International Students. It issues I-20s, which are mailed in early summer after admission [8].
- **Orientation:** Gateways International Student Orientation, 16–18 August 2026, before general Gateways orientation; all internationals are expected to attend [10].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week. First-years shouldn't take shifts until formally hired [8].
  - CPT: you need a confirmed internship or research position first [8].
  - OPT: 12 months of work related to your major. About half of recent international graduates used OPT and STEM OPT [8].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Worcester, Massachusetts [6].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Worcester Regional Airport, MA, 6.5 km from campus: average temperature 48.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 19.8°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 77.5°F; 48.26 inches of precipitation a year, including 72.9 inches of snowfall [11].
- **Housing guarantee:** Not published on the pages checked (holycross.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Annual Security Report:** Holy Cross Public Safety explains its Clery reporting and links its reports from https://holycross.edu/campus-life/public-safety/records-reports/cleary-act-faq [19].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** You can waive the college plan only with comparable US-based coverage, not a plan from a foreign insurer. The waiver deadline is 1 August [8].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Office of Student Accessibility Services handles accommodations through an online portal, usually with documentation; they aren't retroactive. Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) serves all current full-time students, staffed by licensed clinicians and supervised doctoral externs [13].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** Jesuit liberal arts education [6]; Maymester study abroad [9]; strong OPT use among international graduates [8].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni of distinction include Joseph E. Murray '40, the Nobel laureate who performed the first organ transplant [15].
