# Swarthmore College

Swarthmore is a private liberal arts college with engineering, near Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. It has about 1,630 undergraduates and is part of the Tri-College Consortium with Haverford and Bryn Mawr [2][1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Loan-free, full determined need.** Aid for internationals is loan-free and meets 100% of determined need, up to the full cost of attendance [3].
- **Real aid numbers.** 75 international undergraduates received Swarthmore aid, averaging $87,434, or $6.56 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [2].
- **Fast paperwork after admission.** Newly admitted internationals must submit aid materials within 7 days of the admission offer [3].
- **Four-year aid decision.** From the Class of 2030, internationals get a four-year aid decision with a fixed parent contribution. They are not eligible for the Swarthmore Tuition Guarantee [3].
- **Prove your English one of three ways.** Applicants at schools outside the US, whatever their citizenship, must send a Swarthmore video response, an English proficiency score or an InitialView video [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1864 by members of the Religious Society of Friends (Quakers), as a co-educational college. Nonsectarian since 1908 [9].
- **Campus:** 425 acres in a suburb 11 miles southwest of Philadelphia, home to the Scott Arboretum and its more than 4,000 plant varieties [9].
- **Enrollment:** 1,631 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [2]; Swarthmore says about 1,600 [9].
- **Housing:** 100% of first-years and 93.7% of all undergraduates live in college housing (CDS 2025–26 F1) [2].
- **Consortium:** cooperates with Bryn Mawr, Haverford and the University of Pennsylvania [9].
- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked (swarthmore.edu, searched 2026-11-23). The campus is 11 miles from Philadelphia [9].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Loan-free aid that meets 100% of determined need [3]. Our list classes Swarthmore as need-aware for internationals [7].
- **Timing:** Admitted internationals submit aid materials within 7 days of the admission offer. Continuing students reapply by Dec 1 or Mar 1 [3].
- **Four-year decision:** The parent contribution is fixed for four years, starting with the Class of 2030 [3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 75 students; average $87,434; total $6,557,536 [2].
- **Tuition Guarantee:** Not open to international students [3].

## 7. Academics

- **Type:** liberal arts college with an ABET-accredited engineering program [9].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 25%, computer and information sciences 11%, biological and biomedical sciences 11%, mathematics and statistics 9%, engineering 7% [16].
- **Student–faculty ratio:** 8:1 [9]; 8 to 1 in Fall 2025 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [2].
- **Cross-registration:** with Bryn Mawr, Haverford and Penn [9].
- **PhDs:** 3rd nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (21.3%) [8].
- **Majors, Honors Program and study-abroad details:** Not published on the pages checked (swarthmore.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 91.6% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [2]; first-year retention 94.0% (IPEDS) [1].
- **PhDs:** Swarthmore ranks 3rd nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (21.3% of graduates, NSF data compiled by Swarthmore) [8].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard earnings cover federal-aid recipients only, so they exclude internationals [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the International Student Center (ISC), in the Hormel-Nguyen Intercultural Center at Sproul Hall. It serves international, undocumented, permanent-resident, US-abroad and exchange students [10].
- **I-20:** Upload documents to the ISC Portal. Swarthmore email starts in April to help with visas, travel details are due mid-July, and orientation details follow in July and August [11].
- **Support listed by the ISC:** vacation housing, a free round trip home, a supplemental loan for internationals, an emergency fund, and help with state ID and Social Security numbers. Not everyone qualifies for every item [12].
- **Work rules (OPT/CPT):** Swarthmore's International Student Center publishes its F-1 employment rules (on-campus work, CPT, OPT and the 24-month STEM extension) at https://www.swarthmore.edu/international-student-center/employment-f1-j1-students [17].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** suburban, 11 miles from Philadelphia, with the Scott Arboretum on campus [9].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Marcus Hook, PA, 10.9 km from campus: average temperature 57.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 29.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 86.4°F; 41.65 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [14].
- **Housing:** 100% of first-years and 93.7% of undergraduates live in college housing (CDS F1) [2]. Vacation housing is available to internationals [12].
- **Annual Security Report:** Swarthmore links its Annual Fire Safety and Security Report 2026 at https://www.swarthmore.edu/public-safety/annual-fire-safety-security-report. Paper copies: Public Safety, 610-328-8281 [15].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Travel insurance:** Students on approved college travel are enrolled in travel medical insurance and International SOS [13].
- **Insurance:** All students are enrolled in the Student Health Insurance Plan for 2026–27; you can waive before June 15 with proof of qualifying insurance [18].
