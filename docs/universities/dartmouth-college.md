## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-blind for all non-US citizens,** since the Class of 2026, and "100 percent of demonstrated need" is met [6]. Aid is judged "regardless of citizenship or entry round" [7].
- **Many internationals get aid.** In Fall 2025, 483 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid, averaging $87,562, a total of $42,292,307 (CDS H6) [1]. That is about three-quarters of the 648 nonresidents (our calc).
- **No loans in the package,** and families under $125,000 with typical assets have no expected parent contribution [7]. Students still contribute about $1,000 to $2,500 a year from summer or leave-term earnings, an on-campus job or savings [7].
- **Aid forms:** CSS Profile (code 3351), parents' 2025 tax statements from your country, or the free ISFAA if the CSS fee would be a serious hardship [12]. Aid deadlines: Nov 1 (ED) and Feb 1 (RD) [11][12].
- **Testing is flexible for internationals.** If you attend school outside the US, you can choose SAT/ACT, three AP exams, IB Diploma scores, A-levels or a national exam [9][10].
- **ED is large.** 768 of 3,552 ED applicants were admitted in Fall 2025 (CDS C21) [1].
- **English proof** is needed only if English is not your first language and your curriculum was not in English for two years [13].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 4,715 undergraduates and 2,558 graduate students (CDS B1) [1]. The Fall 2025 Fact Card gives 4,653 undergraduates, 818 in Guarini and 626 in medicine [5].
- **Calendar:** Quarter system (CDS A4) [1].
- **Schools:** Arts & Sciences (undergraduates), Guarini School of Graduate & Advanced Studies, Geisel School of Medicine and the Thayer School [5].
- **Founded:** 1769 [19].
- **Calendar:** quarters, under the "D-Plan". Students must be in residence for fall, winter and spring of the first year, two of the three senior-year terms, and one summer term [20].
- **Campus size:** A 269-acre main campus [32].
- **Nearest airport:** Dartmouth suggests flying into Boston Logan (about 2.5 hours away) and taking the Dartmouth Coach to a stop on the Green. Manchester-Boston Regional is about 90 minutes away [30].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-blind for all non-US citizens, announced for the Class of 2026 (including ED). Dartmouth "expanded its longstanding need-blind admissions policy to include all international citizens." It commits to meeting 100% of demonstrated need, funded in part by a $40 million anonymous gift [6].
- **Forms and deadlines:** CSS Profile (code 3351). ISFAA accepted if the CSS fee would be a serious hardship. Parents' 2025 income tax statements, in English (you may translate them yourself), in home currency. IDOC is used to collect documents. Deadlines: ED Nov 1; RD Feb 1; transfer Mar 1 [12]. Certification of Finances: not mentioned [12].
- **What a package contains:** Scholarships, grants and student employment, with no required loan. Under $125,000 family income (typical assets): no parent contribution; $125,000–$175,000: scholarship covering full tuition. No income cutoff for aid. Student contribution $1,000–$2,500 a year from summer earnings, a campus job or savings [7]. Aided students get at least half the cost of the health plan as extra scholarship [8].
- **Actual awards (CDS H6, Fall 2025):** 483 nonresident undergraduates aided; average institutional aid $87,562; total $42,292,307 [1]. Dartmouth's institutional aid for nonresidents is need-based. Nonresident aid applicants submit the CSS Profile plus parents' tax returns (CDS H7) [1].
- **Rules:** Renewal, outside scholarships, aid later for students who didn't apply, and transfer aid for internationals: Not published on the pages checked (dartmouth.edu, searched 2026-11-23). International transfer applicants have a Mar 1 aid deadline [12].
- **Merit scholarships:** none listed. The aid page describes need-based scholarships only [7].
- **Net price calculator:** No version for international students was published on the pages checked (dartmouth.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common App. QuestBridge finalists have their fee waived and can apply through QuestBridge [11]. QuestBridge's National College Match is open only to students at US high schools (any citizenship) and to US citizens and permanent residents abroad [22].
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [11]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Aid by | Decision | Reply by |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Decision | Nov 1, 2026 | Nov 1, 2026 | Mid-December | Late December |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 1, 2027 | Feb 1, 2027 | Late March or early April | May 1, 2027 |

- **Fee and waivers:** The CDS says there is a fee that can be waived for applicants with need (amount not extracted) [1]. QuestBridge finalists are waived [11].
- **Required parts:** Common App essay, the Dartmouth Writing Supplement, two teacher evaluations and a counselor evaluation, test scores, and a mid-year report (January to February). A peer recommendation is recommended, not required [11].
- **English proficiency:** TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo or Cambridge English; online TOEFL Home Edition and IELTS Indicator are accepted; TOEFL MyBest and TOEFL Essentials are not. There is no minimum. Typical successful scores: IELTS above 7, Duolingo above 135, Cambridge above 185, TOEFL above 100 (taken before Jan 21, 2026) or 5 or higher (on the new scale). Exempt if English is your first language or your curriculum was taught in English for two years [13].
- **International documents:** IB and A-level predictions must come from a school official; final results go to Dartmouth before enrolment. Other document rules: Not published on the pages checked (dartmouth.edu, searched 2026-11-23) [9].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 2,297 declared majors among undergraduates; top five majors Economics (342), Government (269), Computer Science (212), Engineering Sciences (171), Biology (123) (Fall 2025, double majors counted once per major) [5]. Curriculum: ten distributive courses across areas such as the arts, literature, social analysis and natural sciences, independent of the major. A major usually takes 8–10 courses, and modified or special majors are possible [23]. Count of majors and minors: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 7.9 to 1 (CDS I2, Fall 2025) [1]. Class sections with undergraduates (CDS I3): 638 in total, of which 125 have 2–9 students, 266 have 10–19, 112 have 20–29, 73 have 30–39, 24 have 40–49, 34 have 50–99 and 4 have 100 or more [1].
- **Fields:** Engineering Sciences is a major; bachelor of engineering degrees (127) were awarded in 2024–25 [5]. Pre-health and pre-law advising: Not published on the pages checked (dartmouth.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Opportunities:** CDS A lists double majors, an honors program and study abroad [1].
- **STEM majors:** Dartmouth's international office doesn't publish a list. STEM OPT extensions are requested through the iDartmouth "STEM Extension Request" form, and eligibility depends on the CIP code on your I-20 matching the federal STEM list [24].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 98.0% (CDS B22: 1,158 of 1,182 Fall 2024 entrants) [1]. For the Fall 2019 cohort of 1,188, 78.5% finished in four years and 95.3% within six (our calc from CDS B lines; the six-year rate for Pell recipients is 92%) [1]. The Fact Card shows 96% six-year for a recent cohort [5].
- **After graduation:** 96.1% of the Class of 2025 had launched into jobs, graduate programs, fellowships or service six months after graduating (Center for Career Design) [17]. The split between jobs and graduate school wasn't published on the pages checked.
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings 10 years after entry are $111,162 [16]. These cover federal-aid recipients only, so they exclude internationals.

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 648 nonresident degree-seeking undergraduates in Fall 2025 (14.5%) [1]; 13% of the first-year class is international [5]. The Fact Card lists "Foreign Countries Represented" with the figure 56 beside it [5] (layout read, check the card). CDS F4 lists special housing for international students [1].
- **Support:** The Office of Visa and Immigration Services (OVIS).
  - International student orientation is mandatory: September 8, 2026, the day before New Student Orientation (September 9–11) [25].
  - F-1 students must check in with OVIS within 30 days of arriving. OVIS then registers the SEVIS record and issues a "Continued Attendance" I-20 with a travel signature [25][26].
- **Work:**
  - CPT needs one academic year of full-time F-1 study and a job tied to the major, through CPT-eligible courses. Request it with the iDartmouth CPT form. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT cancels OPT eligibility [24].
  - Pre-completion OPT during term must be part time and needs the Undergraduate Dean's approval.
  - Post-completion OPT and the STEM extension are requested through iDartmouth [24].
- **Practicalities:** Mentors: a team of 20–25 International Student Mentors leads small groups through the International Student Pre-Orientation Program and the first two terms [33]. Break housing and student organizations: Not published on the pages checked (dartmouth.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** 100% of first-year students and 85% of all undergraduates live in college-owned housing (CDS F1) [1]. Housing options include a Greek system, cooperative housing and theme housing (CDS F4) [1].
- **Location:** Hanover, New Hampshire [3][4]. Nearest city: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Hanover, NH, 0.4 km from campus: average temperature 47.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 15.9°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.3°F; 40.15 inches of precipitation a year, including 60.6 inches of snowfall [18].
- **Safety:** The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report, posted as a 2026 draft with crime and fire statistics for 2023–2025, is at https://www.dartmouth.edu/security/clery-compliance-office/asr.pdf. Paper copies are available from Safety and Security (603-646-4000) [27].
- **Student body:** Fall 2025 undergraduates: 2,440 men and 2,275 women (CDS B1) [1].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Students must buy the Dartmouth Group Health Insurance Plan ($5,216 for 2026–27) unless a comparable plan is certified. Aided students receive at least half the plan cost in extra scholarship [8]. Specific waiver rules for international plans: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Services:** The Dick's House Counseling and Health Services centers are listed among Dartmouth's confidential resources [27]. Disability services: Student Accessibility Services is the first contact for undergraduate disability accommodations (ADA@dartmouth.edu). Health and counseling services are at Dick's House [29].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Distinctive features:** the D-Plan quarter calendar [20], and need-blind admission for international applicants since 2022 [28]. The CDS shows 1,357 bachelor's degrees awarded in 2024–25 (AB 91%, BE 9%) [5].
- **Notable alumni:** Dartmouth's alumni page lists Dr. Seuss (Theodor Geisel), Nobel chemistry laureate K. Barry Sharpless and US Senator Kirsten Gillibrand '88 [31].
