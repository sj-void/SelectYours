# Haverford College

Haverford is a private liberal arts college outside Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, with about 1,420 undergraduates. It is part of the Tri-College Consortium with Bryn Mawr and Swarthmore [2][1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, but few seats.** Haverford says aid for internationals is limited and admission "especially competitive", but it meets 100% of the demonstrated need of all admitted students [3].
- **Real aid numbers.** 55 international undergraduates received aid, averaging $92,126, or $5.07 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [2].
- **Every round is open to aid seekers.** Internationals may apply for aid in ED I, ED II or RD [3].
- **Ask in year one or never.** Students who get no aid in their first year can't receive it later. International transfers generally get no aid [3].
- **Hard English-test minimums.** If English isn't your first language and your school didn't teach mainly in English: TOEFL iBT 100 (old scale; the new 1–6 scale is accepted this cycle), IELTS Academic 7.0 or Duolingo 130 [5].
- **Aid doesn't cover travel or break-time living costs** for students from outside the US [4][6].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1833 [8].
- **Campus:** a 200-acre arboretum about eight miles from Philadelphia. It has 400 species of trees and shrubs, a 3.5-acre duck pond, gardens and woods [8].
- **Enrollment:** 1,420 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [2]; Haverford's at-a-glance figure is 1,503 [8].
- **Senior thesis:** completed by 100% of students [8].
- **Honor Code:** written and run entirely by students; it covers academic and social life [9].
- **Consortium:** Tri-College Consortium with Bryn Mawr and Swarthmore (see the Swarthmore and Bryn Mawr profiles).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Limited funds; meets 100% of demonstrated need for all admitted students [3].
- **Forms:** 2027–28 CSS Profile. If you can't complete it, use Haverford's International Student Financial Aid Application. Foreign citizens living in the US with their families use the CSS Profile, not the FAFSA [3].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15, 2026; ED II Jan 5, 2027; RD Jan 10, 2027 [3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 55 students; average $92,126; total $5,066,953 [2].
- **Rules:** No aid in later years for students who received none in their first year. International transfers generally get no aid [3]. Aid does not cover international travel or living costs while the college is closed [4][6].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Office of International Student Support (ISSO). It offers F-1 workshops and advising, and supports CPT [11].
- **Orientation:** International Student Orientation runs five days before the Customs program. In 2026 it ran 21–26 August, and it's mandatory for F-1 students. It covers immunization compliance, keeping your visa status, work authorization, bank accounts and how campus jobs affect immigration status [12].
- **OPT:** A Haverford Designated School Official authorizes CPT, usually after one full academic year, using a job offer letter, job description and transcript. OPT is filed with USCIS on Form I-765 (Haverford quotes a $410 fee and about three months' processing; check USCIS). Haverford flags SEVP's updated CPT guidance of August 24, 2026 [21].
- **Break housing:** Winter break housing is for approved athletes, approved international students who can't go home, students starting Penn classes early, and extenuating cases; it carries a fee for meals and services [16].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** an arboretum campus eight miles from Philadelphia [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Norristown, PA, 11.7 km from campus: average temperature 55.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 25.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 86.4°F; 50.65 inches of precipitation a year, including 25.3 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Move-in:** campus housing opened Friday 21 August 2026 for international orientation [12].
- **Housing guarantee:** New students must live on campus; returning students are guaranteed housing through the room draw [20].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security Report & Annual Fire Safety Report (crime statistics for 2022–2024) is at https://www.haverford.edu/sites/default/files/Office/Safety/HC-Annual-Security-Report-2025.pdf [18].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Immunizations:** handled during orientation, under Haverford's immunization policy [12].
- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 premium is $2,330 a year (UnitedHealthcare, August 1 to July 31). F-1 students can't waive the college plan [19].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS) is free to all full-time students during the academic year [15].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a student-run Honor Code [9]; universal senior thesis [8]; 8th nationally for PhDs per graduate [7]; Tri-Co and Penn cross-registration.
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni profiles include humorist Dave Barry '69 and mindfulness teacher Jon Kabat-Zinn '64 [17].
