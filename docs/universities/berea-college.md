# Berea College

Berea is a private liberal arts work college in Berea, Kentucky, with about 1,510 undergraduates. It charges no tuition, and every student works on campus [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full cost covered for every admitted international.** All admitted internationals get aid and scholarships covering 100% of tuition, housing, food and fees [2].
- **From Fall 2027, supplies too, and no loans.** Berea will guarantee that tuition, housing, food, fees and supplies are covered without loans for all students [2].
- **$2,200 deposit.** You pay it to confirm enrollment, and it can be used for some expenses later. Your I-20 is issued only after the deposit and the required forms arrive [2]. Our list notes that extra aid is possible if you can't pay the deposit [3].
- **Everyone works.** The Work Program counts as part of your education and aid, so internationals take part without separate work permits. At least 10 hours a week is required. Most internationals work 15–20 hours and earn over $2,000 a year [2].
- **Very few international places,** and a unique financial-eligibility requirement: Berea admits students with financial need [3][4].
- **Fall entry only.** No admission for students who already hold a bachelor's degree. International transfer places are very limited [2].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of tuition, housing, food and fees for every admitted international; supplies and a no-loan guarantee from Fall 2027 [2].
- **Deposit:** $2,200 [2].
- **Work:** At least 10 hours a week; most internationals earn over $2,000 a year [2].
- **Aided internationals:** all of them. About 120 international students are enrolled, and every admitted international is fully funded [2]. CDS H6 counts: not published on the pages checked.

## 7. Academics

- **Model:** a tuition-free, work-focused education. Every student works in the college's Work Program, at least 10 hours a week [8][2].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** computer and information sciences 11%, biological and biomedical sciences 10%, business and marketing 10%, social sciences 8%, education 7% [13].
- **Majors:** Majors in 33 fields of study and 39 minors [15].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (berea.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 79.2%; 6-year graduation rate 66.3% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].
- **Recognition:** Washington Monthly named Berea the No. 1 college in its 2026 guide [7].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 120 international students from over 70 countries [2]. Students come from 74 countries outside the US [10].
- **Intake:** about 40 or fewer new international students a year [2].
- **Office:** the Center for International Education [5].
- **I-20:** issued after the $2,200 deposit and required forms (passport, medical history) are received [2].
- **Work:** the Work Program counts as financial aid, so no separate permit is needed. Most internationals work 15–20 hours a week [2].
- **Timing:** fall entry only [2].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Berea, Kentucky [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Crab Orchard 6 N, KY, 15.7 km from campus: average temperature 56.8°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 28.9°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 84.7°F; 50.55 inches of precipitation a year, including 7.4 inches of snowfall [11].
- **Housing:** residence-hall cost is included in the aid that covers internationals [9][2].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security Report, with Clery totals and a fire safety report for student housing, is on the Public Safety "Statistics and Reports" page. Reports from 2018–2023 are archived there [12].
