# Oberlin College

Oberlin is a private liberal arts college and conservatory of music in Oberlin, Ohio, with about 2,890 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **International admit rate about 8%** for Fall 2026, against 34% overall. Applicants who can contribute at least $35,000 a year are the most competitive [2].
- **Full need, limited budget.** Oberlin meets 100% of demonstrated need for all students it admits, but its international aid budget is limited [2][3].
- **Apply for aid at admission or never** [2].
- **Automatic merit consideration** for internationals: the Oberlin Commitment Scholarship ($10,000) and the Mondlane and Reischauer awards (up to $10,000) [4].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 1587), or the ISAFA with financial documents if the Profile isn't feasible [2].
- **Applicants not seeking aid** must show $90,000 of support for 2027–28 [2].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for admitted students; limited budget [2][3].
- **Package:** Grants and jobs. Oberlin "only occasionally" offers loans directly to internationals (our list says packages can include loans) [2][3].
- **Merit:** Automatic consideration for awards of up to $10,000 [4].
- **Forms and deadlines:** See the key points [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 292 international undergraduates; average $51,631; total $15,076,286 [8].

## 7. Academics

- **Structure:** the College of Arts and Sciences and the Conservatory of Music, plus a double-degree option [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** visual and performing arts 27%, social sciences 13%, biological and biomedical sciences 11%, psychology 8%, area, ethnic and gender studies 6% [15].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 9 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [8].
- **PhDs:** 19th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (11.6%) [6].
- **STEM OPT:** a 24-month extension for designated STEM fields [9].
- **Majors:** More than 40 majors and more than a dozen interdisciplinary minors in the College of Arts and Sciences, plus integrative concentrations [19].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 821 of 1,077 class sections (76%) had fewer than 20 students [8]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 91.0%; 6-year graduation rate 80.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 292 degree-seeking nonresident undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B2) [8].
- **Office:** International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), with advising appointments for I-20, visa, CPT and OPT questions [10].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: no special authorization needed, but you need a Social Security number. You can't keep working after your program end date without authorization [11].
  - CPT: integral to your curriculum and major, usually after one full year. It must be authorized before work starts and can't be backdated [11].
  - OPT: up to 12 months, applying up to 90 days before your program ends. STEM fields can add 24 months [9].
- **Information sessions:** ISSS holds OPT/CPT information sessions [12].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** 100% of first-years and 93% of all undergraduates live in college housing (CDS 2025–26 F1) [8].
- **Setting:** Oberlin, Ohio [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Oberlin, OH, 1.3 km from campus: average temperature 50.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 20.6°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.3°F; 39.97 inches of precipitation a year, including 47.0 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Annual Security Report:** The Annual Campus Safety Report 2025 (Clery Act and VAWA) is at https://oberlin.edu/media/35715/download?inline=; a separate Annual Fire Safety Report covers residence halls [14].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Mandatory health fee:** $500 (2026–27) [7].
- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 premium is $3,028 (Anthem). International students may not waive the college insurance [18].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Office for Disability & Access (Student Accessibility Resources) handles accommodations. The Counseling Center keeps a list of private local mental-health providers [16].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a conservatory alongside the arts and sciences college, with a double-degree option [7]; one of the largest international aid counts on our list (CDS H6: 292) [8]; strong PhD production [6].
- **Notable alumni:** Oberlin counts four alumni Nobel laureates. Other alumni include James Burrows '62, director-producer of Cheers; director Julie Taymor '74; and Lena Dunham '08 [17].
