# Pitzer College

Pitzer is a private liberal arts college in Claremont, California, with about 1,230 undergraduates. It is one of the Claremont Colleges [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No current Common Data Set was found in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Few aided seats, but full need if admitted.** Pitzer can offer aid "to a small number of international students". If you're admitted with aid, it meets 100% of your demonstrated need with work-study and a Pitzer need-based scholarship [2].
- **Answer "yes" to the aid question on the Common App,** or you can never apply for aid while enrolled [2].
- **Pitzer's own form.** Aid applicants file only the Pitzer International Financial Aid Application (PIFAA), not the CSS Profile or FAFSA [2].
- **Aid documents:** The dates page also mentions a Mar 2 date for international aid materials. Check it against your round's deadline [4].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Aid for a small number of internationals. Full demonstrated need is met with work-study and a Pitzer scholarship [2].
- **Form:** PIFAA only [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 10; transfer Apr 1 [4].
- **Rule:** Admitted students who answered "no" to the aid question can never receive need-based aid [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 46 international undergraduates; average $81,824; total $3,763,914 [5].
- **Work-study:** Institutional work-study is open to internationals with financial need [8].

## 7. Academics

- **Most popular intended majors (2026 admits):** Environmental Analysis, Psychology, Political Science, Economics, Biology, then Sociology, Human Biology, and English and World Literature [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 16%, interdisciplinary studies 15%, natural resources 12%, biological and biomedical sciences 10%, psychology 8% [18].
- **Student–faculty ratio:** 9 to 1, Fall 2025 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [5].
- **Study abroad:** run by Study Abroad & International Programs [12].
- **Claremont Colleges:** cross-registration across the consortium.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 81.8% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [5]; first-year retention 93.8% (IPEDS) [1].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard earnings exclude international students [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 121 nonresident undergraduates (CDS B2) [5]. The Pitzer International Student Association runs cultural events and peer networking [12].
- **I-20 and orientation:** Request your I-20 through the International Programs Office. International Student Orientation is mandatory and comes before general orientation. Students transferring from US schools must move their SEVIS record first [13].
- **Work:** On campus only, unless you have CPT, OPT or severe-hardship authorization. Get an advisor's approval before accepting any job. Jobs are posted on Handshake [8].
- **English support:** The Writing Center offers sessions for multilingual students [14].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Claremont, California [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Pomona/Fairplex, CA, 6 km from campus: average temperature 65.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 43.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 89.0°F; 14.47 inches of precipitation a year, including 0.0 inches of snowfall [16].
- **Housing:** A guarantee for degree students was not stated on the pages checked (exchange students are guaranteed housing) [15].
- **Annual Security Report:** Pitzer links its Clery information and report from its Title IX & Clery Statement page, https://www.pitzer.edu/offices/human-resources/title-ix-clery-statement [17].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Shared Claremont services:** counseling (Monsour) and health services are shared across the Claremont Colleges (see the Claremont McKenna and Pomona profiles).
- **Insurance:** International students can't waive the Aetna Student Health Insurance Plan (coverage August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027); one Pitzer page lists the cost as $3,312 for 2026–27 [20].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Notable alumni:** Not published on the official pages checked (pitzer.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
