# Northwestern University

Northwestern is a private research university whose main campus is in Evanston, Illinois, on Lake Michigan about 12 miles north of Chicago [1]. It has six undergraduate schools [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, with a four-year full-need promise.** International candidates seeking aid are "evaluated in a need-aware manner". Northwestern says it will meet 100% of demonstrated need for all four years for admitted first-years. Awards are grants only, with no loans and no work requirement, and cover the full cost of attendance including health insurance, visa renewal and a travel allowance [4][5].
- **Only a small group gets aid.** The aid page says a small group of international first-years receives need-based aid each year [5]. CDS H6: 297 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid in 2025–26, average $74,186, total $22,033,357 [3].
- **You must ask for aid when you apply.** International students who do not say so during admissions are ineligible for institutional need-based aid for their whole undergraduate career. A late appeal is possible only in the final year, in rare and exceptional circumstances, and help is limited to tuition. International transfers get no need-based aid [4][5].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 1565), or the ISAFA if the Profile cannot be submitted from your country or would cause hardship. Parents' tax forms (or employer earnings statements) go through the secure upload, translated into English with amounts converted to US dollars. No CSS Profile fee waivers for internationals [4].
- **Early Decision aid dates are early:** aid checkbox Nov 1, CSS Profile/ISAFA Dec 1, tax documents Dec 5 [4].
- **Testing is optional,** but an English proficiency test is required if English is not your first language or your schooling was not in English [7][8].
- **Interviews:** international applicants may optionally do an InitialView interview; skipping it brings no disadvantage [9].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** Not stated on the pages read. The university's "About" page says the founders bought land in 1853 [1].
- **Campus:** Main campus in Evanston on Lake Michigan, about 12 miles north of Chicago; the university also has campuses in Chicago and Doha, Qatar [1]. Nearest airport and distance: O'Hare is about an hour from Evanston, and Midway is farther. In 2026, OISS ran a free O'Hare-to-Evanston shuttle for students on September 13; Airport Express offers shared rides [26].
- **Academic calendar:** Not published on the pages checked (northwestern.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Enrollment:** CDS 2025–26 B1: 9,039 undergraduates and 14,273 graduate students; 23,312 in total [3]. The university's "About" page says 21,000 enrolled students and "about 25% of full-time students are international" (all levels, school figure) [1].
- **Schools:** Six undergraduate schools: Weinberg College of Arts and Sciences, School of Communication, School of Education and Social Policy, McCormick School of Engineering, Medill, and Bienen School of Music [2].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for international applicants who ask for aid (the request and amount needed may factor into the admission decision). "We guarantee to meet 100% of all admitted first-year students' demonstrated financial need for all four years" [5]. The aid page says packages meet 100% of demonstrated need [4].
- **Forms and deadlines [4]:**

  | Round | Aid request (checkbox) | CSS Profile or ISAFA | Tax documents | Aid offers |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Decision | Nov 1 | Dec 1 | Dec 5 | December |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 2 | Feb 2 | Feb 5 | Late March |
  | Transfer | Mar 2 | Mar 15 | Apr 20 | May |

  The general aid page and admissions page give Feb 1 for the RD CSS Profile and aid, so the RD aid dates differ between pages: confirm in your portal [4][8][10]. CSS code 1565. The ISAFA may need extra documents. Tax or earnings documents must be translated into English with amounts in US dollars and the conversion rate shown. A missed checkbox can be fixed by emailing nuinternational@northwestern.edu before the aid deadline for your round [4]. The Certification of Finances and IDOC are not mentioned on the international page [4].
- **What a package contains:** Grants and scholarships only; no loans and no work requirement. Covers tuition, housing, meals, fees, health insurance, books, personal expenses, visa renewal and a travel allowance. Undergraduate study only [5].
- **Actual awards (CDS H6):** 297 nonresident undergraduates received need-based or non-need-based institutional aid; average $74,186; total $22,033,357. That is 26.8% of the 1,107 nonresident undergraduates (our calculation; H6 does not separate need-based from merit aid) [3]. H7 lists the institution's own form, the CSS Profile and parent/student tax forms for nonresident first-years [3].
- **Rules:**
  - Four-year awards are issued to international applicants, and recipients must reapply for years two to four under different requirements [4][5].
  - You must apply for aid in your first year to be able to apply later [5].
  - Late appeal: only in the final undergraduate year, rare cases, limited to tuition [4].
  - Transfers: no need-based aid for international transfers [5].
  - Outside scholarships and on-campus jobs are listed as other funding; the page does not describe how outside awards reduce aid [5].
  - International students must file US taxes, even without income [5].
- **Merit scholarships:** The page mentions certain academic and institutional scholarships without detail [5].
- **Net price calculator:** College Board, https://npc.collegeboard.org/app/northwestern ; quick estimator, https://c2calc.myintuitionapp.org/institution/fb8aa3af-bfec-4df9-aa1d-4d973ff14cd0/costestimator [10]. The pages do not say whether they work for internationals.

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common Application or Coalition with Scoir; Northwestern supplement required. A QuestBridge round exists (Oct 1, 2026) [8]. Whether international students can use QuestBridge: Not stated.
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [8]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Aid due | Decision |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | QuestBridge | Oct 1, 2026 | Oct 1, 2026 | December 2026 |
  | Early Decision | Nov 1, 2026 | Dec 1, 2026 | December 2026 (mid-December per the international page) [11] |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 4, 2027 | Feb 1, 2027 | March 2027 |

  Reply dates: ED deposit Feb 1; RD deposit May 1 [4]. Requests for exceptions to deadlines should go to nuinternational@northwestern.edu before Nov 15 [11].
- **Fee and fee waivers:** $75, non-refundable; waivers available [8]. The page does not say whether internationals qualify.
- **Required parts:** the application; one 300-word supplement question on how your background shapes your engagement at Northwestern, plus optional prompts under 200 words; a counselor recommendation and one core-subject teacher recommendation; transcripts and the school report; the Early Decision Agreement for ED. Program-specific materials apply to Bienen, ISP, MMSS and the Musical Theatre Certificate; ISP and MMSS need separate applications [8][9]. Mid-year report: Not stated.
- **English proficiency:** Required if your first language is not English or your secondary schooling was not in English. Accepted: TOEFL iBT, IELTS, Duolingo English Test; scores must be official, not self-reported [7][8]. Northwestern publishes no minimum scores for undergraduates. It also accepts the IELTS Indicator, and judges proficiency from test scores, English-taught grades, writing and recommendations [20]. Waiver rules: Not published for first-year applicants on the pages checked.
- **International documents:** Transcripts and translations, predicted grades and national exams: Not published for undergraduates on the pages checked (northwestern.edu, searched 2026-11-23). The page says applicants complete a secondary program that would qualify them for university entry at home [11].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** "More than 100" majors and minors; about 4,000 undergraduate courses [2][12]. Curriculum: Weinberg College students take two courses in each of six foundational disciplines (12 in all), plus two courses on power, justice and equity, one US-focused and one global [21].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 16%, communication 14%, engineering 14%, computer and information sciences 10%, biological and biomedical sciences 10% [23].
- **Teaching:** Student-faculty ratio 6 to 1 (Fall 2025, 8,925 students to 1,401 faculty, CDS I2). Class sections (CDS I3): 1,061 with 2–9 students, 753 with 10–19, 208 with 20–29, 133 with 30–39, 45 with 40–49, 121 with 50–99 and 42 with 100 or more; 2,363 in total [3].
- **Fields:** Engineering, computer science and applied mathematics are in the McCormick School [12]. Pre-health, pre-law and 3-2 programs: Not published for undergraduates on the pages checked (northwestern.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Opportunities:** "Hundreds of labs" open to students of any major; study abroad on every continent; the university says about half of students study abroad [1][12]. Integrated Science Program and Mathematical Methods in the Social Sciences are separate admission programs [9].
- **STEM majors:** Northwestern's international office publishes no list on the pages checked. STEM OPT eligibility depends on the CIP code on your I-20 matching the federal STEM list.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 97.5% (CDS B22: 2,045 of 2,097). 2019 cohort: 85.3% graduated within 4 years and 96.1% within 6 years (our calculation: 1,712 and 1,928 of 2,006) [3].
- **After graduation (Class of 2025 six-month outlook, survey of 1,748):** 72% employed, 24% in graduate or professional school, 2% job-searching, under 1% military or service. By school, employed shares range from 51% (Bienen) to 82% (Medill) [13]. Top employers and graduate schools: Not on the page read.
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings 10 years after entry are $104,255 [18]. These cover federal-aid recipients only, so they exclude internationals.

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 1,107 nonresident undergraduates, 12.3% of degree-seeking undergraduates (CDS B2) [3]; the university says about 25% of full-time students at all levels are international [1]. The Class of 2030 comes from 65+ countries [19].
- **Support:** The Office of International Student and Scholar Services (OISS) advises international students. Undergraduate orientation is the International Wildcat Welcome; students also complete an immigration check-in, an Arrival Checklist and OISS's online orientation. I-20 request steps are linked but not described [14].
- **Work:** OISS helps with on-campus employment, CPT, and OPT; the page read does not mention STEM OPT or give Northwestern-specific rules [14].
- **Practicalities:** CDS F2 lists an International Student Organization [3]. Break housing: Continuous Housing, built with the international student office, lets students stay over winter break in select halls (710 Emerson, 717 University, 1856 Orrington) for a flat fee, with priority by need [25]. Mentor programs: The Buffett Institute's Atlas Peer Mentorship pairs first-years, including F-1 and J-1 students on need-based aid, with experienced students. OISS International Peer Advisors help run International Wildcat Welcome, and OISS sponsors three international student groups [28].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** 99% of first-years and 55% of all undergraduates lived in college housing in Fall 2025 (CDS F1) [3]. The housing page mentions a residency requirement for incoming undergraduates without giving its terms [15].
- **Location:** Evanston on Lake Michigan, about 12 miles north of Chicago [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Chicago Botanic Garden, IL, 13.2 km from campus: average temperature 49.6°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 19.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.3°F; 39.51 inches of precipitation a year, including 33.6 inches of snowfall [17].
- **Safety:** The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report 2026 (Evanston, Chicago and other US sites) covers crime statistics for 2023–2025 and Evanston housing fire statistics: https://www.northwestern.edu/compliance/docs/annual-security-report-evanston-chicago.pdf. Printed copies: clerycoordinator@northwestern.edu or (847) 467-6171 [22].
- **Student body:** CDS B1 all undergraduates: 4,207 men, 4,802 women, 30 unknown [3].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** All degree-seeking students must have comprehensive coverage. Enrollment in the Northwestern student health plan (NU-SHIP) is the default and the premium is billed to your student account ($5,996 in the cost budget). Waivers need comparable coverage meeting Northwestern's checklist; open enrollment ran July 1 to Oct 1, 2026. For international students the page says the requirement is tied to US State Department visa insurance rules and gives no special waiver exception [16][6].
- **Services:** Health center, counseling and disability services: AccessibleNU coordinates accommodations (Evanston and Chicago). Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) is free to full-time students: individual and group counseling, psychiatric consultation and crisis help, with a therapist on call 24/7 [24].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

The university's "About" page cites 3,300+ full-time faculty, 500+ student organizations and 19 intercollegiate teams, and mentions Nobel recognition without a count [1]. Notable alumni: Northwestern's facts page highlights alumni such as Emmy-winning host Stephen Colbert and Gone Girl author Gillian Flynn [27].
