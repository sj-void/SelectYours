# Washington and Lee University

Washington and Lee (W&L) is a private liberal arts university in Lexington, Virginia. It has about 1,880 undergraduates and a law school [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-blind for internationals, and loan-free.** W&L says it is need-blind in admission decisions and meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for every admitted student [2]. It is one of only 10 schools on our list that are need-blind for internationals [3].
- **About 20 internationals per entering class get aid.** Awards range from several thousand dollars to the full cost of attendance [2][4].
- **Four-year grants that keep pace with tuition.** International grants last four years and rise dollar for dollar with tuition increases. They can't be negotiated after you accept admission [2].
- **CSS Profile required.** Request a fee waiver in the applicant portal, then submit the Profile within one week either way [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** It traces its roots to Augusta Academy, founded in 1749, later chartered as Liberty Hall Academy. W&L calls itself the ninth-oldest US college [7].
- **Enrollment (2025–26):** 2,281 students: 1,912 undergraduates and 369 law students [7].
- **Campus:** about 50 acres for the main campus, plus about 40 acres of playing fields and 215 of undeveloped land, in Lexington, Virginia, a town of about 7,000. The Skylark conference center adds 365 acres [8].
- **Nearest airport:** Roanoke, about 45 minutes away on Interstate 81 [8].
- **Leadership:** Kenneth P. Ruscio became interim president on 1 July 2026 [7].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-blind; 100% of demonstrated need met without loans, through grants and student employment [2][6].
- **Form:** CSS Profile (fee waiver on request) [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Dec 1, 2026; ED II Jan 15, 2027; RD Feb 15, 2027 [2].
- **Grant terms:** Four years, rising with tuition, not negotiable after acceptance [2].
- **About 20** internationals per class receive need or merit aid [2][4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 160 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $90,617, total $1,44,98,773 [23]. W&L says about 20 internationals per class receive aid [2].

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

The itemized 2026–27 figures were not on the pages checked (wlu.edu, searched 2026-11-23). College Scorecard lists an earlier-year cost of attendance of $91,700 [1]. W&L meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans [7]. Its expanded tuition-free pledge (family income under $200,000, from fall 2027) is described for US families [7].

## 7. Academics

- **Distinctive programs:** W&L says it is the only top liberal arts college with accredited business and journalism programs, and it has a School of Law [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** business and marketing 32%, social sciences 17%, biological and biomedical sciences 12%, psychology 6%, engineering 6% [15].
- **Global education:** study abroad through the Center for International Education [9].
- **Majors:** 37 majors and 42 minors (other W&L pages give slightly different counts) [22].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 428 of 513 class sections (83%) had fewer than 20 students [23]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 96.6%; 6-year graduation rate 95.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Center for International Education (CIE), in the Ruscio Center for Global Learning. Its International Student Advisor is also the Designated School Official [10].
- **Services:** visa advice, work authorization, taxes, driver's licenses, Social Security numbers, study abroad and cultural adjustment. Announcements come through the HomeBase platform [10][11].
- **Programs:** an International Student Portal for prospective students and a Friendship Family Program [9].
- **Other support:** Student Engagement and Leadership offers laptop lending and emergency funds; Public Safety provides airport transport and a safe-ride program [12].
- **OPT and CPT procedures:** W&L's Center for International Education explains jobs, internships and work authorization for international students at https://www.wlu.edu/center-for-international-education/international-students/current-international-students/jobs-internships-and-volunteering-in-the-us [21].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Lexington, Virginia, a small town about 45 minutes from Roanoke's airport [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Lexington, VA, 1.4 km from campus: average temperature 54.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 24.8°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 85.0°F; 42.89 inches of precipitation a year, including 13.5 inches of snowfall [14].
- **Safety services:** Public Safety runs airport transport and a safe-ride program [12].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2026 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report (Clery statistics for 2023–2025) is at https://my.wlu.edu/document/annual-security-and-fire-report [19].
- **Housing:** Students must live on campus for at least their first three years, and may stay all four as space allows. Housing closes over winter break except in extenuating circumstances [17].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** All full-time international students are enrolled in the Injury and Sickness Insurance Plan and billed at the undergraduate rate [20].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Lindley Center for Student Wellness brings Lindley Health and Lindley Counseling under one roof; accessibility accommodations are a separate service [16].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:**
  - need-blind admission with no-loan full-need aid for internationals [2];
  - accredited business and journalism programs [7];
  - a student-run honor system of more than 150 years, as described by the law school [13];
  - one of the oldest US colleges [7].
- **Notable alumni:** W&L Law graduates include two US Supreme Court justices and John W. Davis, the 1924 Democratic presidential nominee [18].
