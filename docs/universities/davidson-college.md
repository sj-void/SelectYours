# Davidson College

Davidson is a private liberal arts college in Davidson, North Carolina, near Charlotte, with about 1,870 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full calculated need, no loans.** Davidson commits to meeting 100% of calculated need for admitted students, except recruited athletes [2]. Packages are grants and student employment, with no loans (our list) [4].
- **No application fee for non-US citizens.** Davidson waives it, but not the $25 CSS Profile fee [2].
- **Which form:** Every international applicant submits the Financial Verification Form. If your family contribution will exceed $5,000 a year, the CSS Profile is also required [2].
- **Apply for aid with your application.** Requests made after admission are not accepted. Aid is for first-year entrants only, not transfers [2].
- **Scholarship:** Every international aid applicant is considered for the Alvarez Scholars Program. Davidson offers no general merit scholarships [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1837 [5].

- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked. Charlotte is 19 miles away [5].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of calculated need for admitted students; need-based; available in ED and RD [2].
- **Forms:** Financial Verification Form, plus the CSS Profile if your contribution is over $5,000 a year [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 4; RD Jan 11 [3].
- **Transfers:** No aid [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 201 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $60,298, total $1,22,46,462 [17].

## 6. How to apply

- Application fee waived for non-US citizens [2]. Deadlines: see above [3].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** BA and BS in 32 majors plus Interdisciplinary Studies, per an older catalog [8].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 8:1 [9].
- **Alvarez Grant Program:** funds career or academic experiences for international students during their four years [10].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 23%, biological and biomedical sciences 16%, psychology 14%, computer and information sciences 7%, physical sciences 6% [14].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 94.6%; 6-year graduation rate 90.1% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 200 international students from nearly 50 countries [10].
- **Office:** International Student Engagement (ISE), which handles F-1 status, cultural adjustment and programming [10].
- **Pre-orientation:** required for F-1 and J-1 students; 17–19 August 2026. It covers banking, phones and staying in status [11].
- **I-20:** Submit the initial I-20 request with your passport and financial documents. I-20s are issued from January (ED I), February (ED II) or May (RD) [11].
- **Host families:** an optional Host Family Program [11].
- **OPT and CPT details:** The International Student Office assists with work permits [8]; procedures weren't on the pages checked.

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** Residence Life assigns rooms and roommates in July. Students live on campus or in their own housing [11].
- **Setting:** small-town Davidson, near Charlotte, with Lake Norman access [5].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Concord, NC, 25.6 km from campus: average temperature 60.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 30.6°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 90.0°F; 47.87 inches of precipitation a year, including 3.3 inches of snowfall [12].
- **Annual Security Report:** Davidson posts its Clery report and crime statistics on its Campus Crime Stats page, https://www.davidson.edu/offices-and-services/public-safety/campus-crime-stats [13].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Before move-in:** student health documents are required [11].
- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 premium is $2,800, and waivers are due by July 25. For international students with no other coverage option, Davidson raises the Davidson College Grant to cover the plan [18].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling Services sits within Student Health and Well-Being and publishes a mental health resources list. Disability services details weren't in the results [15]. The cost of attendance excludes health insurance [7].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the Honor Code [5]; no-loan aid [7]; the Lake Norman campus [6]; Alvarez grants for internationals [10].
- **Notable alumni:** Music-industry alumni include Bertis Downs '78, R.E.M.'s manager, and John Huie '78, co-head of Creative Artists Agency in Nashville [16].
