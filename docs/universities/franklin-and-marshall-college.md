# Franklin & Marshall College

Franklin & Marshall (F&M) is a private liberal arts college in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, with about 1,800 undergraduates. It has one of the higher international shares on our list, at about 17% [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, locked for four years.** F&M meets 100% of demonstrated need for all four years, based on your family contribution and the information you give before admission. Later changes in circumstances are not considered [2].
- **Limited funds, so apply early** [2].
- **Free form option.** File the CSS Profile (Fall 2027/Spring 2028), or F&M's free International Aid Form if the CSS fee is a hardship [2][3].
- **Not seeking aid?** Submit a Certification of Finances [2].
- **Year one or never.** Students admitted without aid can't get aid in later years. Aid lasts up to eight semesters [2].
- **An F&M loan is possible** in packages (our list) [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Type:** a residential liberal arts college in Lancaster, Pennsylvania [6].
- **Enrollment:** 1,799 degree-seeking undergraduates; 17.0% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Housing:** first-years live in one of three residence halls ("College Houses"), each with a House Director and a faculty House Don [7].
- **Founding year, campus size and nearest airport:** Founded in 1787 as Franklin College; a 220-acre campus. The nearest airport wasn't published on the pages checked [16].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for four years; limited funds [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile or the F&M International Aid Form; Certification of Finances for applicants not seeking aid [2][3].
- **Rules:** No reassessment after admission; no aid later for students admitted without it [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2024–2025 H6):** 152 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $58,920, total $89,55,876 [17].

## 6. How to apply

- **English:** TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo is required unless you've had at least three years of English-medium instruction [11].
- **Testing:** a "Standardized Test Option" for applicants whose scores don't reflect their ability [11].
- **Application fee:** $60 per the catalog, $62 per the CDS 2024–25, or a fee waiver [11][12].
- **Aid:** CSS Profile or F&M's free International Aid Form; a Certification of Finances if you're not seeking aid [2][3].

## 7. Academics

- **Class sizes (CDS 2024–25 I3):** 272 of 431 class sections (63%) had fewer than 20 students [17]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** interdisciplinary studies 26%, biological and biomedical sciences 15%, social sciences 14%, business and marketing 13%, physical sciences 6% [15].
- **Careers:** ISS and the Office of International Programs advise on F-1 work rules [7].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 90.2%; 6-year graduation rate 79.1% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 17% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** International Student Services (ISS), in the Joseph International Center. Each student gets an adviser on committing, who guides the F-1 process [7].
- **Services:** I-20s, Social Security numbers, campus job authorization, OPT authorization and monitoring, and advice on leaves and withdrawals [7].
- **Orientation:** mandatory for all international students, about two weeks before classes [7].
- **Breaks:** housing in academic breaks is available for a fee to students who can't go home [7].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Lancaster, Pennsylvania [6].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Lancaster 2Ne Fltr Plt, PA, 3.8 km from campus: average temperature 53.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 24.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 85.0°F; 44.15 inches of precipitation a year, including 21.4 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Housing:** the College House system [7]; paid break housing [7].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report is at https://www.fandm.edu/_resources/pdfs/2025-Annual-Security-Fire-Safety-Report.pdf. F&M updates it by October 1 each year [14].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Counseling:** available through the Student Wellness Center [7].
- **Insurance:** The plan costs $3,688. International students are enrolled automatically and can waive only with verifiable proof of comparable coverage [19].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the College House system [7]; one of the highest international shares on our list [1]; four-year full-need aid for internationals [10].
- **Notable alumni:** F&M's "Diplomats Forever" profiles include surgeon Stanley Dudrick and NBA coach Chris Finch [18].
