# California Institute of Technology

Caltech is a private nonprofit science and engineering university in Pasadena, California, with almost 1,000 undergraduates on a 124-acre campus [10][11].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, but full need for admitted students.** Caltech says financial need "may" be a factor in admission for international applicants because aid funds for them are limited, yet it remains "committed to meeting the demonstrated need of all admitted students" [3].
- **Early Action and aid: the premise that Caltech gives no aid to international Early Action applicants is NOT supported by Caltech's own pages.** Caltech's current International Financial Aid Statement of Intent lists both Restrictive Early Action (Nov 1) and Regular Decision (Jan 4) as deadlines international citizens "may apply using" for first-year aid [3]. The financial aid office publishes a separate Fall 2027 page for international Restrictive Early Action applicants with its own aid deadlines (CSS Profile Nov 15, 2026; priority Dec 15, 2026) [16], and the admissions deadlines page says REA admits should receive aid notifications before the December break [4]. We found no Caltech page that bars international REA applicants from aid. See the note in section 4.
- **Apply for aid at admission or never.** An international citizen who does not receive aid for the first year is not eligible for aid in any later term; the policy "cannot be appealed" [3][15]. Admitted internationals without aid must be able to pay the full cost before committing [3].
- **No aid for international transfer or 3/2 applicants** [3][15].
- **Fewer aided students than at most peers.** In CDS 2025–26 H6, 59 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid, averaging $85,696 (total $5,056,044) [1].
- **Very selective overall.** 428 of 11,285 applicants were admitted for Fall 2025 (3.8%) [1]. International-specific admit counts are not published in the sources we read.
- **English test required** unless English is your native language or your school's main language of instruction (strongly encouraged even then) [5][7].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Campus and setting:** 124 acres in Pasadena, about 10 miles northeast of downtown Los Angeles and 27 miles from the ocean [10][11]. Nearest airport: Hollywood Burbank (Bob Hope), about a 20-minute drive. From LAX, the FlyAway bus and the Metro A Line reach Lake Street station, a 25–30 minute walk from campus [25].
- **Enrollment:** 971 undergraduates and 1,398 graduate students, 2,369 in total (CDS 2025–26 B1) [1][11].
- **Structure:** Six academic divisions; all students take core classes in each. Majors are called "options" [12].
- **Founded; academic calendar:** Not stated on the pages read.
- **Nearby lab:** NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, managed by Caltech, offers research opportunities [10].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for international citizens. Caltech is need-blind only for US citizens and permanent residents. It states it is "committed to meeting the full demonstrated financial need of all admitted students" [3]. Aid covers the full cost of attendance [5].
- **Forms and deadlines (Fall 2027 entry):**
  - Indicate on the admissions application that you plan to seek aid. There is no separate form; the application asks questions to confirm you understand the policy [3][15].
  - Restrictive Early Action applicants: CSS Profile by Nov 15, 2026; priority deadline Dec 15, 2026 (parents' non-tax-filer statement for foreign filers or non-filers, after the admission decision); parents' 2025 national tax return and Scholarship Data Form by Jul 15, 2027, after enrolling [16].
  - Regular Decision applicants: CSS Profile by Feb 15, 2027; parents' non-tax-filer statement and priority deadline Mar 16, 2027; tax return and Scholarship Data Form by Jul 15, 2027 [17].
  - Caltech's own CDS (H7) lists forms for nonresident first-years, but the checkboxes did not extract [1].
- **What a package contains:** The pages read do not describe grants, loans or work expectations for internationals. International students cannot receive Pell Grants, FSEOG, Federal Work-Study or federal Direct loans [15].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6, nonresident undergraduates):** 59 awarded institutional aid; average $85,696; total $5,056,044 [1].
- **Rules:**
  - Renewal: aid recipients reapply every year [3][16][17].
  - Late applicants: a student not offered aid in the first year cannot get aid later; exceptions may be made for citizens of Canada and Mexico [3].
  - Transfers and 3/2: no aid for international applicants [3][15].
  - Outside scholarships: not covered in the pages read.
- **Merit scholarships:** none for internationals stated on the pages checked (caltech.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Net price calculator:** https://npc.collegeboard.org/app/cit (College Board) [18]. Whether it works for internationals: not stated.
- **Early Action and aid (the claim we were asked to confirm):** We could not confirm that Caltech gives no aid to international Early Action applicants. The Statement of Intent (PDF last modified March 2024 per its file properties, linked from the current admissions page) names REA as a permitted route for aid [3], and the financial aid office has an international REA page with Fall 2027 aid deadlines [16]. Our CSV says "no aid for international Early Action applicants"; the sources above contradict that. The CSV source page (admissions international page) says only that you "may apply either Restrictive Early Action or Regular Decision" [5]. Caltech's wording that aid funds for internationals are limited and need may affect admissions [3] explains why some advisers suggest Regular Decision, but that is an inference, not a Caltech rule. Confirm with finaid@caltech.edu before relying on either reading.

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common App or QuestBridge application, with no preference; Caltech is a QuestBridge partner (National College Match) [5]. The page does not say whether QuestBridge is open to internationals.
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [4]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Materials | Decision | Reply by |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Restrictive Early Action | Nov 1, 2026 | Nov 6, 2026 | Mid-December 2026 (admit, defer, deny) | May 1, 2027 |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 4, 2027 | Jan 10, 2027 | Mid-March 2027 (admit, waitlist, deny) | May 1, 2027 |

- **Fee and waivers:** $85; waivers can be requested [7].
- **Required parts:** Two recommendations (one STEM teacher; one humanities or social science teacher); school report from the counselor; transcripts for grades 9–11 or the international equivalent; SAT or ACT; mid-year report when first-term senior grades are available; international financial aid statement of intent (questions in the application) [7][3]. Fall 2027 supplemental essays: five required short responses (STEM academic interest, 150–200 words; one of two "scholarly character" prompts, 200 words; two of three "scientific drive" prompts, 200 words each; a "fun question," 100–150 words) plus one optional academic short answer [9]. Optional: research paper, STEM or non-STEM portfolio [7].
- **English proficiency:** Required unless English is your native language or the main language of instruction at your school (strongly encouraged then). Accepted: IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo; taken before the deadline. Minimums [5]:
  - IELTS: 7 overall and in each area
  - TOEFL taken before Jan 21, 2026: 100 total, 25 in each area; taken on or after Jan 21, 2026: the page lists 5 total and 5 in each area (as printed)
  - Duolingo: 130 overall and in each area
- **International documents:** Official transcripts sent directly by your school; original plus English translation if not in English (an English teacher or school official may translate); no grade conversion needed [5].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** About 26 majors ("options") on the page, plus minors including Aerospace, Control and Dynamical Systems, Neurobiology, Structural Mechanics and Visual Culture [12].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** computer and information sciences 35%, engineering 28%, physical sciences 15%, mathematics and statistics 12%, biological and biomedical sciences 8% [29].
- **Curriculum:** Core curriculum across six divisions; all first-years take the same classes for two terms and do not declare a major until the end of the first year. An Interdisciplinary Studies Program lets students design their own option [12].
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 7 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2: 2,369 students, 333 faculty). Class sections with undergraduates (CDS I3): 78 of 2–9 students, 94 of 10–19, 44 of 20–29, 21 of 30–39, 13 of 40–49, 13 of 50–99, 10 of 100+ (273 in total) [1]. The admissions homepage gives 3:1 (undergraduates) [19].
- **Fields:** Engineering and computer science are offered as options [12]. Pre-health and pre-law advising: Not published on the pages checked. A 3/2 program exists (no aid for internationals) [3][19].
- **Opportunities:** 95% of undergraduates take part in research (school figure) [19]; cross-registration is linked from the site but not described on the page read [12].
- **STEM majors:** Caltech publishes no list. The degree must be on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List. The 24-month extension needs an E-Verify employer and a job directly related to the degree; self-employment doesn't qualify. USCIS must receive the application within 60 days of the STEM I-20 [26].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 97.4% (College Scorecard) [14]; CDS B22 doesn't read from the PDF text. Cohort data in the CDS (final cohort 235): 182 graduated within four years (77.4%, our calculation) and 211 within six years (89.79% as printed) [1]. College Scorecard shows 77.5% (4-year) and 89.8% (6-year) [14].
- **After graduation (Class of 2025):** about 40% graduate or professional school; 40.9% full-time job; 10% fellowship, internship or similar; 9.1% exploring. Median starting salary range $120,000–$129,000 (year not given) [20].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings $166,642 ten years after entry and $124,285 six years after entry. These cover federal-aid recipients only and exclude international students [14].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 154 nonresident undergraduates (CDS B2), of whom 37 are first-years [1]. Number of countries: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Support:** International Student Programs (ISP), part of the International Offices alongside International Scholar Services and the JPL International Office. It offers immigration advising, intercultural programming and advocacy, runs an international student orientation, and holds events such as World Fest [21]. I-20 issuing: not described on the pages read.
- **Work (F-1) [22]:**
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week in term and more in breaks; no ISP approval needed, but complete the I-9 with ISP first.
  - CPT: undergraduates up to 16 hours a week; submit forms at least three weeks ahead.
  - Pre-completion OPT: after one academic year (three quarters) of full-time study; same hour limits.
  - Post-completion OPT: 12 months; STEM OPT extension: 24 more months (up to 36 in total) if your degree is in a STEM field.
- **Practicalities:** Housing over breaks not described on the page read [23]. Student organizations: ISP Art&Soul and Globe Throopers Softball are listed [21].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** Undergraduates are guaranteed housing and must live on campus for their first two years; most stay all four. There are 11 residence halls (eight Houses and three others). First-years must be 17 by October 1 to live on campus [23]. CDS F1: 99.2% of first-years and 93.2% of undergraduates live in college-owned housing [1].
- **Location:** Pasadena, about 10 miles from downtown Los Angeles [10]. Transport: the Metro A Line (Lake Street station) links Pasadena with Union Station in Los Angeles, and Caltech offers a free Metro pass to community members [25].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Pasadena, CA, 2.1 km from campus: average temperature 66.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 45.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 88.0°F; 20.08 inches of precipitation a year, including 0.0 inches of snowfall [24].
- **Safety:** Caltech Security publishes an Annual Security and Fire Safety Report under the Clery Act. The only edition found by search was 2020 (https://security.caltech.edu/documents/13860/F-2020ASR.pdf), so check the Security website for the current one [27].
- **Student body:** College Scorecard shows 54.8% men and 45.2% women among undergraduates [14]. CDS C1 gives 7,772 men and 3,513 women applicants; 136 men and 112 women enrolled [1]. 14% of students are first-generation (school figure) [19].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The Caltech Student Health Insurance Plan costs $5,388 for 2026–27 ($1,796 per term). Students with comparable coverage do not need it. Aid recipients who enroll can ask for the cost to be added to their cost of attendance [8]. No waiver rules specific to international students were published on the pages checked.
- **Services:**
  - Student Wellness Services combines Health and Counseling, open to all eligible students whatever their insurance. Health: 626-395-6393. Counseling, including after-hours crisis support: 626-395-8331. Counseling is short term, with referrals for longer care [28].
  - Disability accommodations go through Caltech Accessibility Services for Students (CASS) in the Center for Student Success [28].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

Caltech lists 49 Nobel Prizes on its About page [11]. Graduates go on to Stanford, MIT, Harvard, Princeton and Caltech for graduate study (top destinations since 2000, school figure) [20]. Notable alumni: Alumni include astronaut Frank Borman, commander of Apollo 8; Intel co-founder Gordon Moore; filmmaker Frank Capra; and food-science writer Harold McGee [30].
