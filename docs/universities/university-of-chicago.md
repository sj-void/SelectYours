# University of Chicago

The University of Chicago is a private nonprofit research university in the Hyde Park neighborhood of Chicago, Illinois, whose undergraduate division is called the College [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

Note on sources: the College admissions and financial aid pages on collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu and financialaid.uchicago.edu load their text with JavaScript, so automated fetch returned only page titles. Facts from those pages below were read through search-engine index text of the pages, and some indexed copies were 12–18 months old. Confirm dates and amounts on the live pages before relying on them. Where the index text and another page disagree, both are given.

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, but full need.** The admissions office says it must be need-aware for international candidates, and that it commits to meeting 100% of demonstrated need for admitted international students who applied for aid. Packages are grant-based with no loan expectation [5][6].
- **Apply for aid now or never.** International students who do not apply for need-based aid during the admissions process cannot apply in later years. International transfer students are not eligible for need-based aid [5][6].
- **The only aid form is the UChicago International Student Financial Aid Worksheet,** completed in the UChicago Account, with proof of family income and assets. International aid applicants should not submit the CSS Profile or the FAFSA, and the Certification of Finances is not sent with the aid application [5][6]. The Promise page still mentions the CSS Profile; the international pages are the more specific source [12].
- **About 269 undergraduate nonresidents got institutional aid.** The average award was $74,765 and the total $20,111,859 (CDS 2025–26 H6) [3].
- **Very selective for everyone.** 4.5% of first-year applicants were admitted in Fall 2025; the CDS does not split applicants by residency [3].
- **Testing is optional,** and English proficiency tests are optional too: the College invites any test score but accepts applications without one if English mastery shows elsewhere [8][13].
- **No interviews.** UChicago does not offer on-campus or alumni interviews [14].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** UChicago's own pages give 1890 for the university's founding and 1892 as the year classes began; sources differ [15].
- **Campus:** Hyde Park, Chicago; UChicago's history pages give varying acreage (200 to 217 acres) [15]. Distance to the nearest airport: Midway is the closer airport. The #55 Garfield bus from the Midway CTA station reaches campus in about 35–45 minutes in total [41].
- **Calendar:** Four-quarter academic year, which UChicago's history pages credit it with inventing [15].
- **Enrollment:** 7,453 undergraduates (7,452 full-time, 1 part-time per B1 rows) and 16,406 students in total (CDS 2025–26 B1, 8,953 graduate) [3]. The university's Fall 2025 page: 19,140 total, 7,496 undergraduate, 11,236 graduate [4].
- **Structure:** Undergraduates enroll in the College; the university also has graduate divisions and professional schools [1][4].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for internationals; "100%" of demonstrated need met for admitted international applicants who applied for aid, loan-free [5][6].
- **Forms and deadlines:**
  - International Student Financial Aid Worksheet, in the UChicago Account, the only form for non-citizens and non-permanent residents. No CSS Profile, no FAFSA [5][6]. CDS H7 lists the international aid application and Certification of Finances for nonresident first-years [3].
  - Supporting documents: proof of family income and assets, such as home-country tax forms translated to English and converted to US dollars, or an employer statement or salary deposit records if no tax filing exists; also receipts and bank statements [6].
  - Aid deadlines: EA and ED I Nov 15, ED II Jan 15, RD Feb 15. A later request is possible only for a significant life change since applying [5][17].
- **What a package contains:** Grants, no loans [5][6]. Our earlier research (the CSV) records an international student stipend as part of the package; the index text we read did not mention it or give an amount (checked: https://financialaid.uchicago.edu/undergraduate/the-application-process/international/).
- **Actual awards (CDS H6):** 269 undergraduate nonresidents received need-based or non-need-based institutional aid; average $74,765; total $20,111,859 [3]. H6 does not separate need-based from merit aid.
- **Rules:**
  - Late applicants: an international student who does not apply during admissions cannot apply later [5][6].
  - Transfers: international transfers are not eligible for need-based aid [6].
  - Renewal and outside scholarships: Not published on the pages checked (uchicago.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Merit scholarships:** Open to first-year international applicants; all are considered automatically with no extra application [5].
- **UChicago tuition guarantee:** From Autumn 2027, free tuition for families earning under $250,000 and free housing and meals under $125,000 (with typical assets). The announcement does not say whether internationals are covered [18].
- **Net price calculator:** none for internationals on the pages checked (checked: https://financialaid.uchicago.edu/undergraduate/how-aid-works/undergraduate-costs/).

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common Application or Coalition (Scoir); the UChicago supplement is part of either [9]. QuestBridge National College Match is accepted and binding [10]; the page text we read does not state whether international students may use it, so treat it as not confirmed.
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [11][17]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Aid by | Decision |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Action | Nov 2, 2026 | Nov 15, 2026 | mid-December |
  | Early Decision I | Nov 2, 2026 | Nov 15, 2026 | mid-December |
  | Early Decision II | Jan 4, 2027 | Jan 15, 2027 | mid-February |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 4, 2027 | Feb 15, 2027 | late March |

- **Fee and fee waivers:** The index text gave $90 on the Required Materials page and $75 on the counselors page, so we cannot state one figure. No fee is charged to students applying for need-based aid, and QuestBridge matches are waived automatically [9][21].
- **Required parts:** the application and UChicago supplement essays; two teacher evaluations; the school report and transcript with counselor recommendation; the mid-year report (due by Feb 1 or as soon as possible; late is not penalized) [9][21].
- **English proficiency:** Optional. The College invites any English test score (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo or others), self-reported scores are accepted, and applying without one is allowed when English mastery shows elsewhere. No minimum score is set, and there is no ESL program or conditional admission [13].
- **International documents:** Transcripts are evaluated by the educational system of each school, and there is no minimum GPA [13]. Predicted grades and national exam rules: Not published on the pages checked.

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** The College's pages give "69 Majors & 68 Minors"; the admissions site says about 70 majors and 60 minors. About a quarter of students double major [22]. Most popular majors (CDS J): not extracted.
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 40%, mathematics and statistics 9%, computer and information sciences 8%, biological and biomedical sciences 8%, physical sciences 7% [39].
- **Curriculum:** A Core of about a third of a student's time: humanities, arts, civilization studies, social sciences, biological and physical sciences, and mathematical sciences [22].
- **Teaching:** Student-faculty ratio 7 to 1 (Fall 2025, 10,979 students to 1,587 faculty, CDS I2). Class sections (CDS I3): 798 with 2–9 students, 465 with 10–19, 190 with 20–29, 92 with 30–39, 39 with 40–49, 91 with 50–99, and 19 with 100 or more; 1,694 in total [3].
- **Engineering:** a BS in Molecular Engineering through the Pritzker School, with bioengineering, chemical engineering and quantum engineering tracks. Any College student can declare it, with no application or cap, once the math and chemistry prerequisites are done. Almost all majors do research [32].
- **Pre-health and pre-law:** The admissions site cites 90% law-school and 91% medical-school acceptance for UChicago candidates, years not stated [23]. Advising details weren't published on the pages checked.
- **Opportunities:** 89 study-abroad programs in 34 cities; participants keep aid eligibility; about 100 summer grants of $7,000 a year for language study or research abroad [24].
- **STEM majors:** Undergraduates in STEM-designated majors may be eligible for the 24-month STEM OPT extension; UChicago publishes no list of designated majors on the pages checked. Eligibility depends on the CIP code on your I-20 matching the federal STEM list.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 99% (CDS B22: 1,715 of 1,726 Fall 2024 entrants). 2019 cohort: 85.2% graduated within 4 years and 95.0% within 6 years (our calculation from the CDS B cohort rows: 1,468 and 1,638 of 1,724) [3].
- **After graduation:** 98% of the Class of 2026 had a post-college offer by graduation (67% employment, 33% graduate school); these are offer rates, not a first-destination survey. Industries among reporters: financial services 32%, consulting 13%, science and technology 13% [25].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings 10 years after entry are $103,872 [37]. These cover federal-aid recipients only, so they exclude internationals.

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 1,432 nonresident undergraduates (19.3% of degree-seeking undergraduates) [3]. The admissions site says classes are 12–15% international from over 80 countries; another page says 15% of the College [26].
- **Support:** The Office of International Affairs (OIA). New F-1 and J-1 students may enter the US up to 30 days before the program start date on the I-20; the OIA check-in form is due within two weeks of arrival (the College's to-do list says five business days, so treat the shorter deadline as the safe one). An optional International Pre-Orientation is run by the College [27].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: automatic benefit for F-1 students after OIA check-in; an SSN support letter from OIA is needed [28].
  - CPT: needs a job offer and OIA authorization; 12 months of full-time CPT removes OPT eligibility [28].
  - OPT: no job offer needed; OIA must recommend it first [28].
- **Break housing (2026–27):**
  - Most halls close over winter break, December 13, 2026 to January 2, 2027.
  - Four stay open: International House, Granville-Grossman, Woodlawn and Vue53.
  - Apply through myHousing (the window ran October 19 to November 15, 2026). It costs $1,100 for the break, and no aid covers it [33].
  - The admissions site says international students can stay in campus housing over breaks [34].
- **Host programs:** Not published on the pages checked (uchicago.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** 100% of first-years and 60% of undergraduates lived in college-owned housing in Fall 2025 (CDS F1) [3]. No four-year housing guarantee was published on the pages checked; the housing office says it cannot guarantee a return to a former House or room [22].
- **Location:** Chicago, a large Midwestern city on Lake Michigan.
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Chicago Midway Airport, IL, 12.7 km from campus: average temperature 52.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 22.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 82.9°F; 34.42 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [36].
- **Safety:** Annual Security and Fire Safety Report 2026–27 (statistics for 2023–2025): https://safety-security.uchicago.edu/stay-informed/annual-security-and-fire-safety-reports [29]. It says violent crime in the Hyde Park–South Kenwood neighborhood fell 27 percent in 2025 compared with 2024, and robberies fell 33 percent [29]. The Clery table did not extract cleanly, so no counts are given.
- **Student body:** CDS B1 degree-seeking undergraduates: 4,195 men and 3,243 women, with no unknown-sex students counted (full-time) [3].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The student health insurance plan (U-SHIP) is the default: students who neither confirm it nor waive with comparable coverage are enrolled for the full policy year (Sept 1, 2026–Aug 31, 2027). Waivers need proof of coverage meeting the university's checklist; the Autumn 2026 deadline was Oct 16, 2026. The page has no international-specific rule. The 2025–26 college letter put the premium at $5,145 [19][20].
- **Services:**
  - UChicago Student Wellness (840 East 59th Street): counseling at no cost to eligible students, mainly those in full-time degree programs on the Hyde Park campus [35].
  - Let's Talk: free drop-in consultations with therapists [35].
  - Scheduled therapy: brief individual, couples and group counseling. Book on 773.834.9355 or online [35].
  - TimelyCare: 12 free virtual sessions a year, plus 24/7 TalkNow access [35].
  - Disability services: Student Disability Services decides eligibility and issues an Accommodation Determination Letter to instructors; students register through the AIM portal [40].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Nobel laureates:** UChicago's official Nobel page counts 102 laureates who were faculty, students or researchers at some point, and marks the alumni among them. Older pages say "more than 80" or 99, so counts vary by page and date [30][15].
- **Notable alumni:** economists Milton Friedman (AM'33) and Gary Becker (AM'53, PhD'55); novelist Saul Bellow (1976 Nobel in Literature); John B. Goodenough (SM'50, PhD'52), co-developer of the lithium-ion battery; Supreme Court Justice John Paul Stevens (AB'41); writer Kurt Vonnegut (AM'71) [30][31].
