# Wesleyan University

Wesleyan is a private liberal arts university in Middletown, Connecticut, with about 3,070 undergraduates and some graduate programs [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Early Decision is possible with aid, but RD is where most aided internationals get in.** Wesleyan says it "will also consider aid-seeking international students through Early Decision", though most aid-seeking international offers are made in Regular Decision [2]. Our earlier list said ED was closed to aid seekers; that is now corrected.
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 3959), with no fee waivers. If you can't afford it, file the ISFAA instead. Everyone also files a Certification of Finances, due Dec 1 (ED I), Feb 1 (ED II) or Feb 15 (RD) [4][3].
- **English:** Advanced English is expected of all students. A proficiency test is optional but "strongly recommended" if English is not your first language [2].
- **Exam-curriculum applicants** (A level, IB and others) must provide their scores [2].
- **No aid for international transfers** (our list) [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1831, by Methodist leaders and Middletown citizens. It opened with 48 students; the charter was approved 26 May 1831 [6].
- **Campus:** about 315–316 acres in the small city of Middletown, Connecticut (two official pages differ by one acre) [7].
- **Enrollment:** about 3,000 full-time undergraduates [7]; 3,067 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. Wesleyan also awards MA and PhD degrees in 11 fields [8].
- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked (wesleyan.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Meets 100% of every admitted student's demonstrated need; limited international funds [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 3959; no fee waivers) or the ISFAA; plus the Certification of Finances [4][3].
- **Aid deadlines (as listed for 2026–27 entry):** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 1; RD Jan 15 [2].
- CDS H6 counts: Not published on the pages checked (wesleyan.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

- **Expected annual cost:** $100,500, used for the Certification of Finances; set yearly by the trustees [3].

## 6. How to apply

- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15 (decision mid-December); ED II and RD Jan 1 [4].
- **English and testing:** See the key points [2].

## 7. Academics

- **Curriculum:** open, with student choice at its core [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 26%, psychology 14%, visual and performing arts 11%, area, ethnic and gender studies 10%, biological and biomedical sciences 7% [16].
- **Programs:** 46 departments and 45 major fields; BA degrees, plus MA and PhD in 11 fields [8].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 7:1 [7]. An older catalog said about three-quarters of courses have fewer than 20 students [8].
- **STEM OPT:** OISA publishes guidance on filing for the STEM OPT extension [9].
- **Study abroad:** Not published on the pages checked (wesleyan.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 94.9%; 6-year graduation rate 90.0% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Office of International Student Affairs (OISA). It runs International Student Orientation and supports students throughout their time at Wesleyan [10].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: paid through Wesleyan payroll; no prior OISA authorization needed; 20 hours a week combined during semesters [11].
  - CPT: needed for any off-campus work, internship or training. Usable up to twice during your degree [12].
  - OPT: up to 12 months after at least one full year and a declared major. Steps: an OISA workshop, a $150 OISA SEVIS maintenance fee, and a portal request. File with USCIS only after you receive the OPT I-20; the USCIS fee is $470 online. Report your job to OISA within 10 days [13].
- **Returning from leave:** You need two consecutive full-time semesters before you can get CPT or OPT [14].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** in Middletown, Connecticut [7].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Meriden Markham Municipal Airport, CT, 15.1 km from campus: average temperature 50.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 21.1°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.7°F; 44.86 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [15].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Campus Security and Fire Safety Report is at https://www.wesleyan.edu/publicsafety/pdf/ASR2025Final1.21.pdf [20].
- **Housing:** Undergraduates must live on campus all four years (page may be dated). Winter break requests aren't guaranteed, and approved students may get a temporary room [18].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 plan costs $3,267. International plans not based in the US don't count as comparable; the waiver deadline was August 19, 2026 [21].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) gives short-term care with after-hours crisis support. Accessibility Services handles documented disabilities, with accommodations for classes, housing and dining; they aren't retroactive [17].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** an open curriculum [7]; a 7:1 student–faculty ratio [7]; graduate programs at a liberal arts university [8]; ED with aid now possible for internationals [2].
- **Notable alumni:** Featured alumni include Lin-Manuel Miranda, Mad Men creator Matthew Weiner and former Vermont governor Peter Shumlin [19].
