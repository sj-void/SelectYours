# Smith College

Smith is a private liberal arts college for women in Northampton, Massachusetts, with about 2,540 undergraduates. It is part of the Five College Consortium [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **No loans, full documented need.** Smith has removed loans from need-based packages and replaced them with grants. It meets the full documented need of all admitted students who apply for aid by the deadlines [2].
- **Apply for aid before admission, or never.** Internationals who miss the aid deadlines can never receive Smith aid [2].
- **Health insurance covered for aided internationals.** A health grant covers the $4,030 insurance cost for international students receiving need-based aid [2].
- **Documents:** CSS Profile (code 3762), plus a translated, signed parent tax return or the International Parent Non-Filer form [2].
- **Next 150 pledge:** Free tuition for all financially qualifying undergraduates, based on income and assets and reviewed annually [4]. The pages checked don't say whether it covers internationals.

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1871; opened in 1875 with 14 students [5].
- **Campus:** 147 contiguous acres. The Botanic Garden spans the whole campus, including an arboretum and specialty gardens [5].
- **Enrollment:** more than 2,500 undergraduates [5]; 2,544 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. Smith educates women at undergraduate level and admits men and women as graduate students [5].
- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked (smith.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Full documented need met for admitted aid applicants who meet the deadlines; no loans [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 3762), opening Oct 1; parent tax return (translated and signed) or the International Parent Non-Filer form [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 6; RD Jan 25; transfer Mar 11 [2].
- **Health:** Aided internationals get a health grant [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2024–2025 H6):** total institutional aid to nonresident undergraduates $1,36,58,745 [13]. The count and average don't read from the PDF text.

## 7. Academics

- **Curriculum:** open. The only requirement is an introductory writing course. More than 1,200 courses in more than 80 areas of study [5].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 8:1 in most years [5].
- **Five College Consortium:** cross-registration with Amherst, Hampshire, Mount Holyoke and UMass Amherst. The details weren't on the pages checked.
- **STEM OPT:** up to 24 extra months for eligible STEM fields [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 16%, biological and biomedical sciences 10%, physical sciences 8%, computer and information sciences 8%, visual and performing arts 7% [11].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 94.3%; 6-year graduation rate 85.8% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the International Students and Scholars Office (ISSO). It advises on immigration, visas, travel, work authorization and practical training [6].
- **Pre-orientation:** International Student Pre-Orientation ran 27 August to 1 September 2026. It is required for students on a college-sponsored visa, and F-1 immigration workshops are mandatory [7].
- **Work:**
  - On campus only; no off-campus jobs on a student visa except authorized training [7].
  - You need a Social Security number, and ISSO runs shuttles to the Holyoke SSA office. Jobs outside Dining Services need an Employment Evidence Form [7].
  - OPT: up to one year, plus up to 24 months for STEM [6].
- **Travel:** Your I-20 travel signature must be less than 12 months old when you re-enter the US [6].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Northampton, Massachusetts, on a 147-acre botanic-garden campus [5].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Amherst, MA, 11.2 km from campus: average temperature 48.2°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 16.7°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 80.9°F; 46.59 inches of precipitation a year, including 36.5 inches of snowfall [9].
- **Housing:** Off-campus students get a food and housing allowance of $12,300 (2026–27) [8]. A housing guarantee wasn't stated on the pages checked.
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security & Fire Safety Report (published October 2026; crime statistics in Section 7) is at https://www.smith.edu/sites/default/files/2026-09/2025%20Clery%20Report-google-pdf-export-accessible.pdf [10].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- Insurance costs $4,030; aided internationals receive a health grant to cover it [2].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** an open curriculum [5]; no-loan aid with a health grant for aided internationals [2]; Five College cross-registration; a botanic-garden campus [5].
- **Notable alumnae:** Alumnae include Julia Child, Gloria Steinem, Sylvia Plath, Barbara Bush, Nancy Reagan and Margaret Mitchell [12].
