# Bryn Mawr College

Bryn Mawr is a private liberal arts college for women near Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. It has about 1,360 undergraduates and a graduate school, and belongs to the Tri-College Consortium with Haverford and Swarthmore [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

Bryn Mawr's CDS 2025–26 is a fillable PDF whose values can't be extracted as text, so admissions numbers here come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1][3].

## Key points for international applicants

- **CSS Profile is required; no substitute accepted** (college code 2049) [6][7].
- **Applicants not seeking aid file a Declaration of Finances** instead [8].
- **Fixed family contribution.** Your contribution stays the same for four years, and grant aid rises with cost increases [4].
- **Loans possible.** Packages may include an institutional loan, except for families with income under $110,000 and assets under $500,000 (our list, from Bryn Mawr's international page) [5].
- **No merit scholarships for internationals** [9].
- **Fee waivers** for the application: request by Nov 23 (ED I), Jan 8 (ED II) or Jan 29 (RD) [7].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 1,359 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. Bryn Mawr also has a Graduate School of Arts and Sciences [12].
- **Consortium:** Tri-College Consortium with Haverford and Swarthmore, plus cross-registration with Penn.
- **Founding year and campus size:** Bryn Mawr opened in 1885 and has a 135-acre campus [21].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-based aid only. Our list classes Bryn Mawr as need-sensitive for internationals [5][9].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 2049), with no substitute [6][7].
- **Deadlines:** Same as admission: ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 1; RD Jan 15 [6].
- **Package:** The family contribution is fixed for four years [4]. A Bryn Mawr loan may be included unless family income is under $110,000 and assets under $500,000 [5].
- CDS H6 counts: Not available. The CDS 2025–26 is a fillable PDF whose values don't extract as text [3].

The Beacon initiative's free tuition for families earning under $175,000 applies to US applicants [11]. International aid applicants apply once, at admission, and their family contribution stays the same for four years [11].

## 6. How to apply

- **Eligibility:** Bryn Mawr considers eligible "all individuals who have identified and continue to identify as women", including cisgender and trans women. It also considers intersex people who do not identify as male, and people assigned female at birth who have not transitioned to male or who identify outside the gender binary [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 1; RD Jan 15; transfer Mar 1 [6].
- **Financial documents:** CSS Profile for aid applicants; Declaration of Finances for applicants not seeking aid [7][8].

## 7. Academics

- **PhDs:** 17th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (12.3%) [10].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 20%, biological and biomedical sciences 13%, psychology 10%, foreign languages 8%, mathematics and statistics 8% [15].
- **Cross-registration:** Haverford, Swarthmore and Penn.
- **Majors:** More than 35 majors and more than 50 minors and concentrations [24].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (brynmawr.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 92.2%; 6-year graduation rate 83.2% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student and Scholar Advising (ISSA), intl@brynmawr.edu. It handles immigration advising, programming and advocacy, and advises on nonresident tax [12].
- **I-20:** Admitted students submit documents through the GPS portal. F-1 degree students get a Form I-20 and must show financial resources for one year [12].
- **Arrival:** International Student Arrival Day is 24 August 2027, and there's no campus housing before it. You must complete immigration check-in within 10 days of entering the US [12].
- **Travel:** F-1 students must contact ISSA before travelling abroad [13].
- **Work rules (OPT/CPT):** Bryn Mawr caps on-campus work for undergraduates at 17.5 hours a week while classes are in session (20 for graduate students). Its international advising pages explain OPT applications [23].
- **Break housing:** Winter break housing is limited to one hall (Rhoads), and international students are among the groups who may request it [17].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania, in the Philadelphia suburbs [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Norristown, PA, 9.4 km from campus: average temperature 55.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 25.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 86.4°F; 50.65 inches of precipitation a year, including 25.3 inches of snowfall [14].
- **Housing guarantee:** Students are expected to live in campus housing and take the meal plan for all four years; a limited number of releases are given each spring by lottery [22].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security Report and Annual Fire Safety Report is at https://www.brynmawr.edu/sites/default/files/media/documents/2025-09/Annual%20Security%20and%20Fire%20Safety%20Clery%20Report%20Final%209.23.25_FINAL__0.pdf; it is emailed to the community by October 1 each year [19].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** an international student health insurance estimate of $2,794. Waiver rules weren't stated [13].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Counseling Service handles emotional health. Access Services provides reasonable accommodations for documented learning, physical or psychological disabilities [16].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a women's college with an inclusive eligibility policy [2]; Tri-Co and Penn cross-registration; 17th nationally for PhDs per graduate [10].
- **Notable alumni:** Bryn Mawr's notable alumnae include Pulitzer Prize-winning playwright Paula A. Vogel (Class of 1973) [18].
