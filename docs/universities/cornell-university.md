# Cornell University

Cornell University is a private nonprofit research university in Ithaca, New York, made up of several undergraduate colleges and schools that admit and read applications separately [1][12].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

**Note on the CDS.** The Cornell 2025–26 Common Data Set is a fillable PDF form. Its text extracts without reliable row alignment, so values were read by position and checked for internal consistency (for example, the C1 genders sum to the totals and the C1 residency columns sum to the totals; the class-size row sums to its total). Items marked "re-check" could not be fully verified. Re-check against the PDF [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, full need, but with loans.** Cornell says it "meets 100 percent of admitted international undergraduates' demonstrated financial need." Packages contain Cornell grants, a work-study award and "reasonable" student loans that depend on family income [2][8].
- **Apply for aid at admission.** Internationals who want Cornell grant aid at any point must submit the CSS Profile (code 2098) when they apply. International aid deadlines equal the application deadlines: Nov 1 (ED) and Jan 2 (RD) [2][3]. Fee waivers for the CSS Profile are generally not available to internationals [2].
- **Aid numbers (CDS H6):** 318 degree-seeking undergraduate nonresidents received institutional aid in Fall 2025, with an average award of $88,263 and a total of $28,067,649 [1]. That is about 21% of the 1,523 international undergraduates (our calculation).
- **Very selective for internationals.** In Fall 2025, 664 of 18,566 international applicants were admitted (3.6%, our calculation) and 389 enrolled (CDS C1) [1].
- **SAT or ACT is required** for Fall 2027, with no international exemption stated [6][7].
- **English test:** Needed unless English is your native language or the language of instruction for the past four years. TOEFL (Jan 2026 or later scale) minimum 5.0, recommended 5.5; IELTS 7.5; Duolingo 130; Cambridge C1/C2 191 [7].
- **Taxes:** Grant aid above tuition and mandatory fees is taxable income; Cornell withholds 14% of that excess unless a tax treaty applies [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 16,138 undergraduates, of whom 3,827 are first-years (CDS B1, Oct 15, 2025); total 19,941 per the CDS [1].
- **Colleges and schools:** Cornell's admissions site lists majors across the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences; Architecture, Art, and Planning; Arts & Sciences; Human Ecology; the Bowers College of Computing and Information Science; the Duffield College of Engineering; the SC Johnson College of Business; the School of Industrial and Labor Relations; and the Brooks School of Public Policy [9]. Applications are reviewed by the college or school you apply to [10].
- **Founded, campus size and academic calendar:** Founded in 1865 by Ezra Cornell and Andrew Dickson White; it opened in 1868. Campus size and academic calendar weren't published on the pages checked [31].
- **Nearest airport:** Ithaca Tompkins Regional is about 10 minutes away, linked by TCAT Route 32 or Uber and Lyft. Syracuse Hancock International, with more airlines, is about 90 minutes by car [28].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Admission is need-aware for internationals. Cornell "meets 100 percent of admitted international undergraduates' demonstrated financial need" [2][8].
- **Forms and deadlines:**
  - CSS Profile (code 2098), submitted when applying for admission, as soon as possible after Oct 1, 2026 [2].
  - Aid deadlines for internationals: ED Nov 1, 2026; RD Jan 2, 2027; transfers Mar 15, 2027 [2][3].
  - Documents are requested through the College Board IDOC service; upload only signed copies on request. Divorced or separated parents each file a CSS Profile [2].
  - ISFAA and certification of finances: not mentioned on the pages read [2].
  - The CDS (H7) lists forms for nonresident first-years; the form cell doesn't read from the PDF text [1].
- **What a package contains:** Cornell grants, an annual work-study award and "reasonable" student loans that depend on total family income. Amounts and the summer expectation are not stated [2][8].
- **When you hear:** A preliminary Cornell Aid Estimate arrives after decisions; the official aid offer comes around early April [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS H6, Fall 2025):** 318 aided; average $88,263; total $28,067,649 [1].
- **Rules:**
  - Renewal: returning international students file the CSS Profile every year (for 2027–28 by Feb 2, 2027). Cornell aims to keep the family contribution and aid offer consistent year to year. Documents are requested only if finances changed significantly [11].
  - Aid after admission: international applicants who anticipate needing aid must apply by the posted deadline or lose eligibility later [3].
  - Outside scholarships: not addressed on the pages read.
  - Transfers: international transfers apply with the CSS Profile by Mar 15, 2027 [2].
- **Merit scholarships:** none for internationals stated on the pages checked. The Cornell Commitment programs (Cornell Tradition, Presidential Research Scholars, Meinig National Scholars) include scholarships for aid-eligible students; the page does not say if internationals are eligible [13].
- **Net price calculator:** https://npc.collegeboard.org/app/cornell. Cornell says the calculators are not intended for international undergraduates; ask the aid office [14].

**Consistency with our CSV:** No contradiction. The CSV records grants, work-study and student loans, and "apply at admission or never"; the pages above confirm this. We did not verify the CSV's "no partial awards" note [2][3].

## 6. How to apply

- **Platform:** Common App with the Cornell supplement, which has college- or school-specific essays [3]. A QuestBridge applicants page exists on the admissions site; whether internationals can use it was not stated [4].
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [3]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Other materials by | Aid deadline (international) | Decision |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Decision | Nov 1, 2026 | Nov 13, 2026 | Nov 1, 2026 | Mid-December |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 2, 2027 | Jan 19, 2027 | Jan 2, 2027 | Late March |

  Optional music supplement: Nov 6 (ED) or Jan 7 (RD) [3]. The page does not name the entry year; the dates match the Fall 2027 aid deadlines on the aid page [2].
- **Fee and fee waivers:** $85. A waiver is available through the Common App with extra Cornell questions and no documents. The page ties it to planning to apply for aid [3].
- **Required parts:** An official transcript with in-progress courses (mid-year grades when available); a school report and counselor recommendation; two teacher recommendations; the Cornell supplement essays; SAT or ACT scores [3].
- **English proficiency:** See key points. Waived if English is your native language or the primary language of instruction for the past four years. Scores can be self-reported, and enrolling students send official scores [7].
- **International documents:** Official documents only, no photocopies. Transcripts must be translated into English and sent by a school official [10]. Students with 12 or more college credits since high school, or enrolled full-time at a university, usually apply as transfers [10]. Cornell staff issue the I-20 after admission [10]. A military-service deferral is possible [10].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 81 majors listed on the admissions site (minors: count not stated) [9].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** computer and information sciences 19%, engineering 15%, business and marketing 11%, agriculture 11%, biological and biomedical sciences 10% [26].
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 12 to 1 (CDS I2; 22,155 students to 1,829 faculty). In Fall 2025, 2,391 undergraduate class sections: 345 had 2–9 students, 921 had 10–19, 321 had 20–29, 180 had 30–39, 143 had 40–49, 295 had 50–99 and 186 had 100 or more (CDS I3). About 53% had fewer than 20 students (our calculation) [1].
- **Fields:** The Duffield College of Engineering offers majors such as biomedical, electrical and computer, and mechanical engineering. Computer science is offered through Arts & Sciences, Engineering and the Bowers College [9]. Pre-med: biological sciences is the most common major, but any major works if the required courses are taken [10].
- **Opportunities:** Cornell Commitment programs include the Presidential Research Scholars [13]. Global Internships are open to international students (applications due Dec 15) [16]. Study abroad is open in every major and college, with a typical 3.0 GPA requirement [24]. Study-abroad share, cross-registration and honors: Not published on the pages checked.
- **STEM majors:** Cornell publishes no list. Check that the Major 1 or Major 2 code on your I-20 is on the federal STEM list. STEM OPT also needs an E-Verify employer and at least 20 hours a week of paid work in your field. Cornell charges $225 to process OPT and $325 for the STEM extension [25].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 98% (CDS 2025–26 B22: 3,448 of 3,525 Fall 2024 entrants) [1]. Six-year graduation rate 95% for the Fall 2019 cohort (3,001 of 3,174); four-year rate 85.8% (2,724 of 3,174, our calculation) (CDS B4–B21) [1].
- **After graduation:**
  - University-wide, about 91.3–91.4% of graduates were employed or in graduate school within six months. Cornell Career Services pages give knowledge rates of 72.1% and 82.3% from different data snapshots [19].
  - College of Arts & Sciences, Class of 2025 (83% knowledge rate, n=1,065): 66.6% employed, 28.8% in graduate school, 1.3% seeking work [20].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings are $113,531 ten years after entry and $97,242 six years after entry. They cover federal-aid recipients only and exclude international students [12].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 1,523 degree-seeking international undergraduates (CDS B2) [1]. College Scorecard shows 9.55% [12]. The Office of Global Learning describes its international services as helping students and scholars "from 130 countries" (all students and scholars, not undergraduates only) [16].
- **Support:** International Services, part of the Office of Global Learning. Undergraduate Admissions staff issue the I-20; the office runs status activation, the myStatus portal, a free immigration consultation with attorneys, and an international career-services channel. Orientation details: Not published on the pages checked (cornell.edu, searched 2026-11-23) [16][10].
- **Work:**
  - CPT: applications reopened after a court order blocked changes to CPT eligibility [16].
  - OPT: pre-completion OPT can be full time in summer and winter breaks. Post-completion OPT and the 24-month STEM extension follow federal rules, with Cornell fees of $225 and $325 [25].
  - On-campus work: Not published for Ithaca undergraduates on the pages checked.
  - Apply for a Social Security number through International Services [16].
- **Practicalities:** Break housing: Over winter break 2026–27, residents of Holland International Living Center and Loving House can stay in their rooms at no extra cost; other halls charge extra [27]. Mentor programs: Not published on the pages checked (cornell.edu, searched 2026-11-23). The CDS lists an International Student Organization among campus activities [1].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** The residential campus provides housing for all first- and second-year students, in residence halls or Program Houses [17]. CDS F1 (values reconstructed from misaligned cells; re-check): about 98.9% of first-years and 55.4% of undergraduates live in college housing [1].
- **Location:** Ithaca, a small city [12]. Nearest airport: Not published on the pages checked (cornell.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Ithaca Cornell Univ, NY, 2.8 km from campus: average temperature 46.3°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 17.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 78.0°F; 38.29 inches of precipitation a year, including 62.9 inches of snowfall [21].
- **Safety:** Annual Security Report: the 2026 Annual Security Report ("Campus Watch") is at https://publicsafety.cornell.edu/clery/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2026-Campus_Watch_2026_FINAL-ua.pdf [30]. Federal data: https://ope.ed.gov/campussafety.
- **Student body:** CDS B1: men 7,345 and women 8,588 degree-seeking undergraduates (full- and part-time), plus a few of unknown sex [1]. College Scorecard: 45.4% men, 54.6% women [12].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Required. Students meet the requirement by proving qualifying coverage or by being enrolled automatically in the Student Health Plan (SHP). A waiver needs other coverage that meets Cornell's criteria and is not guaranteed. New spring 2027 students must submit waivers by Jan 21 [15]. 2026–27 premium: $4,092 for 12 months; new fall students are billed $1,705 in fall and $2,046 in spring [23]. International-specific rules: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Services:** Cornell Health (medical and mental health care), Student Support and Advocacy Services, Cornell Minds Matter, the peer-support line EARS, and Student Disability Services [18].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Distinctive features:** a Global Internships program open to international students, and study abroad in every college [16][24].
- **Notable alumni:** Cornell's Nobel-winning alumni include Claudia Goldin '67, the first woman to win an unshared Nobel in economics, and Robert Fogel '48 [29].
