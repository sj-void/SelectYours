# Colby College

Colby is a private liberal arts college in Waterville, Maine, with about 2,400 students. More than 10% are not US citizens, and students come from over 80 countries [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Colby Common Data Set was found online, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need with grants and work, no loans.** Colby "meets 100 percent of demonstrated need for all admitted students, including international students". Packages combine grants and employment [2].
- **CSS Profile (code 3280), with fee waivers available.** It is due Nov 15 (ED I), Jan 4 (ED II) or Jan 15 (RD). Send no other aid documents unless asked [2].
- **The English test is required, not optional,** if English is neither your first language nor your school's language of instruction. Colby accepts TOEFL iBT or Home Edition, IELTS Academic or Duolingo, superscores the TOEFL and accepts MyBest scores [2].
- **All application materials must be in English** [2]. Interviews are not required, but Colby accepts video interviews from Duolingo, Vericant and InitialView [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1813. Colby is the 12th-oldest liberal arts college in the US [8].
- **Campus:** 714 acres on Mayflower Hill, above downtown Waterville in central Maine. The college moved there from downtown in 1952 [8].
- **Enrollment:** About 2,400 students from 45+ states and 80+ countries [9]. IPEDS counts 2,407 degree-seeking undergraduates [1].
- **Housing:** Nearly 100% of students live on campus [9].
- **Nearest airports:** Not confirmed on current pages. Older Colby catalogues point to Portland Jetport and Bangor International (checked: colby.edu, 2026-11-23).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Colby meets 100% of demonstrated need for all admitted students, internationals included. Our list classes Colby as need-aware for internationals [2][7].
- **Forms and deadlines:** CSS Profile (code 3280), plus the Non-Custodial Parent CSS Profile or a waiver where relevant. Deadlines: ED I Nov 15, ED II Jan 4, RD Jan 15 [2].
- **What a package contains:** Grants and employment. Families may borrow on their own for their contribution, and payment plans and Flywire are available [2].
- **Outside scholarships:** Encouraged [2].
- CDS H6 counts: Not available (no CDS found).

## 6. How to apply

- **Process:** Internationals follow the same process as domestic applicants [2]. Colby also accepts QuestBridge applicants; whether QuestBridge is open to internationals is not stated [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 4; RD Jan 4 for admission and Jan 15 for aid [5].
- **English and documents:** See the key points [2].

## 7. Academics

- **AI institute:** The Davis Institute for Artificial Intelligence opened in 2021, the first such institute at a liberal arts college [8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 28%, biological and biomedical sciences 13%, psychology 9%, natural resources 8%, computer and information sciences 7% [16].
- **Global study:** DavisConnects runs Global Education Opportunities, including study abroad [10].
- **Majors:** 48 majors and 38 minors (a Colby news release gives 46 and 37) [20].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (colby.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 93.7%; 6-year graduation rate 87.0% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].
- Earnings data in College Scorecard cover federal-aid recipients only and exclude international students [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** More than 10% of students are non-US citizens, from 80+ countries [2][9].
- **Support:** The International Student Program is open to F-1 holders and dual citizens. It pairs each first-year with an upperclass "Buddie" mentor [11].
- **Orientation:** Pre-Orientation is mandatory for incoming F-1 students, and international students can move in early [11].
- **Visas and work:** The Office of International Programs helps with visa-compliant on- and off-campus jobs, CPT, OPT and post-graduation planning. You must check with it before dropping classes, taking leave, changing majors or working off campus [11][12].
- **I-20:** Non-citizens submit the International Student Certification of Finances (ISCOF), showing funding for four years. Documents must be dated within three months [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** Nearly 100% of students live on campus [9].
- **Setting:** Mayflower Hill, overlooking Waterville [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Waterville Trtmt Plt, ME, 4.2 km from campus: average temperature 45.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 12.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 78.1°F; 43.36 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [14].
- **Carbon neutrality:** Colby was the fourth US college or university to achieve it [8].
- **Annual Security Report:** Colby's Clery report is downloadable from its Campus Safety page, https://life.colby.edu/your-safety/campus-safety/ [15]. The Student Handbook sets out community standards [12].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 premium is $2,628 a year. International students are enrolled automatically; a waiver is considered only for a plan from a US-based insurer (foreign and travel plans don't qualify) [19].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Access & Disability Services handles academic, housing and dining accommodations (request with documentation, meet the director, committee review; housing accommodations renewed yearly). Counseling Services offers individual and group counseling [17].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the Davis Institute for AI [8]; DavisConnects career and global programs [10]; a carbon-neutral campus [8]; NESCAC Division III athletics [9].
- **Notable alumni:** Colby's alumni stories include Mike Federle '81, CEO of Forbes [18].
