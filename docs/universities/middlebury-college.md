# Middlebury College

Middlebury is a private liberal arts college in Middlebury, Vermont. It has about 2,740 undergraduates and also runs graduate and language schools [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, but loans and a minimum contribution.** Middlebury meets the full demonstrated need of accepted internationals with a mix of work, loans and grants. There is generally at least a minimum family contribution [2].
- **Request aid with your application,** or not at all [4].
- **CSS Profile only.** Middlebury doesn't accept the ISFAA or a paper CSS Profile, and leaves fee waivers to the College Board [2][5].
- **Tax papers:** Parents' non-US tax forms or earnings statements, translated into English, or a non-filing statement [5].
- **English-test minimums (recommended):** TOEFL iBT 5.5 on the new scale (105 for tests before 21 Jan 2026), Duolingo 130, IELTS 7.0 or Cambridge C1 Advanced. You're exempt if English is your native language, or if your last three or four years of high school were taught entirely in English [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 2,738 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].
- **Calendar:** fall and spring semesters plus a winter J-term. Students must take at least two of four J-terms, within the 36 course credits needed to graduate [7].
- **Schools Abroad:** Middlebury runs its own study-abroad schools, for example in the UK, Germany and China [8].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Chartered on November 1, 1800. Campus size wasn't published on the pages checked [24].
- **Nearest airport:** Nearest airports: Burlington International (35 miles), Albany (112 miles), Montréal–Trudeau (140 miles) and Boston Logan (189 miles) [18].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware, need-blind only as far as funds allow. Full demonstrated need is met with work, loans and grants, usually with a minimum family contribution [2].
- **Forms:** Electronic CSS Profile only; translated parent tax documents [2][5].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15, 2026; ED II Jan 4, 2027; RD Feb 1, 2027; transfer Mar 1, 2027 [5].
- **Loans:** Described as limited in amount [6].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2024–2025 H6):** 198 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $83,697, total $1,65,72,104 [21].

## 7. Academics

- **J-term:** at least two winter terms required [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 32%, biological and biomedical sciences 14%, natural resources 10%, computer and information sciences 7%, psychology 7% [15].
- **Study abroad:** Middlebury Schools Abroad. Fees vary by site (for example UK tuition $28,800 a semester), plus a $2,000-a-semester study-abroad fee [8].
- **CPT for undergraduates:** tied to J-term internships and summer fieldwork courses for credit, not available during fall or spring [12].
- **Number of majors:** Not published on the pages checked (middlebury.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Class sizes (CDS 2024–25 I3):** 311 of 523 class sections (59%) had fewer than 20 students [21]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 93.7%; 6-year graduation rate 92.5% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), which also issues I-20s [3][12].
- **Work:**
  - CPT: needs approval before any training starts, including orientation. Unauthorized work can end your SEVIS record. Usually requires a full year in F-1 status [12].
  - OPT: 12 months, before or after graduation, not tied to an employer, and no job offer needed. Start with the ISSS OPT intake form, and don't work until you have your EAD card and its start date [13].
- **Information sessions:** ISSS holds OPT and CPT sessions each term [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Middlebury, Vermont [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Cornwall, VT, 6.4 km from campus: average temperature 45.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 12.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 78.2°F; 38.67 inches of precipitation a year, including 58.6 inches of snowfall [14].
- **Housing guarantee:** Students must live on campus until they graduate; a limited number of seniors are released to live off campus [22].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2026 Middlebury College Annual Security and Fire Safety Report is at https://www.middlebury.edu/sites/default/files/2026-09/Middlebury_College_Annual_Security_and_Fire_Safety_Report_0.pdf [20].
- **Housing:** Residence halls close over winter break; staying needs approval [17].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 annual plan costs $3,812 (August 15, 2026 to August 14, 2027); enrol-or-waive decisions were due October 1, 2026 [23].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Disability Resource Center gives confidential services and accommodations for needs affecting learning, vision, hearing, speech, mobility, and physical and psychological health. Counseling details weren't in the results [16].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** Middlebury Schools Abroad and language study [8]; the J-term [7]; a full-need commitment for accepted internationals [2].
- **Notable alumni:** Middlebury notes alumnus Alexander Twilight as the first person of color to graduate from an American college. Its film alumni include Shawn Ryan, creator of The Shield [19].
