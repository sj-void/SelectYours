# Vanderbilt University

Vanderbilt is a private nonprofit research university in Nashville, Tennessee, on a 340.7-acre campus. Its undergraduates enroll in the College of Arts and Science, the School of Engineering, Peabody College or the Blair School of Music [10][11][12].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, and a limited number of aid places.** Vanderbilt offers need-based scholarships to "a limited number of first-year international undergraduate applicants," and decisions for aid applicants are need-aware [3][7]. In Fall 2026 it offered need and/or merit aid to 89 international students from 54 countries; awards ran from $16,815 to $99,998 a year for four years [3].
- **Full-need promise is on the admissions FAQ, not on the aid pages.** The first-year-process page says admitted internationals "will have 100% of their demonstrated need met with grants and/or scholarships" [4]. The international costs page and the financial aid office page do not repeat that promise for internationals [3][7]. See "Contradictions".
- **Aid must be requested at application.** Submit the CSS Profile or the ISFAA. International students not seeking aid must be able to pay the full four-year cost [3][7]. Transfer applicants who are not US citizens or eligible non-citizens get no need-based aid; transfer applications that request aid are withdrawn [3].
- **Actual aid (CDS H6):** 266 international degree-seeking undergraduates received institutional aid in Fall 2025, averaging $79,879 (total $21,247,863) [1].
- **Testing is optional for Fall 2027 and 2028, then required for Fall 2029.** Internationals must show English proficiency unless they score above 26 on ACT English or above 630 on SAT ERW [4][5].
- **Merit scholarships are open to internationals.** Apply by December 1, 2026 [2][3][6].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1873 [10].
- **Campus:** 340.7 acres in Nashville, about 1.5 miles from downtown [10][12].
- **Enrollment (Fall 2025):** 7,366 undergraduates (7,305 full-time, 61 part-time) and 13,586 students in total (CDS B1) [1]. The university's quick-facts page rounds to "7,500+" undergraduates and "6,300+" graduate and professional students [10].
- **Schools:** 12 schools and colleges [10][12]. Undergraduates enroll in the College of Arts and Science, the School of Engineering, Peabody College or the Blair School of Music; the College of Connected Computing also appears in the admissions site's list of schools [11].
- **Calendar:** semesters. In 2026–27, fall classes run August 26 to December 10; spring classes begin January 11; commencement is May 14. Maymester and a summer session are offered [23].
- **Nearest airport:** Nashville International (BNA), less than 10 miles away, about a 15–30 minute drive [24].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** "Vanderbilt is need-aware for international citizens" [7]. The international costs page says admission decisions for aid applicants are need-aware and weigh academic qualities, leadership and community engagement, financial need and available resources [3]. The admissions FAQ says internationals admitted with need "will have 100% of their demonstrated need met with grants and/or scholarships" [4].
- **Forms and deadlines:** CSS Profile or ISFAA (International Student Financial Aid Application). The financial aid office page says "the only document we require is the PROFILE" at submission, though Vanderbilt may ask for verification [3][7]. Priority dates: ED I Nov 1, 2026; ED II Jan 1, 2027; RD Feb 1, 2027 [2][3]. FAFSA is for US citizens and eligible non-citizens [3].
- **What a package contains:** The FAQ says need is met with grants and/or scholarships [4]. Expected student contribution, work or summer earnings for internationals: Not published on the pages we read [3][7]. The matriculation deposit is waived for need-based aid recipients [4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 266 international degree-seeking undergraduates received institutional aid; average $79,879; total $21,247,863 [1]. In Fall 2026 the admissions office reports 89 international students offered need and/or merit aid, with awards from $16,815 to $99,998 a year (four-year awards) [3].
- **Rules:**
  - Renewal: the aid office calls the aid "renewable"; conditions not published on the pages we read [7].
  - Late applicants and later years: Not published on the pages we read [3][7].
  - Outside scholarships: the page encourages looking for outside funding (IEFA, eduPass). It gives no offset rule [3].
  - Transfers: no need-based aid for international transfer applicants; aid-requesting transfer applications are withdrawn [3].
- **Merit scholarships:** International first-year applicants may apply for all merit scholarships. Signature full-tuition awards are the Ingram Scholars Program, the Cornelius Vanderbilt Scholarship and the Chancellor's Scholarship; others include the Carell, Fred Russell–Grantland Rice and Curb Leadership scholarships. Merit awards go to first-years only. The deadline is December 1, 2026, and the Cornelius Vanderbilt application is required for that award [2][3][6]. Need is generally not a factor in merit awards [6].
- **Targeted awards for internationals:** Hilppa A. K. Roby Scholarship (Finland, College of Arts and Science), Irene and Thomas Harrington International Scholarship (France first, then EU), Early-White International Scholarship (UK or EU, College of Arts and Science) [3].
- **Net price calculator:** Linked from https://admissions.vanderbilt.edu/affordability/#net-price-calculator. It is for US citizens and eligible non-citizens, and showed the 2025–26 year when we checked [6].

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common App, Coalition (powered by Scoir) or QuestBridge. Vanderbilt does not prefer one; use only one [2]. QuestBridge is accepted, but whether international students can use it: Not stated on the pages we read.
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [2]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Aid forms (priority) | Decision | Deposit |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Decision I | Nov 1, 2026 | Nov 1, 2026 | Mid-December 2026 | Dec 31, 2026 |
  | Early Decision II | Jan 1, 2027 | Jan 1, 2027 | Mid-February 2027 | Mar 1, 2027 |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 1, 2027 | Feb 1, 2027 | Late March 2027 | May 1, 2027 |

  Merit scholarship application: Dec 1, 2026. Blair School of Music application: Nov 1 (ED I) or Dec 1 priority (ED II and RD) [2].
- **Fee and fee waivers:** $50, non-refundable, with waivers for eligible students [4].
- **Required parts:** the Common App or Coalition application with the personal essay and short answers; the official high school transcript; the counselor recommendation; two academic teacher recommendations. Peabody applicants who choose an education major answer a short extra question. Blair applicants add an artistic recommendation, music resume, repertoire list and prescreening video [4]. The mid-year report: not mentioned on the page we read.
- **English proficiency:** Not required if English is your first language or language of instruction, or if you score above 26 on ACT English or above 630 on SAT ERW. Otherwise send one of: TOEFL iBT (recommended minimum 5), TOEFL Essentials 10.5, IELTS 7.0, Cambridge C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency 185, LanguageCert 75, Duolingo 130. Scores come from the testing agency or a school official and are not superscored [4].
- **International documents:** Complete the Common App international section. Send all high school classes and grades to date; leaving exams alone do not complete the application. All documents must be in English, translated by your school or a certified translator (not by you) [4].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** More than 65 undergraduate majors [12]. Minors: Not published on the pages checked (vanderbilt.edu, searched 2026-11-23). Every undergraduate completes "Immersion Vanderbilt," an experiential program ending in a faculty-mentored project [12].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 31%, interdisciplinary studies 16%, engineering 12%, computer and information sciences 9%, biological and biomedical sciences 7% [31].
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 8 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2: 7,329 students, 935 faculty) [1]. Class sections with undergraduates (CDS I3): 1,809 in total: 418 with 2–9 students, 621 with 10–19, 346 with 20–29, 227 with 30–39, 39 with 40–49, 117 with 50–99 and 41 with 100+ [1].
- **Fields:** Engineering is offered through the School of Engineering; the College of Connected Computing is a separate school [11]. Pre-health and pre-law advising and 3-2 programs: Not published for undergraduates on the pages checked (vanderbilt.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Opportunities:** Vanderbilt Global Opportunities lists hundreds of study-abroad programs. Research centers include the Wond'ry innovation center and the Data Science Institute [12]. Cross-registration and honors: Not published on the pages checked.
- **STEM majors:** Vanderbilt publishes no list. DHS sets the eligible fields, so check the field-of-study code on your I-20. STEM OPT applications go through the ISSS portal, take 5 days to process, and cost a $300 administrative fee [28].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 97% (CDS 2025–26 B22) [1]. Graduation (CDS B, Fall 2019 cohort, our calculation from the counts): 89.1% in 4 years (1,424 of 1,598); 92.6% within 6 years (1,480 of 1,598) [1]. The CDS table is split by Pell status, not by citizenship [1].
- **After graduation:** Vanderbilt publishes first-destination results for the Class of 2025 in an interactive dashboard (Office of Data and Strategic Analytics). The figures are not in the page text, so we could not record them [14].
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings are $99,080 ten years after entry and $83,121 six years after entry. These cover federal-aid recipients only and leave out international students [13].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 11.2% of degree-seeking undergraduates are nonresidents (CDS B2); College Scorecard says 11.2% [1][13]. Regular Decision admits to the Class of 2030 held 65 citizenships [26]. The aid page cites 54 countries among the 89 aided international students in Fall 2026 [3].
- **Support:** International Student & Scholar Services (ISSS), (615) 322-2753, isss@vanderbilt.edu, advising by appointment. It has pages on how to get an I-20/DS-2019, orientation, visa process and employment [8]. To get an I-20, submit an Initial I-20/DS-2019 Student Request through the ISSS online portal (about 5–7 business days). After arrival, complete SEVIS check-in by uploading your passport, I-20, visa and I-94 [29].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: F-1 students may work up to 20 hours a week in term and full-time in official breaks. Vanderbilt limits hourly student jobs to an average of 19 hours a week [8a].
  - CPT and OPT: ISSS lists CPT, OPT and a 24-month STEM OPT extension. Post-completion OPT is 12 months per degree level, needs a full year of full-time study, and allows up to 90 days of unemployment [8b]. OPT applicants must complete the OPT workshop and file with USCIS within 30 days of the OPT I-20. STEM OPT costs a $300 ISSS fee [28]. Undergraduate CPT rules: Not published on the pages checked.
- **Practicalities:** Break housing: Residences close over winter break and students aren't entitled to stay; exceptions such as international travel constraints are approved by registration [32]. Mentor programs: iLEAD mentors lead groups of 8–15 new international students (page from 2017) [33]. Student organizations: Not published on the pages checked (vanderbilt.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** All first-years live on the Martha Rivers Ingram Commons residential campus, in one of 10 houses. The page does not state a housing guarantee or a number of years required [15]. 100% of first-years and 74.5% of undergraduates live in college housing (CDS F1) [1].
- **Location and climate:** Nashville. NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Nashville International Airport: mean annual temperature 60.8°F (max 71.6, min 50.0) and 50.51 inches of precipitation [16]. Nashville International (BNA) is less than 10 miles from campus (see section 1) [24].
- **Safety:** [2026 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report](https://cdn.vanderbilt.edu/vu-wpfsx/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2026/11/Final-2026-ASFSR-Report.pdf) [17]. For the University campus, on-campus counts for 2023, 2024 and 2025: rape 13, 5, 20; aggravated assault 2, 8, 5; burglary 4, 3, 7; arson 0, 0, 2. Footnotes in the report explain that the 2025 rape count includes seven offenses by two offenders on multiple occasions, and that the 2024 aggravated assaults involved four incidents with multiple victims. Motor vehicle theft counts are large in the report but we could not read them reliably from the text extraction [17].
- **Student body:** Among full-time degree-seeking undergraduates, 3,441 men and 3,864 women (CDS B1) [1]. 84% of first-years and 87% of undergraduates are from out of state, not counting internationals (CDS F1) [1].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Vanderbilt requires eligible students to carry insurance comparable to or better than its Student Injury and Sickness plan (SHIP). Coverage "varies across policies for domestic undergraduate students, international students, and graduate students." International students are enrolled automatically. A waiver needs comparable US-based employer coverage; marketplace, travel and home-country employer plans don't qualify. The spring 2027 waiver deadline is January 4, 2027 [27]. The university-wide premium wasn't confirmed (email SHIP@vanderbilt.edu) [18]. The cost page says the plan is mandatory unless an online waiver is filed by July 15 [6].
- **Services:** The Student Health Center is staffed by Vanderbilt physicians and nurse practitioners; students who pay the student health fee are eligible regardless of insurance [19]. The University Counseling Center gives individual counseling [20]. Disability services: Student Access, through the Commodore Access Portal. Send current documentation on letterhead; reviews take 5–8 business days, accommodations aren't retroactive, and letters must be requested every semester [30].

## Contradictions with our CSV

Our CSV records Vanderbilt as need-aware and full need met (note: "Limited (fall 2026: 89 students...)"). The admissions first-year-process FAQ states the 100% promise for internationals [4]. But the admissions international costs page says the 100% pledge applies to "U.S. citizens and eligible non-citizens living abroad" and states no percentage for international students [3], and the financial aid office's international page also gives no percentage [7]. We have not changed the verdict. The page wording is inconsistent and should be confirmed with Vanderbilt.
