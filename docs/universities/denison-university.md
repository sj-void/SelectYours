# Denison University

Denison is a private liberal arts college in Granville, Ohio, with about 2,460 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Treated like US students for aid.** Denison offers internationals exactly the same need-based aid and merit scholarships as US citizens, and meets 100% of demonstrated need [3].
- **Big aid numbers.** 358 international undergraduates received Denison aid, averaging $55,613, or $19.9 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [1].
- **Merit scholarships from $5,000 to full tuition,** considered automatically when your application is complete [3].
- **Certification of Finances for everyone, and no CSS Profile.** Every international applicant submits Denison's free Certification of Finances, available Oct 1, whether or not they want aid. Denison does not accept the CSS Profile from internationals [3].
- **English minimums (examples):** TOEFL iBT 80+, TOEFL Essentials 8.5+, IELTS 6.5+ or Duolingo. Waivers are decided case by case [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1831 as the Granville Literary and Theological Institution. It was renamed Denison University in the mid-1850s, after a benefactor [6].
- **Campus:** a 900-acre hillside campus, the most-cited figure, including a 350–360-acre Biological Reserve, in Granville, Ohio [7][8].
- **Enrollment:** 2,458 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [1]; Denison says about 2,500 students in some 65 programs [9].
- **Housing:** fully residential. 100% of first-years and 99% of all undergraduates live on campus (CDS 2025–26 F1) [1][7].
- **Nearest airport:** John Glenn Columbus International (CMH), 24 miles away [7].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need; the same need and merit aid as US students [3].
- **Form:** Denison Certification of Finances (free; required of all internationals); no CSS Profile [3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 358 students; average $55,613; total $19,909,430 [1].
- **Merit:** $5,000 to full tuition, combinable with need-based aid [3].

## 7. Academics

- **Student–faculty ratio:** 9 to 1, Fall 2025 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [1].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 21%, communication 10%, biological and biomedical sciences 9%, psychology 8%, visual and performing arts 6% [13].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 78% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [1].
- **First destination:** 99% of graduates are employed or in graduate school, per Denison [7].
- **Off-campus experience:** 75% study abroad or intern off campus [10].

## 9. International student life

- 382 nonresident undergraduates, about 16% (CDS B1–B2; our calculation) [1].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a 900-acre hillside campus in the village of Granville, Ohio, 24 miles from Columbus airport [7][8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Newark Heath Airport, OH, 7.5 km from campus: average temperature 53.2°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 23.7°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 84.5°F; 38.77 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [11].
- **Housing:** fully residential [7].
- **Annual Security Report:** Denison's Annual Campus Safety and Fire Safety Report is available at https://denison.edu/forms/annual-campus-safety-and-fire-safety-report [12].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 plan is estimated at $1,890 (pending Ohio approval). Waivers need proof of other coverage and aren't guaranteed [16].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Accessibility & Disability Services: register and upload recent documentation (preferably within 3–5 years). Behavioral Health and Psychiatry Services runs a 24/7 on-call counseling line [14].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:**
  - the same need and merit aid for internationals as for US students [3];
  - 99% of graduates employed or in graduate school [7];
  - a 350-acre Biological Reserve [8];
  - one of the highest international aid counts on our list (CDS H6: 358) [1].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni include actor Steve Carell '84, six-term US Senator Richard G. Lugar '54 and author James Clear [15].
