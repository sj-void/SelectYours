# Brandeis University

Brandeis is a private research university in Waltham, Massachusetts, near Boston, with about 3,620 undergraduates. It has one of the higher international shares on our list, at about 19% [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, if you apply during admission.** Brandeis meets 100% of demonstrated need for international undergraduates who apply for aid during the admission process. After decisions are released you can't apply [2].
- **Wien International Scholarship.** It is need-based, for exceptionally accomplished internationals, chosen by Admissions. It lasts up to eight semesters and requires living on campus [2].
- **Four-year guarantee.** Under the Brandeis Commitment (fall 2026 entrants onward), internationals are eligible for a four-year grant guarantee, with scholarships rising up to 3% a year, barring major changes in circumstances. Brandeis may change the program for future classes [4].
- **CSS Profile (code 3092),** plus documentation of non-US income and assets [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 3,618 degree-seeking undergraduates; 19.3% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Gateway Scholars Program:** an English-support pathway for some admitted international students [5].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Founded in 1948; a 235-acre campus in Waltham, near Boston [19].
- **Nearest airport:** The nearest airport is Boston Logan, about an hour away by GO Boston Shuttle (reserve ahead), the T or ride-hailing [15].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for internationals who apply during admission [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 3092) and non-US income and asset documents [2].
- **Wien Scholarship:** Need-based, chosen by Admissions, eight semesters, residence required [2].
- **Guarantee:** Four-year grant guarantee for fall 2026+ entrants [4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 146 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $57,906, total $84,54,245 [18].

## 7. Academics

- **English support:** the Gateway Scholars Program [5].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 19%, biological and biomedical sciences 19%, psychology 8%, public administration and social services 8%, computer and information sciences 7% [12].
- **Majors:** 47 majors and 50 minors [22].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 333 of 562 class sections (59%) had fewer than 20 students [18]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 87.0%; 6-year graduation rate 81.0% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 19% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** the International Students and Scholars Office (ISSO) [7].
- **Orientation:**
  - Required online modules before arrival, then an in-person session after arrival [7].
  - Undergraduates: international move-in 20 August 2026, then International Student Welcome Days on 21–22 August, required for F-1 and J-1 students [8].
- **OPT, CPT and on-campus work rules:** Brandeis's ISSO publishes its on-campus employment rules for F-1 students at https://www.brandeis.edu/isso/employment/on-campus.html; CPT and OPT details weren't summarized in the pages checked [21].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Waltham, Massachusetts, near Boston [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Natick, MA, 11.5 km from campus: average temperature 48.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 19.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 79.6°F; 47.26 inches of precipitation a year, including 45.7 inches of snowfall [11].
- **Housing guarantee:** Brandeis guarantees four consecutive semesters of on-campus housing (except for midyear entrants) [20].
- **Annual Security Report:** Brandeis posts its combined security and fire safety report as a PDF on its Public Safety "Reports and Statistics" page, https://www.brandeis.edu/publicsafety/safety/statistics.html [17].
- **Housing:** Halls close for winter break (noon Dec 22, 2025 to Jan 11, 2026). Winter housing is available only to students keeping the same room for fall and spring [14].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** an estimated premium of $4,700 (2026–27) [6]; waiver rules weren't stated.
- **Counseling and disability services:** Brandeis has a Counseling Center and a Health Center; disability accommodations (including housing) go through Student Accessibility Support, with supporting letters from providers [13].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the Wien International Scholarship [2]; a four-year grant guarantee [4]; a large international community of about 19% [1]; the Gateway Scholars Program [5].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni include David Crane '79 and Marta Kauffman '78, co-creators of "Friends", and Fields Medal winner Edward Witten '71 [16].
