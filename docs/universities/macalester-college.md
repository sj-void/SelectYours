# Macalester College

Macalester is a private liberal arts college in Saint Paul, Minnesota, with about 2,130 undergraduates. It is known for a large international community, at about 15% [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Limited budget, stiffer for the highest need.** The aid budget "is not unlimited". Applicants with the greatest need face stiffer competition, and Early Decision has given "a modest advantage" in recent years [2].
- **Loan included.** Aid-eligible internationals receive the Macalester College International Student Loan, at a 6.0% fixed rate. Macalester can't swap it for extra scholarship [4][5].
- **Campus work** up to $4,500 a year before taxes for 2026–27 [4].
- **Automatic merit consideration.** Every international applicant is considered for the Turck Presidential Honor Scholarship, worth up to $40,000 a year [4].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (no fee waivers for internationals) or the free ISAFA, plus Macalester's Financial Declaration Form [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 2,131 degree-seeking undergraduates; 14.7% international (IPEDS) [1]. Macalester aims for about 550 first-years a year; 26% of applicants were admitted to the last class [8].
- **Campus:** 53 acres in a historic residential neighborhood of Saint Paul, plus the 280-acre Ordway Field Station on the Mississippi [9].
- **Founding year:** Founded in 1874; Macalester opened in 1885 with five professors and six first-year students [26].
- **Nearest airport:** From Minneapolis–St. Paul International (MSP), take the METRO Blue Line to 46th Street, then the A Line or #84 bus to Snelling and Grand (35–45 minutes), or a taxi (about 15 minutes, $20–35) [20].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Limited budget; four-year package (our list) [2][3].
- **Package:** Grants, the international student loan (6.0% fixed) and campus work (max $4,500 in 2026–27) [4][5].
- **Merit:** Turck Presidential Honor Scholarship, up to $40,000 a year, with automatic consideration [4].
- **Forms and deadlines:** See the key points and quick facts [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 287 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $59,839, total $1,71,73,928 [24].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** majors in 39 areas, 40 minors and 11 concentrations [9].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 21%, biological and biomedical sciences 12%, interdisciplinary studies 12%, psychology 10%, physical sciences 9% [17].
- **Most popular majors (2023 graduates):** computer science, economics, political science, psychology and biology [9].
- **Teaching:** average class size 17; student–faculty ratio 10:1 (2023–24) [9].
- **PhDs:** 20th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (11.6%) [7].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 92.3%; 6-year graduation rate 90.8% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 15% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** International Student Programs (ISP) [11].
- **Orientation:** Pre-Orientation for International Students (PO4IS) is mandatory for all new F-1 and J-1 students and comes before general orientation (2025: 19–25 August) [11].
- **Work:**
  - No work or training of any kind before your I-20 is issued [11].
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week during the year, and up to 40 in summer if employed by the college [12].
  - CPT: must earn credit and relate to your major; the position comes first; up to 12 months full time [12].
  - OPT: file from 90 days before to 60 days after completion, no job offer needed, and finish within 14 months of completion. STEM graduates can work up to 36 months in total [13].
- **Breaks:** ISP publishes summer and winter break guidance for international students [14].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a historic residential neighborhood in Saint Paul, Minnesota [9].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at U Of Mn St Paul, MN, 5.8 km from campus: average temperature 44.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 9.1°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 79.2°F; 32.73 inches of precipitation a year, including 40.4 inches of snowfall [16].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2026 Annual Campus Security and Fire Safety Report (crime statistics for 2023–2025) is at https://www.macalester.edu/public-safety/wp-content/uploads/sites/427/2026/10/2026-Macalester-Annual-Security-Report.pdf [22].
- **Housing:** Students must live on campus for their first three years; seniors may move off. International students can be pre-approved to stay over winter break and J-Term [19].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 premium is $3,329. International students must enroll in the UnitedHealthcare Student Resources plan and are enrolled automatically; the waiver window ran June 3 to August 14, 2026 [25].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Macalester's Student Affairs "Services and Support" page lists its health, counseling and disability services [18].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a strong international focus, with about 15% international [1]; city location in the Twin Cities [9]; the Ordway Field Station [9]; four-year aid packages for internationals [4].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni include US Vice President Walter Mondale '50, UN Secretary-General Kofi Annan '61, and writers Tim O'Brien '68 and Mary Karr '76 [21].
