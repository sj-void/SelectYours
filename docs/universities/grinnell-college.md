# Grinnell College

Grinnell is a private liberal arts college in Grinnell, Iowa, with about 1,730 undergraduates. It has one of the highest international shares on our list, at about 21% [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No current Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware.** Grinnell considers demonstrated financial need alongside the rest of your application [2].
- **Your file isn't complete without an aid form.** Aid seekers must file either the CSS Profile (code 6252; no fee waivers) or Grinnell's free International Student Application for Financial Assistance (ISAFA), completed in the applicant portal [2].
- **Aid offers are final; no appeals** (our list) [3]. Grinnell won't consider aid requests from internationals once they have started studying [4].
- **Campus job built in.** New students can earn about $3,000 a year working under eight hours a week. F-1 rules bar off-campus work [4].
- **Merit scholarships:** There is no priority deadline for internationals [4].
- **Large international community,** about 21% of undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. Grinnell runs an International Pre-Orientation Program [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 1,729 degree-seeking undergraduates; about 21% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Aid reach:** 92% of students receive aid, and 64% get need-based aid. The average need-based package is $70,871 [7].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Founded in 1846. Campus size wasn't published on the pages checked [22].
- **Nearest airport:** Des Moines and Cedar Rapids airports are each about an hour away; the college runs no airport shuttles [17].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware [2].
- **Forms:** 2027–28 CSS Profile (opens Oct 1) or the free ISAFA, due by your Common App deadline [2].
- **Package:** Includes campus employment of about $3,000 a year [4].
- **Rules:** No appeals (our list) [3]; no new aid requests after you start studying [4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2024–2025 H6):** 243 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $56,853, total $1,38,15,175 [21].

## 7. Academics

- **PhDs:** 9th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (15.0%) [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 29%, biological and biomedical sciences 17%, computer and information sciences 13%, psychology 9%, physical sciences 7% [14].
- **Internship credit:** INT-300 offers credit for internships. Enrolling doesn't automatically give you CPT [10].
- **Majors, curriculum style and class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (grinnell.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 92.6%; 6-year graduation rate 86.5% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 21% international (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** the Office of International Student Affairs (OISA), the first stop for visa matters. Its dean and staff handle SEVIS compliance [11].
- **Orientation:** the International Pre-Orientation Program (IPOP), required for new internationals, focused on immigration rules and cultural transition [5][11].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week in term, with no limit in breaks [4].
  - CPT: for courses with experiential parts or US internships, requested through the GrinnImmigration portal before you start [4][10].
  - OPT: 12 months, during studies or in the year after graduation [4].
- **Careers:** Career, Life and Service runs an international-student channel and the Interstride platform [12].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Grinnell, Iowa [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Grinnell 3 Sw, IA, 3.8 km from campus: average temperature 47.5°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 13.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.3°F; 38.19 inches of precipitation a year, including 31.0 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Housing guarantee:** All full-time students must live in college residences and eat in college dining for all four years [23].
- **Annual Security Report:** The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report (2026 edition, crime statistics for 2023–2025) is linked from https://www.grinnell.edu/about/leadership/offices-services/student-affairs/safety/annual-security-fire-safety [19].
- **Housing:** Winter break housing is open to international students, students with an international address, and others approved for significant need; approved students stay in their own rooms [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** All students must have ACA-compliant coverage. You'll enroll in the student plan through billing ($2,769 in 2026–27) or request a waiver [4][9].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling Services is part of Student Health. Students who need accommodations for more than a week are referred to Disability Resources and don't have to see a counselor first [15].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** one of the highest international shares on our list (about 21%) [1]; no-loan aid [7]; 9th nationally for PhDs per graduate [6].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni include Robert Noyce '49, co-inventor of the integrated circuit and co-founder of Intel; Academy Award-winning actor Gary Cooper '26; and musician Herbie Hancock [18].
