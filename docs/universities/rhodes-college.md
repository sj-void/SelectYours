# Rhodes College

Rhodes is a private liberal arts college in Memphis, Tennessee, with about 1,840 undergraduates. It was added to our list as a full-need school that LeBeau's list missed [1][4].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need met, but "extremely competitive" for big aid.** Rhodes meets the demonstrated need of admitted international F-1 students, through a mix of merit scholarships, institutional aid and work-study. Admission is extremely competitive for internationals seeking significant aid [2].
- **Need is measured against billed costs only.** Aid doesn't cover transportation, textbooks, SEVIS fees, break housing or personal expenses [2][4].
- **No CSS Profile.** All internationals submit the Rhodes Non-Citizen Financial Supplement with a bank statement. It also serves as the Certification of Finances for the visa [2].
- **Merit scholarships for internationals:** $20,000 a year up to full tuition, according to the international aid page. The 2026–27 catalog says $20,000–$40,000 [2][3].
- **Sample packages** published by Rhodes leave families paying roughly $23,830–$35,830 a year after work-study, which can earn up to $2,500 a year [2].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1848 in Clarksville, Tennessee. It moved to Memphis in 1925 and was renamed Rhodes College in 1984, after Peyton Nalle Rhodes [6].
- **Campus:** a 123-acre wooded Collegiate Gothic campus in historic Midtown Memphis [7].
- **Enrollment:** 1,836 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. A Rhodes history page cites more than 2,000 students from 45 states and 63 countries [8].
- **Arrival:** Rhodes offers airport pickup in your first semester [9].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Meets demonstrated need for admitted F-1 internationals; need-aware [2][4].
- **Forms:** Rhodes Non-Citizen Financial Supplement plus a bank statement; no CSS Profile [2].
- **Package:** Merit scholarships, institutional aid and work-study [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2024–2025 H6):** 89 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $44,787, total $39,86,028 [19].

## 7. Academics

- **Recognition:** Rhodes has a Phi Beta Kappa chapter, one of 290 US colleges with one [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 21%, biological and biomedical sciences 19%, business and marketing 11%, physical sciences 9%, computer and information sciences 7% [14].
- **STEM OPT:** ISS helps with STEM OPT for up to three years after graduation [9].
- **Majors:** More than 70 majors, minors and interdisciplinary programs [18].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2024–25 I3):** 391 of 491 class sections (80%) had fewer than 20 students [19]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 92.3%; 6-year graduation rate 81.2% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].
- **International graduates:** 28 recent graduates were listed as on OPT or STEM OPT [9]. Rhodes publishes an outcomes brochure for international graduates [10].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student Services, in the Buckman Center for Global Engagement [9].
- **Arrival and orientation:** airport pickup in your first semester, a multi-day international student orientation, and a dedicated student associate with peer advisers [9].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: ISS publishes on-campus employment guidance [11].
  - Off campus: needs prior authorization (CPT or OPT). OPT work must relate to your field [9].
- **Travel:** On OPT, you need your EAD card and an I-20 travel signature from the last six months [12].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a 123-acre wooded campus in Midtown Memphis, Tennessee [7].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Memphis International Airport, TN, 10.9 km from campus: average temperature 63.4°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 35.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 90.9°F; 54.94 inches of precipitation a year, including 2.7 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Annual Security Report:** Rhodes links its 2026 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report from https://www.rhodes.edu/campus-life/services-support/campus-safety/annual-security-fire-safety-report-clery-report [17].
- **Housing:** Students under 24 at admission must live in college housing for six consecutive full semesters. Residence Life doesn't guarantee break housing, and international students are told to plan for halls being closed [15].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- International health insurance costs $1,600 (2026–27) [2].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a Phi Beta Kappa liberal arts college in a major city [7]; airport pickup and peer advisers for internationals [9]; merit scholarships from $20,000 a year to full tuition, open to internationals [2].
- **Notable alumni:** Cary Fowler '71 was named a 2024 World Food Prize Laureate [16].
