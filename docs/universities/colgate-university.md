# Colgate University

Colgate is a private liberal arts university in Hamilton, New York, with about 3,180 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Limited aid, full need.** Colgate has "limited financial assistance" for non-US citizens and reserves a limited number of Colgate Grants for internationals each year [2][5]. Our list records that it meets 100% of every aided international student's need with a Colgate Grant and work-study [3].
- **CSS Profile is the only aid form,** due by the application deadline. Internationals can request a CSS fee waiver in the applicant portal [2].
- **Entry year only.** Internationals who don't receive a Colgate Grant when they enter can't get aid later [5].
- **English exam recommended** if English isn't your first language [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Origins:** In 1817, 13 men in Hamilton, New York, founded the Baptist Education Society of the State of New York, from which Colgate grew [6].
- **Campus:** 575 acres with 88 buildings [7]; some catalog pages say nearly 600 acres [8].
- **Enrollment:** 3,180 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]; about 7% international [7].
- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked (colgate.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Not need-blind; limited funds. Full need met for aided internationals (our list) [2][3].
- **Form:** CSS Profile only; fee waiver available on request [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 1; ED II and RD Jan 22 (the admission table shows Jan 15, so confirm) [2].
- **Package:** Colgate Grant plus work-study (our list) [3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 119 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $86,649, total $1,03,11,209 [17].

## 6. How to apply

- **Aid:** CSS Profile by the application deadline; fee waiver on request [2].
- **Testing:** optional through the 2026–27 cycle [4]. **English:** a standardized English proficiency exam is recommended for non-native speakers [5].
- **See also:** Colgate's international applicants page [2].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 56 majors (2024–25 catalog) [8].
- **Curriculum:** the Liberal Arts Core Curriculum, plus a major, electives and physical education. Students must show competence in a foreign or classical language and in English composition [8].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 8:1; average class size 17 [7].
- **Off-campus study:** run by the Center for International Programs [9].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 34%, biological and biomedical sciences 15%, computer and information sciences 9%, psychology 8%, natural resources 6% [14].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 93.4%; 6-year graduation rate 88.8% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Office of International Student Services, 101 McGregory Hall, in the Center for International Programs. It offers F-1 advising, SEVIS administration, orientation and cultural-adjustment support, plus an international kitchen and a textbook library [9].
- **Orientation:** For the Class of 2030, arrival was Tuesday 18 August 2026, with orientation 19–23 August and a mandatory SEVIS check-in [10].
- **Breaks:** The office coordinates meals and activities during breaks [9].
- **OPT, CPT and on-campus work rules:** Colgate publishes its F-1 status rules and step-by-step OPT instructions (https://www.colgate.edu/media/11766/download); the on-campus hour limit and CPT rules weren't summarized in the pages checked [19].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Hamilton, New York, on a 575-acre campus [7].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Morrisville 6 Sw, NY, 15.7 km from campus: average temperature 44.3°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 14.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 75.3°F; 48.14 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [13].
- **Housing guarantee:** Colgate requires and guarantees on-campus housing for all four years for students in good standing, but not beyond eight semesters [18].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security & Fire Safety Report (crime statistics for 2022–2024) is at https://www.colgate.edu/sites/default/files/2025-10/DOC_2025_038_Clery_Report_ASFSR_F_508.pdf [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** about $1,805 for 2026–27, a figure from the exchange-student page [11]; the degree-student rate wasn't stated.
- **Counseling and disability services:** Accessibility Resources reviews documentation to set accommodations. The counseling service offers free, confidential counseling [15].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the Liberal Arts Core Curriculum [8]; small classes (average 17) [7]; full need met for aided internationals [3].
- **Alumni:** more than 34,000 living alumni, per a 2018 article [12]. Notable alumni: Colgate's alumni include New Yorker cartoonist Charles Addams '33, 60 Minutes commentator Andy Rooney and Oscar-nominated filmmaker Joe Berlinger [20].
