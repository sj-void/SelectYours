# Carleton College

Carleton is a private liberal arts college in Northfield, Minnesota, with about 2,090 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Apply Regular Decision if you need aid.** Most international aid is awarded in the RD round, so Carleton recommends applying RD [2].
- **No loans from 2027–28.** All students on need-based aid, internationals included, will have 100% of demonstrated need met through grants, scholarships and student employment [3].
- **Certification of Finances for everyone,** even applicants not seeking aid. It appears on your applicant status page after you apply [2].
- **Free aid form option.** File the free International Student Application for Financial Assistance, or the 2027–28 CSS Profile. Carleton gives no CSS fee waiver because the free form is accepted instead [2].
- **No second chances.** Carleton won't consider new or revised aid applications after an admission offer is made [2].
- **Proof of English proficiency is required** [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 12 October 1866 in Northfield, Minnesota, per Carleton's history page; the catalogs give 14 November 1866, as Northfield College [6][7].
- **Enrollment:** 2,044 students from 49 states and 53 countries [8]; 2,086 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].
- **Calendar:** trimesters, three 10-week terms [8].
- **Campus:** 1,040 acres of campus, arboretum and athletic fields, including an 880-acre arboretum [7][8].
- **Location:** 45 minutes south of Minneapolis and St Paul [8]. The airport was not named on the pages checked.

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Limited international funds. Full need with no loans from 2027–28 [2][3].
- **Forms:** Certification of Finances (everyone), plus the free international aid application or the CSS Profile [2].
- **Timing:** Aid is set at admission; most international aid goes to RD admits [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 63 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $81,596, total $5,140,528 [20].

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

- **Estimated total:** $104,746, covering tuition, housing, food, fees, personal and travel. Expect a 4–5% rise each year [3].

## 6. How to apply

- Deadlines: Fall ED Nov 15; Winter ED and RD Jan 15 [3]. International steps: [2][4].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 33 majors and 39 minors [8].
- **Teaching:** average class size 12 [8].
- **PhDs:** 6th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (17.1%) [5].
- **STEM OPT:** STEM majors can apply for a two-year extension [10].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** computer and information sciences 18%, physical sciences 16%, social sciences 15%, biological and biomedical sciences 11%, mathematics and statistics 8% [14].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 96.5%; 6-year graduation rate 88.6% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** students from 53 countries [8].
- **Office:** International Student Life (ISL), which advises on and processes CPT and OPT [9].
- **Orientation:** required for all students on US-issued visas. In 2026 it began 3 September, before New Student Week on 8 September [11].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: no CPT needed, in term or breaks [9].
  - CPT: needs department approval and course registration. Work starts only after a new I-20; 12+ months of full-time CPT cancels OPT eligibility [9].
  - OPT: after a full academic year; no job offer needed. More than 90 days of unemployment ends your status [10].
  - Unpaid and volunteer work: ISL publishes separate guidance [12].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Northfield, Minnesota, with the 880-acre Cowling Arboretum [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Faribault, MN, 19.2 km from campus: average temperature 44.5°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 8.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 80.1°F; 35.45 inches of precipitation a year, including 43.3 inches of snowfall [13].
- **Housing guarantee and Annual Security Report:** Not published on the pages checked (carleton.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Housing:** Winter break housing costs about $14 a night, and international students qualify under the extenuating-circumstance criteria; dining halls close over the break [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 plan costs $2,355 a year. International students must enroll in Carleton's plan; the waiver deadline is August 15, 2026 [18].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Carleton's Student Mental Health resources and new-student Health and Support page list counseling and accommodation support; disability-related flare-ups can be accommodated for attendance and assignments [15].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** small classes (average 12) [8]; the arboretum [8]; 6th nationally for PhDs per graduate [5]; no loans in need-based aid from 2027–28 [3].
- **Notable alumni:** Carleton's 2022 alumni awards honored transplant surgeon Julie Heimbach and the founders of the advocacy group Indivisible [19].
