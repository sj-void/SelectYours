# Mount Holyoke College

Mount Holyoke is a private liberal arts college for women in South Hadley, Massachusetts, with about 2,170 undergraduates. It is part of the Five College Consortium [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Loans are part of the package.** A typical offer combines a need-based grant, a fixed-interest Mount Holyoke loan and campus job earnings. The loan grows by about $1,000 a year, to roughly $20,000–$28,000 over four years [2][5].
- **Merit scholarships exist for internationals.** About 45% of incoming internationals receive need-based aid (average $62,351) or merit scholarships (average $17,500) [2].
- **Tick "aid" on the admission application,** and finish all aid steps by the deadlines. Otherwise you can't get need-based aid later, even if circumstances change [2].
- **Fixed contribution.** The family contribution calculated at admission stays the same every year until graduation [2].
- **You pay** health insurance, visa and travel costs, on top of the family contribution [2].
- **CSS Profile (code 3529),** with a translated parental tax return, or an employer letter if no return was filed [2].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Package:** Grant, a fixed-interest college loan (about $20k–$28k over four years) and campus work [2][5].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 3529) plus a translated tax return or employer letter [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II Jan 5; RD Jan 15 (older catalog; confirm) [4].
- **Work:** Internationals generally can't work off campus, because of visa limits [2].
- CDS H6 counts: Not published on the pages checked (mtholyoke.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 6. How to apply

- **Testing:** SAT/ACT optional; Mount Holyoke has been test-optional since fall 2001 [10].
- **English:** TOEFL or IELTS is required only if English is neither your first language nor your school's language of instruction. The catalog doesn't mention Duolingo [10].
- **Aid:** CSS Profile (code 3529) by the deadlines [2][4].

## 7. Academics

- **Five College Consortium:** exchange and shared buses with Amherst, Hampshire, Smith and UMass Amherst [11].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 19%, biological and biomedical sciences 16%, visual and performing arts 11%, English 8%, computer and information sciences 7% [17].
- **Lynk:** every student can get funding for an internship, faculty-mentored research or a creative project [12].
- **CPT:** Courses with "Practicum" in the title may qualify F-1 students for CPT [13].
- **Majors:** 48 majors, many of which also offer minors [23].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (mtholyoke.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 88.6%; 6-year graduation rate 82.3% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 19.5% of undergraduates are international, one of the highest shares on our list (IPEDS) [1].
- **Office:** the McCulloch Center for Global Initiatives. It advises on immigration, OPT, STEM OPT, CPT, Social Security cards, Massachusetts IDs and taxes, and sends regulation updates by email [13].
- **I-20:** Send the Visa Document Request Form by 31 May (fall entry), with the Declaration of Finances and admission deposit, before your I-20 is mailed [14].
- **Orientation:** pre-orientation is coordinated by the Center. The International Student Organizing Committee runs orientation each semester [13][15].
- **Breaks:** Students whose families live abroad may stay on campus between the fall semester and intersession. Intersession housing and meals need an advance application, and being international counts as a qualifying reason [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** South Hadley, Massachusetts [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Amherst, MA, 14.9 km from campus: average temperature 48.2°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 16.7°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 80.9°F; 46.59 inches of precipitation a year, including 36.5 inches of snowfall [16].
- **Break housing:** see section 9 [13].
- **Housing guarantee:** All undergraduates must live on campus for their whole enrollment (local students may commute); housing is guaranteed for incoming first-years [22].
- **Annual Security Report:** Not published on the pages checked (mtholyoke.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Housing:** December break housing is open to students whose permanent home address is abroad and to students with hardship getting home [18].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- Internationals pay the student health insurance premium [2].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** one of the largest international shares on our list [1]; Lynk-funded internships and research for every student [12]; Five College cross-registration [11]; merit aid open to internationals [2].
- **Notable alumnae:** Alumnae include Emily Dickinson, Frances Perkins and Wendy Wasserstein, and Suchi Saria '04, named to TIME's 100 Most Influential People in AI 2026 [20].
