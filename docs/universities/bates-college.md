# Bates College

Bates is a private liberal arts college in Lewiston, Maine, with about 1,760 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **No loans for internationals.** Awards are a grant plus on-campus work. Bates does not include loans in aid for non-US citizens. Aid can run for up to eight semesters [2].
- **Full need, regardless of citizenship,** for admitted students who qualify [2].
- **Need-based only;** no merit scholarships [2].
- **Declare aid before your admission decision.** Bates accepts no appeals from internationals who try to apply for aid after admission, "without exception" [2].
- **Bates's own international aid form** (BISAFA), not the CSS Profile (our list) [3].
- **Health plan required** for international students [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1855, as New England's first co-educational college [6].
- **Campus:** a 133-acre main campus in Lewiston, Maine, 35 miles north of Portland. Bates also has the 574-acre Bates-Morse Mountain Conservation Area on the coast [6].
- **Calendar:** 4-4-1: two semesters plus a 3½-week Short Term in spring [6].
- **Enrollment:** about 2,000 degree-seeking students [6]; 1,760 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].
- **Nearest airport:** Not stated on the pages checked. Portland is 35 miles away [6].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-based only; full demonstrated need met regardless of citizenship [2].
- **Package:** Grant plus campus work; no loans for non-US citizens [2].
- **Form:** BISAFA, Bates's international aid form (our list) [3].
- **Rule:** Aid intent must be stated before admission, with no exceptions [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 106 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $84,952, total $90,00,952 [14].

## 6. How to apply

- **Aid:** Declare that you're seeking aid before your admission decision, and file the BISAFA [2][3].
- **Testing and English proficiency:** An English test is required unless English is your native language or was the main language of instruction for four years of secondary school [13]. See Bates's international applicants page [5].
- **Deadlines:** Not published on the pages checked (bates.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 36 majors, 23 departmental and 11 interdisciplinary [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 26%, biological and biomedical sciences 22%, psychology 11%, natural resources 8%, physical sciences 7% [11].
- **Most popular majors (2023–24 graduates):** Economics (43), Politics (42) and Psychology (21) [7].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 9:1 [6].
- **Short Term:** an intensive 3½-week spring term [6].
- **STEM OPT:** The Center for Global Education advises on STEM OPT extensions [8].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 91.3%; 6-year graduation rate 88.1% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Center for Global Education, formed in 2017. It covers visas, I-20s, SEVIS and employment support, including CPT, OPT and STEM OPT [8].
- **Orientation:** International Circle Pre-Orientation is expected of all international students and is free. It covers immigration rules, US healthcare, campus life and on-campus jobs [9].
- **Work:** Aided internationals get on-campus employment of up to 20 hours a week during the academic year [2].
- **Breaks:** Bates has a "Vacation Periods" page for international students; its details weren't on the pages checked [8].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Lewiston, Maine, 35 miles north of Portland, with a coastal conservation area [6].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Lewiston, ME, 1.2 km from campus: average temperature 46.3°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 15.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 77.7°F; 45.18 inches of precipitation a year, including 65.1 inches of snowfall [10].
- **Housing guarantee and Annual Security Report:** Not published on the pages checked (bates.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Housing:** International students may stay on campus over winter break at no housing charge; which residences stay open depends on occupancy [12].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- The college health plan is mandatory for internationals [4].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** New England's first co-ed college [6]; the Short Term [6]; loan-free aid for internationals [2]; the Morse Mountain conservation area [6].
- **Notable alumni:** Civil-rights leader Benjamin Mays (Class of 1920) later mentored Martin Luther King Jr [15].
