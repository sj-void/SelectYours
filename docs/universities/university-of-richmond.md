# University of Richmond

The University of Richmond is a private university in Richmond, Virginia, with about 2,980 undergraduates. It has arts and sciences, business, leadership, law and continuing-studies schools [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, then full need.** Admission is "very competitive" for international aid applicants, but if you're admitted Richmond meets 100% of your demonstrated need [2].
- **Merit scholarships open to internationals.** Apply by Dec 1, the Richmond Scholars deadline, to be fully considered [2].
- **Certification of Financial Responsibility for everyone.** All international applicants submit it, stating how much support is available toward the four-year cost. Aid applicants also file the CSS Profile (code 5569) [2].
- **Late filing costs money.** Aid applications up to two months late get 10% less need-based grant [2].
- **Transfers:** International transfer aid is "extremely limited", and transfers get no merit scholarships [2].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware; full demonstrated need met for admitted students; grants (our list) [2][4].
- **Forms:** Certification of Financial Responsibility (all internationals) and CSS Profile (code 5569) [2].
- **Deadlines:** EA and ED I Nov 15, 2026; ED II and RD Feb 1, 2027 [2].
- **Average international award:** $69,900 in 2025–26 [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 187 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $67,911, total $1,26,99,322 [16].

## 7. Academics

- **Teaching:** average undergraduate class size 16 [5].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** business and marketing 40%, interdisciplinary studies 10%, biological and biomedical sciences 9%, social sciences 9%, psychology 5% [12].
- **Schools:** undergraduate programs in arts and sciences, business (Robins) and leadership studies (Jepson) [6].
- **Richmond Guarantee:** support for internships and research. Career Services has an international-student channel for US work and internships [8].
- **Majors:** More than 80 majors, minors and concentrations combined [15].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 90.8%; 6-year graduation rate 86.5% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

- **Getting involved:** The Center lists international clubs and programs [10].
- **Orientation details, OPT/CPT rules and break housing:** Not published on current pages checked (richmond.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a suburban campus of 350–378 acres with a 13-acre lake, in Richmond, Virginia [5][6].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Ashland Hanover Co Municipal Airport, VA, 17.2 km from campus: average temperature 58.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 29.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 86.8°F; 45.81 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [11].
- **Housing guarantee:** Not published on the pages checked (richmond.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Annual Security Report:** The University of Richmond Police Department explains its Clery reporting and annual report at https://police.richmond.edu/clery/clery-faq.html [17].
- **Housing:** International students with fall and spring housing are automatically allowed to stay over breaks if they submit a request; breaks are free with advance notice [14].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** all international students must buy the university's health plan [9].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) provides counseling, and its clinicians can document the need for accommodations. Disability Services approves accommodations and issues an accommodation notice; instructors have up to 7 days to put them in place [13].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths
