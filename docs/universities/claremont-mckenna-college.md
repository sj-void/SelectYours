# Claremont McKenna College

Claremont McKenna (CMC) is a private liberal arts college in Claremont, California, focused on economics, government and public affairs. It has about 1,390 undergraduates and is one of the Claremont Colleges [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

The newest Common Data Set linked on CMC's site is 2023–24, so admissions numbers here come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1][2].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Apply for aid at admission; late applications are refused.** Funding is limited, so internationals must request need-based aid when they apply, by the published deadlines [5].
- **CMC's own aid form, not the CSS Profile.** Internationals submit the Financial Aid Application for International Students through the applicant portal. The CSS Profile is not required [5].
- **Family contribution fixed for four years.** Grants rise each year to offset cost increases [5]. Packages may include limited loans and summer savings (our list) [3].
- **Merit scholarships are possible.** Admission decides them, with no separate application. Apply by Dec 1 for priority [5].
- **No aid or merit scholarships for international transfers** [5].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-based aid must be requested at admission; there is limited funding [5].
- **Form:** CMC Financial Aid Application for International Students, submitted in the applicant portal; no CSS Profile [5].
- **Package:** The contribution is fixed for four years and grants rise with costs [5]. Limited loans and summer savings may be included (our list) [3].
- **Merit:** Decided through admission review; priority date Dec 1 [5].
- **Transfers:** No need-based or merit aid for international transfers [5].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 33 majors plus 11 sequences; 11 research institutes and centers [8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 43%, interdisciplinary studies 13%, biological and biomedical sciences 7%, psychology 6%, business and marketing 6% [16].
- **Most common majors (Class of 2024):** Economics, Government, Psychology, International Relations, and Philosophy, Politics & Economics (PPE) [8].
- **Student–faculty ratio:** 8:1 [8].
- **Cross-registration:** across the Claremont Colleges.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 98.1%; 6-year graduation rate 89.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student Services, part of the Dean of Students Office, with an Assistant Dean and an International Student Advisor [9].
- **Arrival:** International students moved in Thursday 20 August 2026, three days before other new students, for pre-orientation. Classes began 31 August [10].
- **Paperwork:** CPT, OPT, travel signatures, visa letters, extensions and reduced course loads all go through the International Student Portal [9].
- **OPT:** No job offer is needed to apply. You can apply from 90 days before to 60 days after your program end date, and you start work only once you have your EAD card [11].
- **Address changes:** Report them to the advisor within 10 days [11].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** Housing is guaranteed only if you submit the housing application on time. About 20% of each building is held for incoming students [12].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Pomona/Fairplex, CA, 5.5 km from campus: average temperature 65.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 43.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 89.0°F; 14.47 inches of precipitation a year, including 0.0 inches of snowfall [14].
- **Hall dates:** published each year by Residential Life [13].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2025 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report (calendar 2024 data) is at https://www.cmc.edu/sites/default/files/2025_asr_final.pdf. CMC publishes it by October 1 each year [15].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Shared Claremont services:** Monsour Counseling and Psychological Services, Student Health Services and telemedicine [12].
- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 Student Health Insurance Plan costs $3,312 a year (August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027; rates pending state approval). International students are enrolled; those with coverage from a US-based insurer that pays claims in the US can ask the Dean of Students Office for a waiver [18].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** economics, government and PPE focus; 11 research institutes [8]; merit scholarships open to internationals [5]; a fixed four-year family contribution [5].
- **Notable alumni:** Not published on the official pages checked (cmc.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
