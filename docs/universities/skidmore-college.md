# Skidmore College

Skidmore is a private liberal arts college in Saratoga Springs, New York, with about 2,690 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Few awards, but need is met.** Skidmore offers only a very limited number of aid awards to non-US citizens, yet commits to meeting demonstrated need for all admitted students [3].
- **Free form option.** File the CSS Profile, or Skidmore's free International Student Financial Aid Application, which appears in the applicant portal after you apply. Internationals don't need the FAFSA [3][4].
- **Year one or never.** Internationals who enroll without aid can't apply for it in later years [3].
- **Tests optional** [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1903 by Lucy Skidmore Scribner, as the Young Women's Industrial Club [5].
- **Enrollment:** 2,719 undergraduates (2,691 full time; CDS 2025–26 B1) [6]; Skidmore says about 2,700 [5]. The Class of 2030 enrolled 711 first-years [2].
- **International share:** 298 nonresident undergraduates, 11.0% (CDS B2) [6].
- **Location:** Saratoga Springs, New York [5].
- **Campus size:** More than 750 acres of wooded land. Skidmore began in 1903 and became a four-year college in 1922 [22].
- **Nearest airport:** Albany International is the nearest airport, about 30 minutes by taxi or rideshare ($65–85 plus tip) [19].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Very limited awards; need met for admitted students [3].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile or Skidmore's free international form [3].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 8; ED II and RD Jan 15 [4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 97 international undergraduates; average $81,400; total about $7.9 million [6].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** more than 40 majors and minors [5].
- **Teaching:** average class section 15.4 (median 15); student–faculty ratio 7.8 to 1 (CDS 2025–26) [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 16%, business and marketing 15%, visual and performing arts 13%, psychology 9%, biological and biomedical sciences 9% [17].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 89.7%; 6-year graduation rate 81.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 11% of undergraduates are nonresidents (CDS B2) [6]. There is an International Student Union [9].
- **Office:** International Student Services in the Learning Commons, which helps with visas, English skills and academic success [10].
- **Orientation:** required for new F-1 students; 31 August to 2 September 2026 [11].
- **I-20:** proof of funds for at least the first year is required [12].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: open to all students, F-1 included [12].
  - CPT: must relate directly to your major and carry credit [12].
  - OPT: up to one year, plus a two-year STEM extension. You need a Social Security number for any US job [12].
- **Breaks:** Internationals can request on-campus housing during holidays and breaks [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** guaranteed on campus for all four years [13].
- **Setting:** Saratoga Springs, New York [5].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Saratoga Springs 4 Sw, NY, 6.9 km from campus: average temperature 48.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 17.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 81.9°F; 46.38 inches of precipitation a year, including 63.5 inches of snowfall [15].
- **Annual Security Report:** The Campus Safety and Security Report published in fall 2026, with crime statistics for 2023–2025 for the main and London campuses, is at https://www.skidmore.edu/campus_safety/clery/asr.pdf [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Skidmore's bursar estimated the annual fee at $4,065 (page may be dated) [21].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Counseling Center offers confidential mental-health services. Accessibility Services (in the Learning Commons) handles accommodations [18].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** four-year housing guarantee with break housing for internationals [13]; small classes (median 15) [6]; test-optional [4].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni stories include Emily Lazar '93, Grammy winner for Best Engineered Album [20].
