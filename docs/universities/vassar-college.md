# Vassar College

Vassar is a private liberal arts college in Poughkeepsie, New York, with about 2,500 undergraduates [2][1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Very few funded seats.** Each year over 850 international students apply for admission with aid, and 35–45 are admitted with funding [3].
- **Real aid numbers.** 57 international undergraduates received Vassar aid, averaging $88,709, or $5.06 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [2].
- **Apply for aid at admission or never.** Internationals who might want aid at any point in four years must apply when they apply for admission. Vassar requires the CSS Profile and gives no CSS fee waiver codes [3].
- **No aid for international transfers** [3].
- **Small loan in packages.** Vassar packages for internationals include a small Vassar loan (our list) [4]. A Class of 2023 example showed a $3,500 loan and up to $3,000 of campus work; that is old data [3].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 2,514 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [2].
- **Campus:** described as a 1,000-acre campus in a 2020–21 Vassar fact sheet [7].
- **Class of 2028:** 665 enrolled from 12,447 applicants (18.6% admitted); about 18% are international citizens, including dual citizens [8].
- **Founding year:** Founded in 1861 by Matthew Vassar as a women's college (first students in 1865); it became coeducational in 1969 [20].
- **Nearest airport:** Stewart International (SWF) in Newburgh is the closest airport. Vassar ran a free JFK shuttle for new international students traveling without family (August 20, 2026) [17].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-based only. Merit, sports and special accomplishments don't affect aid [3].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile; no fee waivers [3].
- **Competition:** 850+ international aid applicants a year; 35–45 admitted with funding [3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 57 students; average $88,709; total $5,056,441 [2].
- **Rules:** Apply at admission to be eligible in any year. No aid for international transfers. No pre-reads or early aid estimates, but two online calculators are available [3].

## 6. How to apply

- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 1, per a 2020–21 fact sheet; confirm on the admission site [7].
- **Testing:** SAT/ACT are optional. Applicants choose whether to submit [9].
- **English proficiency:** An older Vassar catalogue set TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.0 as minimums. The current rule was not confirmed [10].
- **Aid:** CSS Profile; you must apply for aid when you apply for admission [3].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 51 majors, 65 correlates (minors) and 4 dual-degree programs. There are no general education requirements [8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 22%, biological and biomedical sciences 18%, visual and performing arts 10%, psychology 7%, English 7% [15].
- **Teaching:** average class size 19 [8]; student–faculty ratio 7 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [2].
- **Study abroad:** more than 130 programs [8].
- **PhDs:** 24th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (11.1%) [6].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 88% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [2]; first-year retention 94.9% (IPEDS) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 155 nonresident undergraduates (CDS B2) [2]; about 18% of the Class of 2028 are international citizens [8].
- **Office:** The Office of International Services advises on visas, immigration, tax, employment and cultural matters. It runs new-student orientation and guides you through the visa process [11].
- **Work:**
  - On-campus jobs: up to 10 hours a week during the academic year (Vassar policy) [12].
  - CPT: from the summer after your first year, part time (up to 20 hours a week) during term and full time in breaks. It is job-specific, has no fee and takes about a week [12].
  - OPT: up to a year of work related to your major. It takes 3–5 months and has a fee [13].
  - CPT plus all other work can't exceed 20 hours a week during term [12].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Poughkeepsie, New York [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Poughkeepsie, NY, 4.9 km from campus: average temperature 50.5°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 21.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 80.9°F; 47.18 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [14].
- **Annual Security Report:** Vassar posts its 2025 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report (with 2023 and 2024 editions) at https://offices.vassar.edu/campus-safety/statistics/ [19].
- **Housing:** Housing is guaranteed for all full-time students. Intersession housing gives priority to students in need, international students, athletes and intersession workers (both pages may be dated) [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** $3,591 for the full year if not waived (2026–27) [5].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** an open curriculum with no general education requirements [8]; more than 130 study-abroad programs [8]; strong PhD production [6].
- **Notable alumni:** Drama alumni include Jason Blum, founder and CEO of Blumhouse Productions [18].
