# Pomona College

Pomona is a private liberal arts college in Claremont, California, with about 1,670 undergraduates. It is the founding member of the Claremont Colleges consortium [1][2].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

Pomona publishes its CDS 2025–26 only as an interactive Tableau dashboard [3]. Admissions numbers here come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware, full need, about half aided.** Admission is need-aware for internationals. Pomona offers aid to about half of admitted international students each year and meets their full need [4].
- **No loans.** Packages are grants and student work (our list) [6]. There are no merit or athletic scholarships [4].
- **Two aid-form options.** Use the CSS Profile (code 4607). If its fee is a hardship, use the free Pomona College International Financial Aid Application, which opens Oct 15. Submit 2025 tax documents too [5].
- **English test required** if your secondary schooling was not mainly in English: TOEFL or IELTS [4].
- **Claremont consortium.** Pomona students can take classes at the other Claremont Colleges [10].
- **One trip home** is reimbursed for aided internationals during their four years [11].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 1,666 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. The Class of 2030 has 414 first-years, and a record 29% are first-generation [9].
- **International share:** about 12% of students come from outside the US [11].
- **Consortium:** a member of the Claremont Colleges [2].
- **Nearest airports:** LAX and Ontario (ONT). Airport pickup may be offered for move-in-day arrivals [11].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Founded in 1887; about 140 acres, roughly 35 miles east of Los Angeles [19].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware; full need met for admitted internationals [4].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 4607), or the Pomona International Financial Aid Application if the CSS fee is a hardship; plus 2025 tax documents [5].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 15 [5].
- **Renewal:** Pomona's pages conflict. One FAQ says internationals needn't reapply unless circumstances change; another says every student reapplies each year [4][7].
- CDS H6 counts: Not available (Tableau-only CDS) [3].

## 6. How to apply

- **English:** TOEFL or IELTS if your schooling was not primarily in English [4].
- **Aid forms and deadlines:** see section 4 [5].
- **Testing and English proficiency:** If your secondary school didn't teach mainly in English, send TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo results (official or self-reported), with minimums of TOEFL iBT 100, IELTS 7 and Duolingo 130. SAT and ACT are optional, with no advantage to submitting [18].
- **Essays and platforms:** Not published on the pages checked (pomona.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 48 majors. Students explore for two years and declare by the end of sophomore year [10].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 20%, computer and information sciences 17%, biological and biomedical sciences 14%, physical sciences 7%, mathematics and statistics 6% [16].
- **Claremont Colleges:** Students take classes across the consortium. Book funding for consortium courses is capped at $90 a course and $200 a semester for eligible students [10].
- **PhDs:** 10th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (14.9%) [8].
- **STEM OPT:** 24-month extension for STEM graduates [12].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (pomona.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 97.6%; 6-year graduation rate 92.1% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student and Scholar Services (ISSS), in the Montgomery Building, with a dedicated F-1 advisor [11].
- **Arrival:** All F-1 and J-1 students move in 19 August and attend New International Student Orientation. The pre-arrival session was 15 July 2026 [11][13].
- **Mentoring:** the International Student Mentor Program pairs new and returning students [11].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week during term, full time in breaks [12].
  - CPT: needs ISSS authorization before an off-campus internship [12].
  - OPT: before or after graduation, with a USCIS application. Graduates get 12 months, or 36 in total with the STEM extension [12].
- **Breaks:** Residence halls close over winter break, but internationals can arrange housing through residential life [11].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Claremont, California, about an hour's drive from LAX [1][11].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Pomona/Fairplex, CA, 5.1 km from campus: average temperature 65.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 43.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 89.0°F; 14.47 inches of precipitation a year, including 0.0 inches of snowfall [14].
- **Annual Security Report:** Pomona's Clery Reporting page links the 2025 Annual Security and Fire Safety Report, and a paper copy is available on request [15].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Counseling:** Monsour Counseling offers on-campus and off-site services to all students [11].
- **Insurance:** $3,219–$3,312 (2026–27) [10]; waiver rules were not on the pages checked.

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** full need met for admitted internationals without loans [11]; Claremont Colleges cross-registration [10]; 21 NCAA Division III sports with Pitzer [9]; 10th nationally for PhDs per graduate [8].
- **Notable alumni:** The theatre department lists alumni Richard Chamberlain, Kris Kristofferson and Jim Taylor, Oscar-winning screenwriter of Sideways [17].
