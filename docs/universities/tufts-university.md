# Tufts University

Tufts is a private research university in Medford and Somerville, Massachusetts, near Boston, with about 7,060 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, regardless of citizenship** [2].
- **Request aid on your application, or it's gone for good** [2].
- **CSS Profile after admission.** It isn't required at application. Admitted aid applicants submit it within 5–7 days of the decision, with a fee-waiver code on the status page. Tufts gives applicants no CSS fee waivers, but the ISFAA is an optional alternative [2].
- **Tax returns** must be translated into English and converted to US dollars [2].
- **Transfers:** International transfer aid is extremely limited. There is no aid for international citizens in the REAL program. Internationals aren't eligible for the Tuition Pact (our list) [2][3].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for every admitted student, regardless of citizenship [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile after admission (fee waiver given), or the optional ISFAA. Foreign tax returns, translated and converted to US dollars [2].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 16 / Dec 1; ED II Jan 15 / Feb 1; RD Feb 1 / Feb 15 [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 214 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $83,901, total $1,79,54,997 [19].

## 6. How to apply

- **Aid:** Request aid on your application. Admitted aid applicants submit the CSS Profile within 5–7 days, with a fee waiver; the ISFAA is optional [2].
- **Deadlines (aid documents):** ED I Nov 16 / Dec 1; ED II Jan 15 / Feb 1; RD Feb 1 / Feb 15 [2].
- **Testing, English minimums and essays:** Not published on the pages checked (tufts.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **CPT for undergraduates:** part time only during the academic year [7].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 20%, engineering 13%, interdisciplinary studies 12%, computer and information sciences 12%, biological and biomedical sciences 11% [11].
- **Majors:** More than 90 undergraduate majors and minors in Arts and Sciences, and nearly 150 across Arts and Sciences and Engineering [18].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 791 of 1,323 class sections (60%) had fewer than 20 students [19]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 95.5%; 6-year graduation rate 93.6% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Tufts International Center (I-Center) [8].
- **Work:**
  - CPT: needs a CPT Cooperative Agreement with the employer and authorization before work starts, with at least seven business days' notice. Part time (20 hours or less) in term, full time in summer [7].
  - OPT: 12 months per degree level after one full academic year. Apply from 90 days before to 60 days after completion; no more than 90 days of unemployment [9].
  - A full year of full-time CPT cancels OPT eligibility [7].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Medford/Somerville, Massachusetts, near Boston [4].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Boston Logan International Airport, MA, 10.3 km from campus: average temperature 51.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 25.6°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 79.6°F; 43.59 inches of precipitation a year, including 49.2 inches of snowfall [10].
- **Housing guarantee:** Students must live in university housing for four semesters; the housing page describes a two-year guarantee for incoming first-years [17].
- **Annual Security Report:** Tufts posts its Annual Security Report (2026 edition, with print copies on request) at https://publicsafety.tufts.edu/asr [15].
- **Housing:** All undergraduate halls close over winter break. You can stay only if a university-sponsored activity requires it; other countries' travel rules don't count as an exception [13].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** $4,976 for 2026–27, and waivable [4]; the waiver criteria for internationals weren't stated.
- **Counseling and disability services:** Counseling and Mental Health Service (through Tufts Health Service). The StAAR Center arranges accessibility accommodations such as exam time, notetakers, CART, lecture recordings, and housing, dining and transport adjustments [12].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** full need regardless of citizenship [2]; Boston-area location [4]; a research university with a liberal arts core.
- **Notable alumni:** Tufts alumni include Nobel laureates Eugene Fama (Economics) and Roderick MacKinnon (Chemistry 2003, Tufts School of Medicine '82) [20].
