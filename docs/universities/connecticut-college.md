# Connecticut College

Connecticut College is a private liberal arts college in New London, Connecticut, with about 1,940 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was found in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need regardless of citizenship,** for students who request aid when applying [2].
- **CSS Profile (code 3284), no fee waivers** [2].
- **No yearly reapplication.** Returning internationals' aid is based on their initial application, though they can ask for a review [2].
- **English proficiency required** if you attended secondary school outside the US (TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo). Native speakers and students with three years at an English-medium high school are exempt [5].
- **No spring entry for internationals** [4].
- **Aid excludes** transportation and personal costs (our list) [6]. On-campus jobs are capped at 20 hours a week [7].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1911 in New London, Connecticut; opened 1915. A women's college until it became co-educational in 1969 [8].
- **Campus:** a 750-acre arboretum campus overlooking Long Island Sound and the Thames River [9].
- **Enrollment:** 1,937 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]; internationals were 6% of full-time undergraduates in Fall 2023 [10].
- **Getting there:** New London has Amtrak service, about 2 hours to Boston and 2½ to New York. Air travel options were not on the pages checked [11].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Demonstrated need met regardless of citizenship [2].
- **Form:** CSS Profile (code 3284); the 2027–28 Profile opens Oct 1, 2026 [2][4].
- **Deadlines:** EA and ED I Nov 16, 2026; ED II and RD Jan 15, 2027 [4].
- **Renewal:** Based on the initial application [2].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 111 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $69,638, total $77,29,861 [21].

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

- **Comprehensive fee:** $44,830 per semester for tuition, fees, housing and food, plus an estimated $2,000 for health insurance in the fall [3].

## 6. How to apply

- Requirements and deadlines: [4]. English: see the key points [5].

## 7. Academics

- **Curriculum:** "Connections", a four-year curriculum combining integrated coursework, internships, research and study abroad [9].
- **Programs:** 44 majors and 47 minors [9].
- **Teaching:** average class size 19; student–faculty ratio 9:1 [9].
- **Study away:** offered through the global focus programs [12].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 24%, biological and biomedical sciences 22%, psychology 11%, visual and performing arts 11%, computer and information sciences 7% [16].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 89.7%; 6-year graduation rate 81.7% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** about 6% of full-time undergraduates (Fall 2023) [10].
- **Adviser:** a dedicated international student adviser for all four years. The adviser helps with F-1 questions, transport, jobs, healthcare and housing, and with OPT and CPT [13].
- **Orientation:** the Odyssey pre-orientation. Each new international student gets an International Adviser, an older international student [13].
- **Work:** on campus, up to 20 hours a week [7].
- **Breaks:** Residence halls close in winter and spring breaks, but approved internationals may stay at no charge for room and board. Summer housing is limited to students with campus jobs or research [14].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** an arboretum campus on Long Island Sound in New London [9].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Groton, CT, 6.7 km from campus: average temperature 52.3°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 26.0°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 78.6°F; 48.91 inches of precipitation a year, including 24.5 inches of snowfall [15].
- **Break housing:** free for approved internationals in winter and spring breaks [14].
- **Housing guarantee:** Not published on the pages checked (conncoll.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Annual Security Report:** Connecticut College posts its Annual Security Report and Annual Fire Safety Report (2024 editions found) at https://www.conncoll.edu/campus-life/campus-safety/clery-information-crime-statistics/index.html [19].
- **Housing:** Residence houses close over winter break except year-round housing; international students are among those allowed to stay [17].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- Health insurance is estimated at $2,000, charged in the fall [3].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** the Connections curriculum [9]; the arboretum campus [9]; free break housing for internationals [14]; full need regardless of citizenship [2].
- **Notable alumni:** Connecticut College's alumni pages feature Ethan Brown '94, CEO of Beyond Meat; author Sloane Crosley '00; and Pfizer executive Sally Susman '84 [18].
