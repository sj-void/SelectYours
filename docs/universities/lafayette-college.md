# Lafayette College

Lafayette is a private liberal arts college with engineering in Easton, Pennsylvania, with about 2,760 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full-need guarantee.** Lafayette guarantees to meet 100% of admitted and enrolled students' demonstrated need. That covers calculated need, not every expense, and not summer or interim costs [3].
- **Real aid numbers.** 111 international undergraduates received Lafayette aid, averaging $64,791, or $7.19 million in total (CDS 2025–26 H6) [2].
- **CSS Profile required; ISFAA not accepted.** If your family can contribute under $20,000 a year and the CSS fee is a real hardship, ask for Lafayette's alternative International Financial Aid Form after applying [3].
- **English test required:** TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo [3].
- **Need-aware and competitive** for non-US citizens. Transfer aid is "extremely limited" [6].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Guarantees full demonstrated need for admitted students, reviewed yearly [3][4].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile and IDOC (no ISFAA). There is an alternative form for hardship cases with a contribution under $20,000 [3][5].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 15 [5].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 111 students; average $64,791; total $7,191,808 [2].

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

Not on the pages checked (lafayette.edu, searched 2026-11-23). College Scorecard lists an earlier-year cost of attendance of $87,092 [1]. Lafayette guarantees full demonstrated need, but not summer or interim costs [3].

## 7. Academics

- **Degrees:** BA in 34 fields; BS in 15 fields, 5 of them in engineering [8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 30%, engineering 22%, biological and biomedical sciences 14%, psychology 9%, interdisciplinary studies 4% [15].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 10 to 1 [8] (CDS 2025–26 I2 agrees) [2].
- **Careers:** the Gateway Career Center helps with majors, campus jobs, internships, externships, job searches and graduate school [9].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 379 of 587 class sections (65%) had fewer than 20 students [2]. The PDF text scrambles the columns, so they were matched by checking that the seven size bands add up to the total.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 89.7%; 6-year graduation rate 85.4% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 186 nonresident undergraduates (CDS B2) [2], from 44 countries [8].
- **Office:** International Student Advising (Intercultural Development). Its Associate Director is the Principal Designated School Official [10].
- **Orientation:** International Orientation is mandatory, the week before first-year orientation. It covers paperwork, phones, bank accounts, health insurance enrollment and the local area [10][11].
- **Work:** On campus, usually 10–12 hours a week in term. CPT and OPT workshops cover the rules and timelines [10][11].
- **Breaks:** Housing and food support may be offered over summer recess if needed. Talk to the office before travelling [12].
- **Host program:** an International Friendship Program pairs students with local friends [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Easton, Pennsylvania, in the Lehigh Valley [8].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Phillipsburg-Easton Brg, NJ, 0.9 km from campus: average temperature 52.1°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 23.2°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 83.0°F; 51.01 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [14].
- **Annual Security Report:** Not published on the pages checked (lafayette.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Housing:** On-campus housing is guaranteed for all students; students staying over winter break remain in their fall room [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** International students are enrolled in health insurance during orientation [10].
- **Counseling:** free and confidential at the Counseling Center [9].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** engineering within a liberal arts college [8]; a full-need guarantee for internationals [3]; the Gateway Career Center [9].
- **Notable alumni:** Alumni include Nobel laureates Philip Hench and Haldan Keffer Hartline, and US Treasury Secretary William E. Simon [17].
