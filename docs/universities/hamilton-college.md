# Hamilton College

Hamilton is a private liberal arts college in Clinton, New York, with about 2,030 undergraduates. It has an open curriculum, with no general distribution requirements [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No current Common Data Set was read in this pass; the newest one found was 2024–25. Admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Certification of Finances for everyone.** Every international applicant must submit it, through the applicant portal, whether or not they apply for aid [3].
- **CSS Profile for aid seekers,** with tax documents sent through the College Board's IDOC service [3].
- **Need-aware, full need.** Domestic first-years are reviewed need-blind, but family finances may be considered for internationals. Admitted internationals who qualify get their full demonstrated need met if their aid file is complete by the deadlines [2].
- **Package:** scholarship plus a campus job (our list) [4].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Campus:** a 1,350-acre hilltop campus in Clinton, New York [6].
- **Enrollment:** about 2,046 students; student–faculty ratio 9:1 [6].
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Arts, across 59 areas of study [6].
- **Class of 2030 admit rate:** 14.3% [6].
- **Founding year and nearest airport:** Founded in 1793 as the Hamilton-Oneida Academy and chartered as a college in 1812; the hilltop campus covers 1,350 acres. The nearest airport wasn't published on the pages checked [21]. Airport or station pickup is offered on a limited basis at arrival [7].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for internationals; full need met for admitted students with complete files [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile, IDOC documents and the Certification of Finances (required of all internationals) [3].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 19, 2026; ED II and RD Jan 15, 2027; QuestBridge Nov 2, 2026; fall transfer Apr 1, 2027 [3].
- CDS H6 counts: Not read. The newest Hamilton CDS found was 2024–25.

## 6. How to apply

- **Transfers:** Internationals without an active US study visa can't be admitted as spring transfers [5].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 59 areas of study, all leading to a BA [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 30%, biological and biomedical sciences 16%, computer and information sciences 8%, psychology 6%, visual and performing arts 6% [16].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 9:1 [6].
- **Careers:** every student is paired with a career coach for all four years [9].
- **Global learning:** off-campus study programs [10].
- **Class sizes (CDS 2025–26 I3):** 338 of 469 class sections (72%) had fewer than 20 students [22]. The PDF text scrambles the columns; the six printed size bands add up to the total, with the 100+ band blank.

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 94.8%; 6-year graduation rate 89.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** Internationals are about 8% of the student body, per a current admission page; an older page says 6% [9].
- **Office:** International Student Services, in the Days-Massolo Center, led by a director who is the Principal Designated School Official [11].
- **Orientation:** mandatory, 15–17 August 2026, before general orientation. No arrivals before 13 August [7].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: up to 20 hours a week during term and up to 40 in vacations. Off-campus work needs the director's approval first [12].
  - **CPT paused:** From 24 August 2026, Hamilton paused all new CPT authorizations for Fall 2026 under new SEVP guidance. The pause covers off-campus work, training, internships and co-ops tied to your program [13].
- **Support:** The Counseling Center helps with cultural adjustment and homesickness [14].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** a 1,350-acre hilltop campus in Clinton, New York [6].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Utica Oneida Co Airport, NY, 10.7 km from campus: average temperature 46.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 16.8°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 78.4°F; 44.00 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [15].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2026 Annual Safety, Security and Fire Safety Report is at https://www.hamilton.edu/documents/clery/2026Clerybook.pdf [19].
- **Housing:** On-campus housing is guaranteed for all four years. Halls stay open over fall and spring breaks but close for winter break [17].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Counseling:** the Counseling Center, including help with cultural adjustment [14].
- **Insurance:** The Aetna student plan costs $3,611 a year. International students must be on it unless they hold US-based insurance meeting the same standards; the fall waiver deadline was August 14, 2026 [20].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a four-year career coach for every student [9]; a full-need commitment for all four years [6]; a 1,350-acre campus [6].
- **Notable alumni:** Graduates include US Secretary of Agriculture Tom Vilsack, civil-rights figures Bob Moses and Mary Bonauto, former P&G CEO A.G. Lafley, Nobel laureate Paul Greengard and Vice President James S. Sherman [18].
