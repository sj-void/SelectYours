# Reed College

Reed is a private liberal arts college in Portland, Oregon, with about 1,320 undergraduates. It is known for a senior thesis that every student writes [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

No Common Data Set was read in this pass, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Full need, but with loans.** Reed commits to meeting 100% of demonstrated need for admitted first-year internationals who apply for aid by the deadlines. Packages combine grants, work and loans, and the loans rise by about $500–$1,000 each year [2][4].
- **Need-based only.** There are no academic or athletic scholarships [2]. Reed does plan full-tuition scholarships for two students from each continent, starting with the Fall 2026 entering class [4]. UWC graduates get the Davis–Reed award [4].
- **Say "yes" to aid on the application,** or you can never receive aid at Reed [2].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 4654), or the ISFAA if the CSS fee is a hardship [2].
- **No adjustments for currency swings** or changed family circumstances [4].
- **English:** TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo is recommended if English isn't spoken at home or used at your school. SAT or ACT scores may stand in for it. This is from an older catalog page [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 1,320 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1]. Reed's ISS brochure describes an international community of more than 170 students [9].
- **Aid reach:** more than 57% of students receive need-based aid [8].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Founded in 1908 under the wills of Simeon and Amanda Reed; the first classes met in 1911. Campus size wasn't published on the pages checked [23].
- **Nearest airport:** Portland International is 12 miles away: MAX Red Line to Hollywood Transit Center, then bus #75 (about 1–1.5 hours), or a cab for about $55 [16].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** 100% of demonstrated need for admitted first-year internationals who meet the deadlines [2].
- **Package:** Grants (most of the aid), work and loans that rise each year [2][4].
- **Forms:** CSS Profile (code 4654) or the ISFAA [2].
- **Rules:** You must request aid at application. Reed doesn't reconsider aid for exchange-rate changes [2][4].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–2026 H6):** 71 nonresident undergraduates received institutional aid; average award $65,475, total $46,48,738 [19].

## 6. How to apply

- **Aid deadlines (2027–28 cycle):** ED I and EA Nov 1, 2026; ED II and RD Jan 15, 2027 [8].
- **Early Decision I:** application due Nov 1, decisions in December [10].
- **Testing:** "test-free" [3]. **English:** TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo recommended where applicable (older catalog) [5].
- **Plans:** internationals may apply in any application plan [2].

## 7. Academics

- **PhDs:** 5th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (17.9%) [6].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** biological and biomedical sciences 17%, social sciences 16%, English 11%, psychology 9%, foreign languages 8% [13].
- **Experiential learning:** ISS advises on the EXEL program with CPT [9].
- **Majors:** 38 majors and interdisciplinary programs, with minors listed separately [22].
- **Class sizes:** Not published on the pages checked (reed.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 87.6%; 6-year graduation rate 73.5% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** International Student Services (ISS). It covers immigration advising, SEVIS, I-20s, travel signatures, work authorization (OPT, EXEL with CPT), leaves and reduced course loads [9].
- **Orientation:** International Student Orientation is required for F-1 and J-1 students, before first-year orientation. It's free, with meals included, and covers campus jobs and keeping your status [9].
- **Peer support:** the InterConnect program matches new internationals with a current student and a peer group [11].
- **Advising:** weekly drop-in hours, plus in-person, phone or virtual appointments (iss@reed.edu) [9].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Portland, Oregon [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Portland Kgw-Tv, OR, 6.2 km from campus: average temperature 54.7°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 37.8°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 77.9°F; 44.07 inches of precipitation a year, including 3.6 inches of snowfall [12].
- **Housing guarantee:** Reed has a two-year on-campus residency guarantee and requirement [21].
- **Annual Security Report:** The 2026 Annual Security & Fire Safety Report (calendar 2025 plus the two previous years) is at https://www.reed.edu/community-safety/assets/downloads/annual-security-and-fire-safety-report-2026.pdf [18].
- **Housing:** Winter break housing is only for students already living on campus, with priority for those facing housing or financial insecurity and those whose home is in another country [15].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** Reed lists international health insurance at $3,654 a year (domestic plan $4,272). International students can request a waiver for comparable coverage, but only while in the US and not after the first day of classes [20].
- **Counseling and disability services:** Disability & Accessibility Resources handles accommodations; documentation for variable mental-health conditions should ideally be under six months old. The Health & Counseling Center offers care, medication management and referrals [14].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** 5th nationally for PhDs per graduate [6]; full-need aid for admitted first-year internationals [2]; a city location in Portland [1].
- **Notable alumni:** Reed's technology alumni include Howard Vollum '36, co-founder of Tektronix, and Steve Jobs '76 of Apple [17].
