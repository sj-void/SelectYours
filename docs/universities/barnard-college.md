# Barnard College

Barnard College is a private nonprofit women's liberal arts college in New York City, in formal partnership with Columbia University [1][14]. Its students take courses at Columbia and Barnard together, but Barnard has its own admissions, faculty, financial aid and degree [9][12].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

**Note on the CDS.** The Barnard 2025–2026 Common Data Set is a fillable PDF form. Its text extracts without reliable row alignment, so the numbers below were read by position and cross-checked for internal consistency (for example, the class-size row sums to its total). Re-check them against the PDF [1]. Barnard's page lists two files (the later one ends in "II"); both gave the same values for every item used here [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-aware and limited.** Barnard is need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents and need-aware for everyone else. The aid pages say Barnard "has limited funding for International first-year applicants" and awards a small number of need-based scholarships in each first-year class [3][4][5]. The CDS agrees: 31 degree-seeking nonresident undergraduates got institutional aid in Fall 2025 (H6) [1].
- **Apply for aid at admission or never.** Students who don't apply for aid at admission are not eligible in later years. International transfer students cannot receive aid [3][4].
- **Forms:** the CSS Profile (code 2038). The ISAFA from IACAC can replace it for applicants from a list of countries or who cannot complete the Profile online. Parent foreign income documents are needed, translated into U.S. dollars [3].
- **Aid deadlines:** Nov 1, 2026 for ED and QuestBridge; Jan 15, 2027 for RD [3]. The RD aid deadline is later than the RD application deadline.
- **No merit aid.** "Barnard gives no merit scholarships." All institutional aid is need-based [4].
- **English test:** TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo may be required unless you qualify for a waiver. Competitive scores are TOEFL 105 or higher, IELTS 7.5 or higher, DET 135 or higher [2].
- **Extra first-year charges for international students:** a $650 orientation fee and a $160 visa (I-20) fee [7].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 3,187 degree-seeking undergraduates (3,181 full-time women and 6 others; 36 part-time) and no graduate students, counted Oct 15, 2025 (CDS 2025–26 B1). 3,223 undergraduates in total [1].

- **Calendar:** Fall and spring semesters; a minimum of 12 credits per semester is required [7].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-aware for non-U.S. citizens and non-permanent residents. Barnard says it "meets 100% of demonstrated need for every admitted student" [8], and its international page repeats that it meets 100% of need [2]. The aid pages also say funding for international first-years is "limited" and only a small number of need-based scholarships go to each class [3][5].
- **Forms and deadlines:**
  - CSS Profile, code 2038, open from October 1 [3].
  - ISAFA (IACAC) as an alternative for applicants from Benin, Cameroon, Cuba, Ghana, Iran, Nigeria, North Korea, Sudan or Tonga, or anyone who cannot complete the Profile online [3].
  - Parent foreign income documentation in U.S. dollars: a tax return, or a signed employer letter listing wages, bonuses and benefits and stating whether gross or net. Families who file U.S. returns send the 2025 return and W-2s instead [3].
  - Deadlines: ED and QuestBridge Nov 1, 2026; RD Jan 15, 2027 [3].
  - The CDS (H7) lists the CSS Profile and "documentation of foreign income translated to dollars" for nonresident first-years [1].
- **What a package contains:** Barnard's general policy says it meets need with a mix of grant, loan and job awards [4]. The pages read do not say whether international packages include loans. Job earnings cover personal expenses, and outside scholarships do not reduce the job award [4].
- **Actual awards (CDS H6, Fall 2025):** 31 degree-seeking undergraduate nonresidents aided (about 5.8% of the 535, our calculation); average $80,574; total $2,497,802 [1].
- **Rules:**
  - Renewal: students admitted with a Barnard grant can expect renewal if they keep meeting need and academic standards and reapply each year. Aid runs for at most eight semesters [4].
  - Aid after admission: not possible [3][4].
  - Outside scholarships: they first reduce any Stafford loan, then the student contribution; any excess reduces the Barnard grant. They do not reduce the parent contribution or the job award [4].
  - Transfers: Barnard cannot assist international transfer students [3][4].
- **Merit scholarships:** None [4].
- **Net price calculator:** http://npc.collegeboard.org/app/barnard. The page says the calculators are not intended for international students; contact the college instead [5].

**Consistency with our CSV:** No contradiction. The CSV's "small number of international first-years; no aid for international transfers" matches the aid pages and CDS H6 [3][4].

## 6. How to apply

- **Platforms:** Common App; a counselor can also send materials through Slate. Barnard participates in QuestBridge; whether internationals can use it is not stated [9].
- **Deadlines for Fall 2027 [8]:**

  | Round | Apply by | Aid forms by | Decision |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Early Decision | Nov 1, 2026 | Nov 1, 2026 [3] | Mid-December 2026 |
  | Regular Decision | Jan 1, 2027 | Jan 15, 2027 [3] | Late March 2027; reply by May 1 |

  The CDS gives ED closing date Nov 1 [1].
- **Fee and fee waivers:** $75, non-refundable. Waivers: Common App, NACAC, SAT or ACT forms, or a counselor letter. The page does not say whether internationals qualify [9].
- **Required parts:** Common App essay; two Barnard questions (a 50-word one on a question on your mind, and a 200–250 word one about a woman whose approach differs from yours); a counselor recommendation with transcript and school profile; two teacher letters from core academic subjects; the mid-year report (the page gives both Feb 28 and Mar 1, so confirm). Optional: up to two extra letters. Counselor and teacher letters are due Nov 20 (ED) or Jan 15 (RD) per the admissions calendar [9].
- **English proficiency:** TOEFL iBT (including Home Edition), IELTS (including Indicator) or Duolingo may be required. Not accepted: TOEFL Essentials and MyBest scores. Scores are not superscored and are valid for two years. Waivers: English as first language; English as the language of instruction throughout secondary school; an English-medium IB Diploma or A Level; SAT EBRW 700 or higher; ACT English or Reading 29 or higher [2].
- **International documents:** An official transcript or exam results through e-docs or mail, not copies of certificates. Non-English records need certified English translations. Students with national or board exams send those results plus transcripts for each secondary year. Final-year applicants state the expected credential and date [2].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** Admitted Class of 2030 students named 46 different majors as their first choice [18]. The total number of majors wasn't published on the pages checked.
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 29%, biological and biomedical sciences 14%, interdisciplinary studies 9%, computer and information sciences 8%, psychology 8% [21].
- **Teaching:** Student–faculty ratio 9 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2; 3,200 students to 364 faculty; the page carries a stray "2024–2025" footer) [1]. In Fall 2025, 712 undergraduate class sections: 155 had 2–9 students, 341 had 10–19, 89 had 20–29, 30 had 30–39, 24 had 40–49, 61 had 50–99 and 12 had 100 or more (I3). About 70% had fewer than 20 students (our calculation) [1].
- **Cross-registration with Columbia:** Barnard students can take most Columbia undergraduate courses numbered 4000 and below. They register for fall and spring Columbia courses alongside their Barnard courses, and the courses appear on the Barnard transcript. Some Columbia schools (Business, Journalism, Public Health, GSAPP) have their own rules, graduate and professional courses may need approval, and some courses limit the number a Barnard student may take (restrictions are listed in Vergil, the Columbia course directory). Courses at the Jewish Theological Seminary (maximum two), the Juilliard School and the Manhattan School of Music are also open under cross-registration agreements [12].
- **Fields:** Engineering, 3-2 and pre-health/pre-law advising: Not published on the pages checked (barnard.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Opportunities:** The CDS shows cross-registration and student-designed majors are offered [1]. Study abroad (Barnard Global), honors and research details: Not published on the pages checked.
- **STEM majors:** Barnard publishes no list. The ICE STEM Designated Degree Program List decides eligibility for the 24-month extension, which needs an E-Verify employer [19].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 97% (CDS 2025–26 B22: 688 of 710 Fall 2024 entrants) [1]. Six-year graduation rate 92% for the Fall 2019 cohort; four-year rate 80.6% (502 of 623, our calculation) (CDS B4–B21) [1]. College Scorecard shows 91.65% completion and 96.57% retention [14].
- **After graduation:**
  - 91% of graduates from the Classes of 2018–2025 were working or in graduate school six months after graduating [15].
  - 25% of the Class of 2025 had been admitted to graduate or professional school by graduation [16].
  - A Class of 2025–only placement rate and top employers weren't published on the pages checked.
- **Earnings:** College Scorecard median earnings are $98,280 ten years after entry and $67,694 six years after entry. They cover federal-aid recipients only and exclude international students [14].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 535 degree-seeking international undergraduates (CDS B2) [1]. College Scorecard shows 15.6% non-resident students [14]. The admitted Class of 2030 came from 58 countries [18].
- **Support:** International Student Services (ISS) advises on visa rules, travel, on- and off-campus work and campus involvement. New students attend a mandatory summer Pre-Orientation Program. Newly admitted students work with ISS to obtain the I-20 (F-1) or DS-2019 (J-1). A Global Ambassador student leader program exists [11].
- **Work:**
  - OPT: up to 12 months of full-time OPT per degree level. Pre-completion OPT needs a completed academic year and full-time enrolment.
  - STEM extension: 24 months for degrees on the ICE STEM list, with an E-Verify employer.
  - CPT: Barnard doesn't give credit for internships, but you can get CPT by taking a 1-credit independent study. CPT can't be backdated.
  - Questions: iss@barnard.edu [19].
- **Practicalities:** Break housing: Halls close for winter break; students who need access must sign up for Break Housing by the deadline [22]. Mentor programs: Not published on the pages checked (barnard.edu, searched 2026-11-23). The CDS lists an International Student Organization among campus activities [1].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Housing:** A housing guarantee wasn't published on the pages checked. CDS F1: 97% of first-years and 75% of all undergraduates live in college-owned or affiliated housing; 67% of first-years are from out of state [1]. Residential Life and Housing: housing@barnard.edu [13].
- **Location:** Manhattan, a large city. Nearest airport: Not published on the pages checked (barnard.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at New York Cntrl Pk Twr, NY, 3.5 km from campus: average temperature 55.8°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 30.4°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 82.6°F; 49.52 inches of precipitation a year, including 29.8 inches of snowfall [17].
- **Safety:** Barnard's Annual Security Report: The newest edition found is the report for calendar year 2022, https://barnard.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/Barnard%20College%20Clery%20ASR%20for%202022%20calendar%20year.pdf; check Barnard Public Safety for a newer one [23]. Federal data: https://ope.ed.gov/campussafety.
- **Student body:** Women's college; all full-time degree-seeking undergraduates in the CDS are women except 6 [1][14].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** All students must have health insurance, either the Aetna student plan billed by Barnard or their own coverage. The fall 2026 waiver deadline was Aug 28, 2026. First-term spring waivers: the window opens Nov 11, 2026 and closes Jan 10, 2027 (a search extract of the Barnard page; the page itself said spring-only waiver details were still to be set) [7][10]. No waiver rules specific to international students were published on the pages checked.
- **Cost:** $5,126 for 2026–27 [7].
- **Services:** The Primary Care Health Service (PCHS) sees all students regardless of insurance type, with no fee or co-pay for visits (some medications, vaccines and tests are charged) [10].
- **Counseling:** The Rosemary Furman Counseling Center, in Hewitt Hall, offers free short-term counseling, medication evaluations, referrals and crisis help to all registered students (212-854-2092) [20].
- **Disability services:** The Center for Accessibility Resources and Disability Services (CARDS) handles accommodations, including for learning disabilities and ADHD. You apply online, send documentation and have an intake meeting. Accommodations aren't retroactive (212-854-4634, cards@barnard.edu) [20].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Notable alumnae:** Barnard's alumnae news archive highlights writers Jhumpa Lahiri and Sigrid Nunez, actor Cynthia Nixon and filmmaker Greta Gerwig [25].
