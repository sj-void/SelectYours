# Trinity College

Trinity College is a private liberal arts college in Hartford, Connecticut, with about 2,110 undergraduates [2][1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Key points for international applicants

- **Odds for internationals:** In Fall 2025, 167 of 3,348 international applicants were admitted, about 5.0%, against 29.5% overall (CDS 2025–26 C1) [2].
- **Many internationals receive some aid.** CDS 2025–26 H6 reports 295 international undergraduates aided, averaging $70,437, or $20.8 million in total [2]. That count is higher than the 252 nonresident undergraduates in B2, probably because the two items cover different groups or years.
- **Full calculated need; limited funds.** Need-based aid comes as scholarships, grants and loans [3].
- **CSS Profile only (code 3899).** Fee waivers are limited and decided case by case. The ISFAA is accepted only from Iran, North Korea and Sudan [3].
- **Not applying for aid?** Submit a Statement of Finances instead [3].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Founded:** 1823. Trinity is a liberal arts college in an urban setting, in Hartford, Connecticut [5].
- **Historic campus:** the Long Walk district was added to the National Register of Historic Places in April 2024 [5].
- **Enrollment:** 2,112 undergraduates (CDS 2025–26 B1) [2]. Trinity's own pages give 2,056 full-time and 2,150+ [5].
- **Campus size:** A 100-acre campus, occupied since 1878. Trinity opened in 1823 as Washington College [18].
- **Nearest airport:** Bradley International is about 30 minutes away by car [14].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Trinity says it is committed to meeting the full calculated need of all admitted students. Aid for internationals is limited and comes as scholarships, grants and loans [3].
- **Form:** CSS Profile (code 3899) [3].
- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 17; spring transfer Nov 1; fall transfer Apr 1 [4][3].
- **Actual awards (CDS 2025–26 H6):** 295 students; average $70,437; total $20,779,007 [2].

## 6. How to apply

- **Deadlines:** ED I Nov 15; ED II and RD Jan 17 [4].
- **English:** Duolingo, IELTS or TOEFL required; a waiver can be requested if you meet certain criteria [7].
- **Aid:** CSS Profile; non-aid applicants file a Statement of Finances [7].
- **Testing policy and essays:** Not published on the pages checked (trincoll.edu, searched 2026-11-23).

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** 50+ majors, 45+ minors and 25+ experiential certificates. Engineering is available as a BS [5].
- **Teaching:** student–faculty ratio 8 to 1 (CDS 2025–26 I2) [2]; Trinity's pages say 8:1 or 9:1 [5].
- **Study away:** run by the Global Engagement Office [8].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 38%, biological and biomedical sciences 12%, psychology 11%, public administration and social services 7%, computer and information sciences 4% [11].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** 6-year graduation rate 89% for the 2019 cohort (CDS 2025–26 B) [2]; first-year retention 90.0% (IPEDS) [1].

## 9. International student life

- **Community:** 252 nonresident undergraduates (CDS B2) [2].
- **Office:** the Office of International Students and Scholars (OISS), part of the Global Engagement Office, in the Raether Library and IT Center. It handles visas, work authorization, I-20s and international student programs [8].
- **Visa rule:** F-1 students must take a full load of four courses a semester [7].
- **OPT and CPT:** Trinity's Office of International Students and Scholars publishes its F-1 work rules at https://www.trincoll.edu/oiss/immigration/employment/ [19].
- **Break housing:** International students may stay over winter break at no extra charge [13].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** urban Hartford, with the historic Long Walk [5].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Hartford Brainard Fld, CT, 3.6 km from campus: average temperature 52.0°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 22.8°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 82.4°F; 42.83 inches of precipitation a year (this station has no snowfall normal) [10].
- **Housing guarantee:** Not published on the pages checked (trincoll.edu, searched 2026-11-23).
- **Annual Security Report:** The newest edition found is the October 1, 2024 Annual Security Report (crime statistics for 2021–2023), https://www.trincoll.edu/campus-safety/wp-content/uploads/sites/117/2024/10/Campus-Safety-Annual-Report-2023v4.pdf [16].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Study-away insurance:** required international medical and travel cover for students abroad [9].
- **Insurance:** The domestic plan costs $3,252 (August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027). F-1 and J-1 students are enrolled automatically in an ISO Care plan, billed at $2,040 a year on the fall bill [17].
- **Counseling and disability services:** The Counseling Center offers confidential counseling, psychotherapy, medication consultation and off-campus referrals. Accommodations need an online application with documentation from a qualified provider [12].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** a liberal arts college in a state capital [5]; engineering BS [5]; a high international aid count (CDS H6: 295) [2].
- **Notable alumni:** The student newspaper lists Pulitzer Prize winners among its alumni: George F. Will, Jim Murray, William K. Marimow and Steven Pearlstein [15].
