# Wellesley College

Wellesley is a private liberal arts college for women in Wellesley, Massachusetts, near Boston. It has about 2,300 undergraduates [1].

**Last verified:** 2026-11-23. **Entry cycle:** Fall 2027 (applying 2026–27). Rules: [README.md](README.md).

Wellesley's site blocks automated reading of its CDS 2025–26, so admissions numbers come from federal IPEDS data (Fall 2024) [1].

## Key points for international applicants

- **Need-sensitive and "extraordinarily competitive".** Aid for internationals is limited, so admission is need-sensitive and selection is extraordinarily competitive [5].
- **Ask for aid at application, or never.** International citizens who may want aid at any point in four years must apply when they apply for admission [5].
- **Full need, fixed contribution.** Wellesley meets 100% of calculated need for admitted internationals who applied at admission, and the family contribution is fixed for all years (our list) [4].
- **Travel is not covered.** International travel is excluded from the cost of attendance [5].
- **CSS Profile only if asked.** Start it early, but submit it only when Student Financial Services instructs you to [5].

## 1. Snapshot

- **Enrollment:** 2,300 degree-seeking undergraduates (IPEDS) [1].
- **Programs:** more than 50 departmental and interdepartmental majors [8].
- **Founding year and campus size:** Founded in 1870 by Henry and Pauline Durant (the first students arrived in 1875); a 500-acre campus [23].
- **Nearest airport:** Boston Logan is the nearest airport: take a taxi to the college, or the Logan Express bus to Framingham [20].

## 4. Financial aid for international students

- **Policy:** Need-sensitive. Full calculated need met for admitted aid applicants [4][5].
- **Forms:** Wellesley's aid application; the CSS Profile only if requested [5].
- **Rules:** Aid must be requested at the time of application [5]. Travel is not included in the cost of attendance [5].
- CDS H6 counts: Not available. Wellesley's CDS page blocks automated reading.

## 5. Cost of attendance 2026–27

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Tuition | $72,570 [9] |
| Housing | $12,020 [9] |
| Meals | $11,186 [9] |
| Activity fee | $360 [9] |
| **Comprehensive fee (our sum)** | **$96,136** |

The average aid award is $70,519 [9]. The Wellesley Tuition Promise (free tuition for family incomes of $200,000 or less, from fall 2027) is for US families [10]. Travel isn't included in international students' cost of attendance [5].

## 6. How to apply

- **Eligibility:** Wellesley considers any applicant who lives as a woman and consistently identifies as a woman [2]. In 2023 it said it had "no plan to revisit" its mission or admission policy [2][6].
- **Deadlines:** See the quick facts [5].

## 7. Academics

- **Programs:** more than 50 majors [8].
- **Teaching:** typical class size 17–20; student–faculty ratio 7:1 [8].
- **Cross-registration:** MIT, Brandeis, and the Three-College Alliance [8].
- **STEM OPT:** a 24-month extension for eligible STEM degrees [11].
- **Most common fields (share of bachelor's degrees, IPEDS via College Scorecard):** social sciences 23%, computer and information sciences 15%, biological and biomedical sciences 12%, physical sciences 9%, psychology 7% [19].

## 8. Outcomes

- **Completion:** First-year retention 96.5%; 6-year graduation rate 90.9% (IPEDS via College Scorecard) [1].
- **PhDs:** Wellesley ranks 25th nationally for graduates who go on to earn PhDs (11.1%, NSF data compiled by Swarthmore) [7].

## 9. International student life

- **Office:** the Slater International Center. Its advisers help with immigration, employment, academic, social and cultural questions [12].
- **Orientation:** International Student Orientation is required for all non-immigrant students [12].
- **Work:**
  - On campus: possible for F-1 and J-1 students in valid status, until your program ends [13].
  - CPT: work authorization tied to your academic program [13].
  - OPT: 12 months, no job offer needed. It requires a Slater recommendation, a mandatory workshop and a USCIS filing, from 90 days before to 60 days after completion [14].
  - STEM OPT: an extra 24 months [11].
- **Careers:** Career Education has resources for international students [15].

## 10. Campus, location and safety

- **Setting:** Wellesley, Massachusetts, near Boston [1].
- **Climate:** NOAA 1991–2020 normals at Natick, MA, 3.2 km from campus: average temperature 48.9°F over the year; winter (Dec–Feb) average low 19.3°F; summer (Jun–Aug) average high 79.6°F; 47.26 inches of precipitation a year, including 45.7 inches of snowfall [17].
- **Housing:** Students must live on campus and take the meal plan to qualify for the Tuition Promise [10]; a general four-year guarantee wasn't stated.
- **Annual Security Report:** The newest report found is the 2024 Annual Security & Fire Safety Report, https://www1.wellesley.edu/sites/default/files/assets/departments/police/files/wellesley_college_2024_annual_security_fire_safety_report_final.pdf. Check the Wellesley police site for a newer edition [18].

## 11. Health and wellbeing

- **Insurance:** The 2026–27 rate is $4,051. International students can waive only with a US-filed, ACA-compliant plan covering emergency and non-emergency care in Massachusetts; the waiver deadline was August 3, 2026 [22].

## 12. Notable alumni and strengths

- **Strengths:** MIT cross-registration [8]; small classes (typically 17–20) [8]; full calculated need for admitted internationals [4].
- **Notable alumnae:** Alumnae include Madeleine Korbel Albright '59, US Secretary of State, and journalist Diane Sawyer '67 [21].
