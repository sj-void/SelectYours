# Prestige and admission difficulty: the 62 full-need schools

**Computed:** 2026-11-23 by `tools/compute-indexes.ps1`. **Method:** [methodology-prestige-difficulty.md](methodology-prestige-difficulty.md) (version 1). **All numbers:** [university-metrics.csv](university-metrics.csv). Every input value has its own source URL in `universities/_data/`, and each school profile cites it.

How to read this:
- **Prestige Index (0–100)** = 55% US standing (U.S. News 2027 60%, Forbes 2027 40%) + 30% global recognition (QS 2027, THE 2027, ARWU 2026) + 15% endowment per student (FY2025). The WSJ 2026 rank is shown for the top 100 but not scored.
- **Difficulty Index (0–100)** is for an international student who needs aid: 60% overall admit rate, 20% SAT midpoint, 10% need-aware penalty, 10% scarcity of aided international seats (CDS H6).
- The two are separate on purpose. Compare the columns to find schools that are prestigious but less hard to get into.
- The global sub-score is 0 for most liberal arts colleges, because they are not eligible for QS or THE. Use the **US standing** column for a US-only view.
